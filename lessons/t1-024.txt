title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 24 · Transportation
episode: T1-024
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Bonjour, Marinette ! Tu vas à la gare en bus ? | Hi, Marinette! Are you going to the train station by bus?
Marinette: Oui, puis je prends le train. Et toi ? | Yes, then I’m taking the train. And you?
Adrien: Je vais en métro. Tu as ton billet ? | I’m going by subway. Do you have your ticket?
Marinette: Oui. Après, on peut louer un vélo. | Yes. After that, we can rent a bicycle.
Adrien: Bonne idée ! Pour aller sur l’île, on prend un bateau. | Good idea! To go to the island, we’ll take a boat.
Marinette: D’accord. Je peux aussi prendre un taxi. | Okay. I can also take a taxi.
Adrien: Et demain, tu viens en avion ? | And tomorrow, are you coming by plane?
Marinette: Non, je viens en scooter ! | No, I’m coming by scooter!

[grammar]
grammar: French articles | For singular nouns beginning with a consonant sound, use le with masculine nouns and la with feminine nouns as the definite article.
example: Je prends le train. | I’m taking the train.
example: Je vais à la gare. | I’m going to the train station.
