title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 59 · Nouns of place and direction 2
episode: T1-059
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Salut Marinette, tu connais ce quartier ? | Hi, Marinette, do you know this neighborhood?
Marinette: Oui, ce quartier est près du centre du village. | Yes, this neighborhood is near the center of the village.
Adrien: Allons au centre. Quel chemin prendre ? | Let’s go to the center. Which path should we take?
Marinette: Suis le trottoir jusqu’au pont, s’il te plaît. | Follow the sidewalk to the bridge, please.
Adrien: Après le pont, on tourne au carrefour ? | After the bridge, do we turn at the intersection?
Marinette: Oui, puis au rond-point, prends la deuxième sortie. | Yes, then at the roundabout, take the second exit.
Adrien: Et la boulangerie est au coin ? | And is the bakery on the corner?
Marinette: Oui, juste là, au coin de la rue ! | Yes, right there, on the corner of the street!

vocab: le pont | bridge | POHN
example: Le pont est près de la gare. | The bridge is near the station.
vocab: le rond-point | roundabout | rohn-PWAHN
example: Le rond-point est devant l'école. | The roundabout is in front of the school.
vocab: le coin | corner | KWAHN (nasal)
example: Le café est au coin de la rue. | The café is on the corner of the street.

[grammar]
grammar: À + le = au | When à comes before the masculine singular article le, they combine to form au. Use au to say “at” or “to” a masculine singular place introduced by le, as in the dialogue.
example: Nous allons au centre. | We are going to the center.
example: Tourne au carrefour. | Turn at the intersection.

[quiz]
quiz: What does le trottoir mean? | the sidewalk | the bridge | the country | A | Le trottoir is the path beside a street for people walking.
quiz: What does le rond-point mean? | the village | the roundabout | the corner | B | Le rond-point is a circular intersection.
quiz: What does “le centre” mean? | path | center | sidewalk | B | le centre means “center”.
quiz: What does “le pays” mean? | bridge | corner | country | C | le pays means “country”.
