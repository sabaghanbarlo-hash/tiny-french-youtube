title: Telling the Time
episode: EF2
scene: station

[story]
Marinette: Excuse-moi, tu as l'heure ? | Excuse me, do you have the time?
Adrien: Oui, il est trois heures et demie. | Yes, it's half past three.
Marinette: Oh non ! Le film commence à quatre heures moins le quart ! | Oh no! The film starts at quarter to four!
Adrien: Pas de panique, on a encore un quart d'heure. | Don't panic, we still have a quarter of an hour.
Marinette: À quelle heure finit le film ? | What time does the film end?
Adrien: À cinq heures et quart. | At quarter past five.
Marinette: Parfait. On mange à sept heures ? | Perfect. Shall we eat at seven?
Adrien: Oui, à sept heures ! | Yes, at seven!

[grammar]
grammar: Il est + number + heure(s) | The time always starts with il est. Use heure for one o'clock and heures for the rest. The h is silent, so there is liaison: trois heures sounds like "trwah-ZUHR".
example: Il est une heure. | It's one o'clock.
example: Il est sept heures. | It's seven o'clock.
grammar: Half, quarter and “to” | Add et demie for half past and et quart for quarter past. For quarter to, subtract: moins le quart. For minutes to the hour use moins cinq, moins dix. Midi and minuit take et demi without the final -e.
example: Il est deux heures et demie. | It's half past two.
example: Il est trois heures moins le quart. | It's quarter to three.
grammar: À + time for “at” | To say when something happens, use à + the time: à sept heures, à midi, à quatre heures moins le quart.
example: Le film est à huit heures. | The film is at eight o'clock.
