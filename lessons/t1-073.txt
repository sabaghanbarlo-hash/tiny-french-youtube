title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 73 · Transportation and traffic 2
episode: T1-073
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: On doit conduire jusqu'au parking, Marinette. | We have to drive to the parking lot, Marinette.
Marinette: Oui, mais il y a un embouteillage sur l'autoroute. | Yes, but there's a traffic jam on the highway.
Adrien: Le camion devant nous ralentit. | The truck in front of us is slowing down.
Marinette: La moto derrière nous va vite. Attention ! | The motorcycle behind us is going fast. Watch out!
Adrien: Je vais freiner au feu rouge. | I'm going to brake at the red light.
Marinette: Merci. On peut traverser la rue ici ? | Thanks. Can we cross the street here?
Adrien: Oui, quand le feu est vert. Regarde bien. | Yes, when the light is green. Look carefully.
Marinette: D'accord, puis on se gare au parking. | Okay, then we'll park in the parking lot.

[vocab]
vocab: conduire | to drive | kohn-DWEER
example: Je conduis la voiture. | I drive the car.
vocab: traverser | to cross | trah-vehr-SAY
example: Nous traversons la rue. | We cross the street.
vocab: freiner | to brake | freh-NAY
example: Je freine avant le feu. | I brake before the traffic light.
vocab: ralentir | to slow down | rah-lahn-TEER

vocab: le feu | fire | luh FUH

[grammar]
grammar: The near future | To say what someone is going to do, use the present tense of aller followed by an infinitive. In the dialogue, vais freiner means “am going to brake.”
example: Je vais conduire. | I'm going to drive.
example: On va traverser la rue. | We're going to cross the street.
