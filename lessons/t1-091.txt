title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 91 · Verbs 9
episode: T1-091
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Tu veux vivre près de la mer un jour ? | Do you want to live near the sea one day?
Marinette: Oui, j’aime entendre les vagues et rêver du soleil. | Yes, I like hearing the waves and dreaming about the sun.
Adrien: Tu souris déjà ! Ta blague me fait rire. | You’re already smiling! Your joke makes me laugh.
Marinette: Et cette chanson me plaît beaucoup. | And I like this song a lot.
Adrien: Appuie sur ce bouton, s’il te plaît. | Press this button, please.
Marinette: D’accord. Une minute suffit ? | Okay. Is one minute enough?
Adrien: Oui, et après, on s’amuse au parc. | Yes, and afterward, we’ll have fun at the park.
Marinette: Super ! La fête dure jusqu’à huit heures. | Great! The party lasts until eight o’clock.

[vocab]
vocab: vivre | to live | VEEVR
example: Je vis près du centre. | I live near the center.
vocab: entendre | to hear | ahn-TAHN-dr
example: J'entends la musique. | I hear the music.
vocab: rire | to laugh | REER
example: Nous rions beaucoup ensemble. | We laugh a lot together.
vocab: sourire | to smile | soo-REER
example: Elle sourit à son ami. | She smiles at her friend.
vocab: appuyer | to press | ah-pwee-YAY
example: Appuie sur le bouton. | Press the button.
vocab: suffire | to be enough | sü-FEER
example: Ça suffit pour deux personnes. | That's enough for two people.
vocab: plaire | to please; to appeal to | PLEHR
example: Ce livre me plaît beaucoup. | I really like this book.
vocab: rêver | to dream | reh-VAY
example: Je rêve souvent de vacances. | I often dream about vacation.
vocab: s’amuser | to have fun | sah-mü-ZAY
example: Les enfants s’amusent dans le jardin. | The children are having fun in the garden.
vocab: durer | to last | dü-RAY
example: Le film dure deux heures. | The movie lasts two hours.

[grammar]
grammar: The verb s’amuser | S’amuser is a pronominal verb, so it uses a small pronoun that matches the subject. With on, the pronoun se becomes s’ before a vowel, and the verb takes a singular form: on s’amuse.
example: On s’amuse au parc. | We have fun at the park.
example: Je m’amuse avec mes amis. | I have fun with my friends.
