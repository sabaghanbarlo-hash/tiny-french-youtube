title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 35 · Frequency and degree adverbs
episode: T1-035
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Salut Marinette, tu vas souvent au café après les cours ? | Hi Marinette, do you often go to the café after class?
Marinette: Parfois, mais rarement le lundi. | Sometimes, but rarely on Mondays.
Adrien: Normalement, je prends seulement un thé. | Normally, I only have a tea.
Marinette: Moi, j'aime vraiment le chocolat chaud, surtout quand il fait froid. | I really like hot chocolate, especially when it's cold.
Adrien: Il est presque midi. Tu as tellement faim ? | It's almost noon. Are you so hungry?
Marinette: Oui, j'ai tellement faim ! | Yes, I'm so hungry!
Adrien: On peut aller au café du coin, c'est plutôt calme. | We can go to the café nearby; it's quite calm.
Marinette: Bonne idée, et le service est vraiment rapide. | Good idea, and the service is really fast.

[vocab]
vocab: souvent | often | soo-VAHN
example: Il lit souvent le soir. | He often reads in the evening.
vocab: parfois | sometimes | par-FWAH
example: Parfois, nous mangeons au restaurant. | Sometimes, we eat at a restaurant.
vocab: rarement | rarely | rar-MAHN
example: Je prends rarement le taxi. | I rarely take a taxi.
vocab: normalement | normally | nor-mal-MAHN
example: Normalement, le train arrive à huit heures. | Normally, the train arrives at eight o’clock.
vocab: presque | almost | PRESK
example: Le magasin est presque fermé. | The store is almost closed.
vocab: vraiment | really | vreh-MAHN
example: Ce livre est vraiment intéressant. | This book is really interesting.
vocab: seulement | only | suhl-MAHN
example: Il mange seulement du riz. | He eats only rice.
vocab: surtout | especially | syur-TOO
example: Elle aime surtout le fromage. | She especially likes cheese.
vocab: tellement | so; so much | tel-MAHN
example: Il fait tellement chaud ici. | It is so hot here.
vocab: plutôt | rather; quite | plew-TOH
example: Ce café est plutôt bon. | This coffee is quite good.

[grammar]
grammar: Adverbs after the verb | Many short adverbs are placed after a conjugated verb. This includes frequency adverbs such as souvent and limiting adverbs such as seulement. In the dialogue, souvent follows vas, and seulement follows prends.
example: Tu vas souvent au café. | You often go to the café.
example: Je prends seulement un thé. | I only have a tea.

[quiz]
quiz: What does souvent mean? | often | rarely | almost | A | Souvent means often.
quiz: Which word means especially? | plutôt | surtout | seulement | B | Surtout means especially.
quiz: What does “tellement” mean? | almost | often | so | C | tellement means “so”.
quiz: What does “vraiment” mean? | really | especially | so | A | vraiment means “really”.
quiz: What does “l'avis” mean? | true | opinion | idea | B | l'avis means “opinion”.
