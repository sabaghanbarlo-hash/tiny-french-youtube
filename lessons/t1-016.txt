title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 16 · Directions and positions
episode: T1-016
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Excuse-moi, où est la boulangerie ? | Excuse me, where is the bakery?
Marinette: Elle est là, près de la place. | It’s there, near the square.
Adrien: On continue tout droit ? | Do we keep going straight ahead?
Marinette: Oui, puis tourne à gauche. | Yes, then turn left.
Adrien: Le café est à droite ? | Is the café on the right?
Marinette: Non, il est devant le musée, pas derrière. | No, it’s in front of the museum, not behind it.
Adrien: La gare est loin d’ici ? | Is the station far from here?
Marinette: Non, elle est près d’ici. Tourne à droite. | No, it’s near here. Turn right.

[vocab]
vocab: à gauche | to the left | ah GOHSH
example: Le café est à gauche. | The café is on the left.
vocab: à droite | to the right | ah DRWAHT
example: Le magasin est à droite. | The store is on the right.
vocab: tout droit | straight ahead | too DRWAH
example: La route va tout droit. | The road goes straight ahead.
vocab: devant | in front of | duh-VAHN
example: Le bus est devant l'école. | The bus is in front of the school.
vocab: derrière | behind | deh-RYEHR
example: Le jardin est derrière la maison. | The garden is behind the house.
vocab: près de | near | PREH duh
example: J'habite près de la gare. | I live near the station.
vocab: loin de | far from | LWAHN duh
example: Le parc est loin d'ici. | The park is far from here.
vocab: ici | here | ee-SEE
example: Je suis ici. | I am here.
vocab: là | there | LAH
example: Le métro est là. | The metro is there.
vocab: tourner | to turn | toor-NAY
example: Je tourne à gauche. | I turn left.

[grammar]
grammar: Giving a simple command | To give an informal direction to one person, use the tu command form without the subject tu. For regular -er verbs, the command ends in -e: tu tournes becomes tourne.
example: Tourne à gauche. | Turn left.
example: Tourne à droite. | Turn right.

[quiz]
quiz: What does « tout droit » mean? | Straight ahead | Behind | Near | A | « Tout droit » means to continue straight ahead.
quiz: How do you say “to the left” in French? | à droite | à gauche | devant | B | « À gauche » means to the left.
quiz: What does “près de” mean? | straight ahead | near | to turn | B | près de means “near”.
quiz: What does “ici” mean? | there | in front of | here | C | ici means “here”.
quiz: What does “le lundi” mean? | Sunday | Monday | Wednesday | B | le lundi means “Monday”.
