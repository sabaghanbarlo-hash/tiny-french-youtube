title: Days of the Week
episode: EF1
scene: home

[story]
Adrien: Quel jour sommes-nous aujourd'hui ? | What day is it today?
Marinette: Nous sommes jeudi. | It's Thursday.
Adrien: Et demain ? | And tomorrow?
Marinette: Demain, c'est vendredi. Hier, c'était mercredi. | Tomorrow is Friday. Yesterday was Wednesday.
Adrien: Tu travailles le samedi ? | Do you work on Saturdays?
Marinette: Non, le samedi, je ne travaille pas. | No, on Saturdays I don't work.
Adrien: Et le dimanche ? | And on Sundays?
Marinette: Le dimanche, je dors ! | On Sundays I sleep!

[grammar]
grammar: No capital letters | Days of the week are masculine and are written with a small letter in French: lundi, mardi, jeudi. The week starts on Monday on French calendars.
example: lundi, mardi, mercredi | Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday
grammar: Samedi or le samedi? | A day on its own means this coming day: samedi, on va au cinéma. With le it means every week: le samedi, je dors.
example: Samedi, je travaille. | This Saturday I'm working.
example: Le samedi, je ne travaille pas. | On Saturdays I don't work.
grammar: Quel jour sommes-nous ? | Ask Quel jour sommes-nous ? or Quel jour est-ce ? and answer Nous sommes + day, or simply C'est + day. Hier c'était means "yesterday it was".
example: Nous sommes jeudi. | It's Thursday.
example: Hier, c'était mercredi. | Yesterday was Wednesday.
