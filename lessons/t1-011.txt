title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 11 · Family and relatives
episode: T1-011
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Tu me montres ta famille ? | Will you show me your family?
Marinette: Oui, voici mes parents, mon grand-père et ma grand-mère. | Yes, here are my parents, my grandfather, and my grandmother.
Adrien: Cette fille, c’est ta sœur ? | Is this girl your sister?
Marinette: C’est ma demi-sœur, et ce garçon est mon demi-frère. | She’s my half-sister, and this boy is my half-brother.
Adrien: Et ce garçon, c’est ton demi-frère ? | And this boy, is he your half-brother?
Marinette: Oui, nous avons le même père. | Yes, we have the same father.
Adrien: Le mari de ta mère, c’est ton beau-père ? | Is your mother’s husband your stepfather?
Marinette: Oui. La femme de mon père est ma belle-mère. | Yes. My father’s wife is my stepmother.

[grammar]
grammar: Possessive adjectives | French possessive adjectives agree with the noun that follows them, not with the person who owns it. Use mon with a masculine singular noun, ma with a feminine singular noun, and mes with a plural noun. Before a feminine singular noun beginning with a vowel or mute h, use mon to avoid two vowel sounds.
example: Voici mes parents. | Here are my parents.
example: C’est ma demi-sœur. | She’s my half-sister.
