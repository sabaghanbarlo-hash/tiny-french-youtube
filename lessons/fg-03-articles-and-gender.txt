title: Articles and Gender
episode: FG3
scene: kitchen

[story]
Adrien: Voilà le pain et la confiture. | Here are the bread and the jam.
Marinette: Merci. Tu as un couteau et une assiette ? | Thanks. Do you have a knife and a plate?
Adrien: Oui, voici un couteau et une assiette. Et les fruits ? | Yes, here's a knife and a plate. And the fruit?
Marinette: Les pommes sont sur la table. L'orange est dans le sac. | The apples are on the table. The orange is in the bag.
Adrien: Où est le fromage ? | Where is the cheese?
Marinette: Le fromage est dans le frigo. Il y a aussi des œufs. | The cheese is in the fridge. There are also some eggs.
Adrien: Super ! Un petit-déjeuner parfait. | Great! A perfect breakfast.

[grammar]
grammar: Every noun has a gender | French nouns are masculine or feminine, even objects. The gender is shown by the little word before the noun. Always learn the noun together with its article: le pain, la confiture, not just pain and confiture.
example: un couteau (m.) | a knife
example: une assiette (f.) | a plate
grammar: A or the? | Un / une mean “a”: un couteau, une assiette. Le / la mean “the”: le pain, la table. Before a vowel or a silent h, le and la both become l': l'orange, l'hôtel. For more than one thing use les (the) or des (some): les pommes, des œufs.
example: le pain, la table, l'orange | the bread, the table, the orange
example: les pommes, des œufs | the apples, some eggs
grammar: Making a plural | Most plurals just add -s, which you do not pronounce. The article is what you hear: la pomme / les pommes, un œuf / des œufs. Before a vowel, les and des link with a z sound: les œufs (lay-ZUH).
example: une pomme → des pommes | an apple → some apples
example: un œuf → des œufs | an egg → some eggs

[quiz]
quiz: Which article means “a” for a feminine noun? | une | un | le | A | Une goes with feminine nouns.
quiz: What is “the orange” in French? | le orange | l'orange | la orange | B | Before a vowel, le and la become l'.
quiz: What does “des œufs” mean? | the egg | an egg | some eggs | C | Des = some (plural).
