title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 21 · Shopping and money
episode: T1-021
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Tu veux acheter ce livre ? | Do you want to buy this book?
Marinette: Oui. Combien coûte ce livre ? | Yes. How much does this book cost?
Adrien: Le prix est de dix euros. | The price is ten euros.
Marinette: C'est un peu cher. J'ai de l'argent. Je peux payer par carte ? | It's a little expensive. I have money. Can I pay by card?
Adrien: Oui, le paiement par carte est possible. | Yes, payment by card is possible.
Marinette: Alors, je paie par carte. Et ce stylo ? | Then, I'll pay by card. And this pen?
Adrien: Il coûte un euro. | It costs one euro.
Marinette: Parfait, je prends les deux. Merci ! | Perfect, I'll take both. Thanks!

[grammar]
grammar: Vouloir + an infinitive | After vouloir (to want), use the next verb in its infinitive form. The infinitive stays the same, even when the subject changes.
example: Je veux acheter ce livre. | I want to buy this book.
example: Tu veux payer par carte ? | Do you want to pay by card?
