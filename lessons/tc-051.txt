title: Tiny French Conversations · Lesson 51 · Welcome to My Home
episode: TC-051
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Bonjour, Marinette, bienvenue chez moi ! | Hello, Marinette, welcome to my home!
Marinette: Bonjour, Adrien ! Merci ! | Hello, Adrien! Thank you!
Adrien: Entre, s'il te plaît. Voici le salon. | Come in, please. Here is the living room.
Marinette: Merci. Le salon est très beau. | Thank you. The living room is very beautiful.
Adrien: Tu veux t'asseoir ? | Would you like to sit down?
Marinette: Oui, avec plaisir. | Yes, gladly.
Adrien: Tu veux de l'eau ou du thé ? | Would you like water or tea?
Marinette: De l'eau, s'il te plaît. | Water, please.
Adrien: Voilà. Le salon est calme et confortable. | There you go. The living room is quiet and comfortable.

[grammar]
grammar: Tu veux… ? | Use tu veux… ? to ask someone informally what they want or would like. Follow it with an infinitive for an activity or a noun for a thing.
example: Tu veux t'asseoir ? | Would you like to sit down?
example: Tu veux de l'eau ou du thé ? | Would you like water or tea?
