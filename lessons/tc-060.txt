title: Tiny French Conversations · Lesson 60 · Getting Ready for Bed
episode: TC-060
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Il est tard, Marinette. Tu te brosses les dents ? | It’s late, Marinette. Are you brushing your teeth?
Marinette: Oui, je me brosse les dents maintenant. | Yes, I’m brushing my teeth now.
Adrien: Et après, tu te couches ? | And after that, are you going to bed?
Marinette: Oui, mais je veux lire un peu. | Yes, but I want to read a little.
Adrien: D'accord. Tu peux lire un livre, puis dormir. | All right. You can read a book, then sleep.
Marinette: Tu viens me dire bonsoir, s'il te plaît ? | Will you come say good night to me, please?
Adrien: Bien sûr. Je viens dans la chambre. | Of course. I’m coming into the bedroom.
Marinette: Merci. Tu éteins la lampe ? | Thanks. Will you turn off the lamp?
Adrien: Oui. Dors bien, Marinette. | Yes. Sleep well, Marinette.

[grammar]
grammar: Reflexive verbs for routines | French often uses a reflexive pronoun with verbs for actions you do to yourself. The pronoun changes with the subject: je me brosse, while tu te couches.
example: Je me brosse les dents. | I brush my teeth.
example: Tu te couches ? | Are you going to bed?
