title: Tiny French Conversations · Lesson 95 · Music We Like
episode: TC-095
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Salut, Marinette ! Tu aimes la musique ? | Hi, Marinette! Do you like music?
Marinette: Oui, beaucoup ! Et toi, tu écoutes souvent la radio ? | Yes, a lot! And you, do you often listen to the radio?
Adrien: Oui, j’écoute la radio, surtout le soir. | Yes, I listen to the radio, especially in the evening.
Marinette: Tu aimes la musique aussi ? | Do you like music too?
Adrien: Oui, et j’aime chanter. Et toi ? | Yes, and I like to sing. And you?
Marinette: Moi, j’aime danser, mais je chante peu. | I like to dance, but I don’t sing much.
Adrien: Tu veux aller au concert demain ? | Do you want to go to the concert tomorrow?
Marinette: Oui, avec plaisir ! Quand ? | Yes, gladly! When?
Adrien: À sept heures, si tu veux. | At seven o’clock, if you like.

[grammar]
grammar: Aimer + an activity | Use aimer followed by an infinitive to say that you like doing something. The infinitive stays unchanged after aimer.
example: J’aime chanter. | I like to sing.
example: J’aime danser. | I like to dance.
