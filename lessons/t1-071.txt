title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 71 · Money and prices 2
episode: T1-071
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: À la caisse, le total est correct ? | At the checkout, is the total correct?
Marinette: Oui, et tu as une réduction de cinq euros. | Yes, and you have a five-euro discount.
Adrien: Super ! Je paie en espèces. | Great! I’ll pay in cash.
Marinette: Tu as de la monnaie ? J’ai une pièce. | Do you have any change? I have a coin.
Adrien: Oui, elle est dans mon portefeuille. | Yes, it’s in my wallet.
Marinette: N’oublie pas : le sac est gratuit aujourd’hui. | Don’t forget: the bag is free today.
Adrien: Parfait, je vais moins dépenser pour économiser. | Perfect, I’m going to spend less to save money.
Marinette: Bonne idée ! | Good idea!

vocab: économiser | to save | ay-koh-noh-mee-ZAY

example: Le café est gratuit. | The coffee is free.

[grammar]
grammar: The near future | Use aller followed by an infinitive to talk about something that is going to happen. The form of aller changes to match the subject, while the main verb stays in its infinitive form.
example: Je vais économiser. | I am going to save money.
example: Nous allons payer en espèces. | We are going to pay in cash.

[quiz]
quiz: What does une réduction mean? | A discount | A wallet | A coin | A | Une réduction means a discount or price reduction.
quiz: Where do you pay for your shopping? | À la caisse | Dans le portefeuille | En espèces | A | La caisse is the checkout.
quiz: What does “les espèces” mean? | to save | checkout | cash | C | les espèces means “cash”.
quiz: What does “économiser” mean? | to save | bill | to spend | A | économiser means “to save”.
