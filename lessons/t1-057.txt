title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 57 · Verbs: open, close, put, take, bring, carry, turn on...
episode: T1-057
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Tu peux ouvrir la fenêtre, s’il te plaît ? | Can you open the window, please?
Marinette: Oui. J’allume la lampe aussi. | Yes. I’ll turn on the lamp too.
Adrien: Pose ton sac ici et enlève ton manteau, s’il te plaît. | Put your bag down here and take off your coat, please.
Marinette: D’accord, je mets le livre sur la table. | Okay, I’ll put the book on the table.
Adrien: Tu amènes Zoé chez nous ce soir ? | Are you bringing Zoé to our place tonight?
Marinette: Oui, je l’emmène avec moi après le cours. | Yes, I’m taking her along with me after class.
Adrien: Je porte les boissons. Tu peux fermer la porte ? | I’ll carry the drinks. Can you close the door?
Marinette: Bien sûr, et j’éteins la lampe en partant. | Of course, and I’ll turn off the lamp when we leave.

[vocab]
vocab: ouvrir | to open | oo-VREER
example: J'ouvre le placard. | I open the cupboard.
vocab: fermer | to close | fair-MAY
example: Elle ferme le rideau. | She closes the curtain.
vocab: mettre | to put | METR
example: Je mets du sucre. | I put some sugar.
vocab: porter | to carry; to wear | por-TAY
example: Il porte une caisse. | He carries a crate.
vocab: allumer | to turn on | ah-lyoo-MAY
example: J'allume la lampe. | I turn on the lamp.
vocab: éteindre | to turn off | ay-TANDR
example: J'éteins la lampe. | I turn off the lamp.
vocab: poser | to put down | poh-ZAY
example: Je pose la tasse. | I put down the cup.
vocab: enlever | to remove; to take off | ahn-luh-VAY
example: Il enlève son chapeau. | He takes off his hat.
vocab: amener | to bring someone | ahm-NAY
example: Zoé amène Paul chez moi. | Zoé brings Paul to my place.
vocab: emmener | to take someone along | ahn-mNAY
example: Zoé emmène Paul. | Zoé takes Paul along.

[grammar]
grammar: Object pronouns before verbs | In French, a short object pronoun usually goes before the verb. Le or la becomes l’ before a vowel sound or a silent h, as in « je l’emmène » (I take him or her along).
example: Je l’emmène avec moi. | I’m taking him or her along with me.
example: Je la porte aujourd’hui. | I’m wearing it today.
