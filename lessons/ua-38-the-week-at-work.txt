title: My Work Week
episode: UA38
scene: classroom

[story]
Marinette: Tu travailles du lundi au vendredi ? | Do you work Monday to Friday?
Adrien: Oui, de neuf heures à dix-sept heures. Mercredi matin, j'ai une réunion. | Yes, from nine to five. On Wednesday morning, I have a meeting.
Marinette: Tu es libre jeudi après-midi ? | Are you free on Thursday afternoon?
Adrien: Jeudi, j'ai rendez-vous chez le dentiste à quinze heures. | Thursday, I have an appointment at the dentist's at three p.m.
Marinette: Alors vendredi ? | Then Friday?
Adrien: Vendredi matin, c'est parfait. On déjeune ensemble ? | Friday morning is perfect. Shall we have lunch together?

[grammar]
grammar: Days and periods of the day | Use du au for from to: du lundi au vendredi. With a single day, use no article for this coming day, and add le for every week: le lundi means every Monday. For the part of the day, add matin, après-midi or soir: jeudi après-midi, vendredi matin.
example: Du lundi au vendredi. | Monday to Friday.
example: Le lundi, je travaille. | On Mondays I work.
grammar: Avoir rendez-vous | Avoir rendez-vous means to have an appointment: j'ai rendez-vous, tu as rendez-vous, il a rendez-vous. Use chez for a person and à plus time for when. To say you are free or busy, use libre or occupé.
example: J'ai rendez-vous à quinze heures. | I have an appointment at three.
example: Je suis libre jeudi matin. | I'm free on Thursday morning.
