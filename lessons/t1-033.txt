title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 33 · Opposite adjectives
episode: T1-033
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Bonjour, Marinette ! Le trajet est long. | Hello, Marinette! The trip is long.
Marinette: Oui, mais le bus est rapide, pas lent. | Yes, but the bus is fast, not slow.
Adrien: La rue est propre, mais le trottoir est sale. | The street is clean, but the sidewalk is dirty.
Marinette: Le café est ouvert ? La porte est ouverte. | Is the café open? The door is open.
Adrien: Oui. Cette file est courte, heureusement ! | Yes. This line is short, thankfully!
Marinette: La salle est pleine, mais la rue est vide. | The room is full, but the street is empty.
Adrien: Le magasin est fermé maintenant ? | Is the shop closed now?
Marinette: Oui, désolée. | Yes, sorry.

[grammar]
grammar: Adjective agreement | In French, many adjectives change to match the gender and number of the noun they describe. For a feminine singular noun, a final -e is often added, as in courte, pleine, and ouverte.
example: Cette file est courte. | This line is short.
example: La porte est ouverte. | The door is open.
