title: Tiny French Conversations · Lesson 65 · Asking the Teacher
episode: TC-065
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Bonjour, madame. Après le cours, j'ai une question. | Hello, ma’am. After class, I have a question.
Marinette: Bonjour, Adrien. Oui, bien sûr. | Hello, Adrien. Yes, of course.
Adrien: Comment dit-on ce mot ? | How do you say this word?
Marinette: On dit « bonjour ». | You say “hello.”
Adrien: Merci. Et comment écrit-on ce mot ? | Thank you. And how do you spell this word?
Marinette: B-o-n-j-o-u-r. | B-o-n-j-o-u-r.
Adrien: Je ne comprends pas. | I don’t understand.
Marinette: Je peux répéter. | I can repeat.
Adrien: Merci beaucoup. Au revoir, madame. | Thank you very much. Goodbye, ma’am.

[grammar]
grammar: Negation with ne...pas | To say that you do not do or understand something, put ne before the verb and pas after it. In everyday spoken French, people often drop ne, but it is included here for a clear beginner pattern.
example: Je ne comprends pas. | I don't understand.
example: Je ne sais pas. | I don't know.
