title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 6 · Time Words
episode: T1-006
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Bonjour, Marinette ! Qu'est-ce que tu fais aujourd'hui ? | Hi, Marinette! What are you doing today?
Marinette: Je vais au café le matin. Et toi ? | I go to the café in the morning. And you?
Adrien: Je travaille maintenant, mais je suis libre ce soir. | I'm working now, but I'm free this evening.
Marinette: Super ! On peut se voir demain après le cours. | Great! We can see each other tomorrow after class.
Adrien: Oui. Hier, j'ai étudié toute la journée. | Yes. Yesterday, I studied all day.
Marinette: Tu étudies beaucoup chaque semaine ! | You study a lot every week!
Adrien: Oui, mais j'aime prendre du temps pour mes amis. | Yes, but I like to make time for my friends.
Marinette: Moi aussi ! Un an, ça passe vite ! | Me too! A year goes by fast!

[vocab]
vocab: aujourd'hui | today | oh-zhoor-DWEE
example: Aujourd'hui, je travaille. | Today, I’m working.
vocab: demain | tomorrow | duh-MAN
example: Demain, je pars tôt. | Tomorrow, I’m leaving early.
vocab: hier | yesterday | YAIR
example: Hier, j'ai travaillé. | Yesterday, I worked.
vocab: maintenant | now | mant-NAHN
example: Maintenant, je comprends. | Now, I understand.
vocab: le jour | day | luh ZHOOR
example: Le jour commence tôt. | The day starts early.
vocab: le matin | morning | luh ma-TAN
example: Le matin, je bois du café. | In the morning, I drink coffee.
vocab: le soir | evening | luh SWAHR
example: Le soir, nous mangeons ensemble. | In the evening, we eat together.
vocab: un an | year | uh NAHN
example: Un an passe vite. | A year goes by quickly.
vocab: le temps | time | luh TAHN
example: Le temps passe vite. | Time goes by quickly.
vocab: la semaine | week | lah suh-MEN
example: La semaine commence lundi. | The week starts on Monday.

[grammar]
grammar: French noun articles | French nouns often appear with an article, which shows their grammatical gender. Use le with masculine nouns and la with feminine nouns; learn the article together with each noun.
example: le matin | the morning
example: la semaine | the week
