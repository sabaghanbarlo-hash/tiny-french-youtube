title: The French U and R
episode: FS4
scene: classroom

[story]
Marinette: Écoute : tu. | Listen: tu.
Adrien: Tou ? | Too?
Marinette: Non, pas « tou ». Dis « i » avec les lèvres rondes : tu. | No, not “too”. Say “ee” with rounded lips: tu.
Adrien: Tu. Tu. C'est mieux ? | Tu. Tu. Is that better?
Marinette: Oui ! Maintenant, la rue. | Yes! Now, la rue.
Adrien: La rue… Le r, c'est difficile ! | La rue… The r is difficult!
Marinette: Le r est dans la gorge. Répète : rouge, merci, très bien. | The r is in the throat. Repeat: rouge, merci, très bien.
Adrien: Rouge. Merci. Très bien ! | Rouge. Merci. Très bien!

[grammar]
grammar: u is not ou | French has two different sounds. U is made by saying "ee" and rounding your lips as if for "oo": tu, rue, salut. Ou is the plain English "oo": tout, rouge, bonjour. The difference changes the meaning: rue and roue.
example: tu / tout | TEW / TOO
example: rue / roue | REW / ROO
grammar: The French r | The French r is made at the back of your throat, like a soft gargle. Start by saying "k" or "g" and then relax the movement so air still passes. Do not roll it with your tongue.
example: rouge | red
example: merci | thank you
grammar: Short commands for learners | To tell a friend to do something, use the tu command: écoute, dis, répète. They are very useful when practising.
example: Écoute ! | Listen!
example: Répète : tu. | Repeat: tu.
