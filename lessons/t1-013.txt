title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 13 · Days of the week and date words
episode: T1-013
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Bonjour, Marinette ! Aujourd’hui, c’est samedi 13. Tu es libre lundi ? | Hi, Marinette! Today is Saturday the 13th. Are you free Monday?
Marinette: Bonjour, Adrien. Oui ! Le mardi, j’ai cours ; le mercredi aussi. | Hi, Adrien. Yes! I have class on Tuesdays; on Wednesdays too.
Adrien: Et le jeudi, tu es libre ? | And are you free on Thursdays?
Marinette: Non, je travaille le vendredi. | No, I work on Fridays.
Adrien: Alors, on se voit lundi 15 ? Après-demain, c’est lundi. | So, shall we see each other Monday the 15th? The day after tomorrow is Monday.
Marinette: Oui ! Le lendemain, mardi 16, j’ai cours. | Yes! The following day, Tuesday the 16th, I have class.
Adrien: D’accord. On prépare tout la veille, dimanche 14 ? | Okay. Shall we prepare everything the day before, Sunday the 14th?
Marinette: Avec plaisir ! Le samedi, je fais du vélo. | Gladly! On Saturdays, I ride my bike.

[vocab]
vocab: le lundi | Monday | luhn-DEE
example: Le lundi, je vais au travail. | On Mondays, I go to work.
vocab: le mardi | Tuesday | mahr-DEE
example: Le mardi, elle joue au tennis. | On Tuesdays, she plays tennis.
vocab: le mercredi | Wednesday | mehr-kruh-DEE
example: Le mercredi, nous faisons du sport. | On Wednesdays, we exercise.
vocab: le jeudi | Thursday | zhuh-DEE
example: Le jeudi, il travaille à la maison. | On Thursdays, he works at home.
vocab: le vendredi | Friday | vahn-druh-DEE
example: Le vendredi, je retrouve mes amis. | On Fridays, I meet my friends.
vocab: le samedi | Saturday | sahm-DEE
example: Le samedi, ils vont au marché. | On Saturdays, they go to the market.
vocab: le dimanche | Sunday | dee-MAHNSH
example: Le dimanche, nous mangeons ensemble. | On Sundays, we eat together.
vocab: le lendemain | the following day | lahn-duh-MAN
example: Le lendemain, nous travaillons. | The following day, we work.
vocab: la veille | the day before | lah vey
example: La veille, je suis arrivé. | The day before, I arrived.
vocab: après-demain | the day after tomorrow | ah-preh duh-MAN
example: On part après-demain. | We're leaving the day after tomorrow.

[grammar]
grammar: Le with weekdays | In French, le + weekday often describes a regular routine, like “on Tuesdays.” For a specific upcoming day with a date, French often uses the weekday without le.
example: Le mardi, j’ai cours. | I have class on Tuesdays.
example: On se voit lundi 15. | We’ll see each other Monday the 15th.
