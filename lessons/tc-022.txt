title: Tiny French Conversations · Lesson 22 · Buying Bread
episode: TC-022
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Bonjour, je voudrais un pain, s'il vous plaît. | Hello, I would like a loaf of bread, please.
Marinette: Bonjour. Un pain, oui. | Hello. A loaf of bread, yes.
Adrien: Il est frais ? | Is it fresh?
Marinette: Oui, il est frais aujourd'hui. | Yes, it's fresh today.
Adrien: Combien coûte le pain ? | How much does the bread cost?
Marinette: Un euro, s'il vous plaît. | One euro, please.
Adrien: Voilà. Merci beaucoup. | There you are. Thank you very much.
Marinette: Merci. Bonne journée ! | Thank you. Have a nice day!
Adrien: Au revoir, madame. | Goodbye, ma'am.

[grammar]
grammar: A polite request with voudrais | Use “Je voudrais” followed by a noun to make a polite request. It means “I would like”; add “s'il vous plaît” to say “please.”
example: Je voudrais un pain, s'il vous plaît. | I would like a loaf of bread, please.
example: Je voudrais un livre. | I would like a book.
