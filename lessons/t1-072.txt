title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 72 · Fashion and appearance
episode: T1-072
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Salut, Marinette ! Tu aimes la mode ? | Hi, Marinette! Do you like fashion?
Marinette: Oui, j'aime la mode et les vêtements. | Yes, I like fashion and clothes.
Adrien: Ton chapeau est joli, et tes lunettes aussi. | Your hat is pretty, and your glasses too.
Marinette: Merci ! Je porte un jean et des baskets aujourd'hui. | Thanks! I'm wearing jeans and sneakers today.
Adrien: Pour la fête, je mets un costume élégant. | For the party, I'm putting on an elegant suit.
Marinette: Très bien ! Tu es élégante. | Very good! You look elegant.
Adrien: Et ton maquillage est très joli. | And your makeup is very pretty.
Marinette: Merci, Adrien. On y va ? | Thanks, Adrien. Shall we go?

[grammar]
grammar: The definite articles le, la, and les | French uses le before a masculine singular noun, la before a feminine singular noun, and les before plural nouns. Before a vowel or silent h, le and la become l’. These articles usually mean “the,” but French also uses them when talking about things in general.
example: J'aime la mode. | I like fashion.
example: J'aime les vêtements. | I like clothes.
