title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 63 · Describing people
episode: T1-063
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Comment est ton ami Léo ? | What is your friend Léo like?
Marinette: Il est gentil, drôle et intelligent. | He is kind, funny, and intelligent.
Adrien: Il est blond aussi ? | Is he blond too?
Marinette: Oui, il est sympathique, mais un peu timide. | Yes, he is nice, but a little shy.
Adrien: Et son père ? | And his father?
Marinette: Il est sérieux et fort. | He is serious and strong.
Adrien: Et son grand-père ? | And his grandfather?
Marinette: Il est âgé et mince, mais très gentil ! | He is elderly and slim, but very kind!

[grammar]
grammar: Adjective agreement | French adjectives usually agree with the person or thing they describe. For many adjectives, add -e for the feminine form; some, like gentil, change spelling in the feminine.
example: Léo est blond. | Léo is blond.
example: Léa est blonde. | Léa is blonde.

[quiz]
quiz: What does drôle mean? | funny | shy | strong | A | Drôle means funny.
quiz: Which word means shy? | mince | timide | âgé | B | Timide means shy.
quiz: What does “mince” mean? | blond | slim | funny | B | mince means “slim”.
quiz: What does “âgé” mean? | elderly | slim | blond | A | âgé means “elderly”.
quiz: What does “aucun” mean? | a liter | no | more | B | aucun means “no”.
