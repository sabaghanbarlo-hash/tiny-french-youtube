title: Reading Signs and Menus
episode: FS5

[story]
Adrien: Regarde : « Boulangerie ». On entre ? | Look: “Boulangerie”. Shall we go in?
Marinette: Non, c'est fermé. Regarde le panneau : « Ouvert à huit heures ». | No, it's closed. Look at the sign: “Open at eight o'clock”.
Adrien: Alors, le café en face. La porte : « Poussez » ou « Tirez » ? | Then the café opposite. The door: “Push” or “Pull”?
Marinette: Poussez ! | Push!
Adrien: Au menu : « soupe du jour », « fromage », « tarte aux pommes ». | On the menu: “soup of the day”, “cheese”, “apple tart”.
Marinette: Je prends la soupe du jour. Et toi ? | I'll have the soup of the day. And you?
Adrien: Moi, la tarte aux pommes. Où sont les toilettes ? | Me, the apple tart. Where are the toilets?
Marinette: Au fond, à gauche. Regarde : « Toilettes ». | At the back, on the left. Look: “Toilettes”.

[grammar]
grammar: Read by the rules | Put the rules together. In poussez, ou is "oo" and the final z is silent, so you say poo-SAY. In fermé, the é is "ay" and the m is pronounced: fehr-MAY. In sortie, the final e is silent: sor-TEE. Silent endings, accents and nasal vowels do most of the work.
example: poussez | poo-SAY
example: fermé | fehr-MAY
grammar: Words that look like English | Many French words look like English: restaurant, hôtel, musée, information, menu. Their meaning is easy to guess, but say them the French way: hôtel is oh-TEL, musée is mew-ZAY.
example: un restaurant | a restaurant
example: un musée | a museum
grammar: Signs speak to you with vous | Signs and notices often use the vous command form: poussez, tirez, entrez, attendez. Recognise the -ez ending and you will understand many instructions.
example: Entrez ! | Come in!
example: Poussez. | Push.

[quiz]
quiz: What does “fermé” mean? | Open | Exit | Closed | C | Fermé = closed, ouvert = open.
quiz: How do you read “poussez”? | poo-SAY | poo-SEZ | pows-ZEZ | A | Ou is “oo” and the final z is silent.
quiz: A sign over a door says “Sortie”. What is it? | The entrance | The exit | The toilets | B | Sortie = exit.
quiz: Which word means “pull”? | Poussez | Entrez | Tirez | C | Tirez = pull, poussez = push.
