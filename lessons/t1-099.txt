title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 99 · Verbs 11
episode: T1-099
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Tu peux garder mon sac, s’il te plaît ? | Can you keep my bag, please?
Marinette: Oui. Je vais te montrer la cuisine. | Yes. I’ll show you the kitchen.
Adrien: Merci ! Je m’assois ici ? | Thanks! Shall I sit down here?
Marinette: Oui, puis nous allons cuisiner. | Yes, then we’re going to cook.
Adrien: Après, je vais nettoyer la table et essuyer les verres. | Afterward, I’ll clean the table and wipe the glasses.
Marinette: D’accord. Je promets de t’aider, puis on va s’amuser. | Okay. I promise to help you, then we’ll have fun.
Adrien: Super ! Après le repas, je vais m’allonger un peu. | Great! After the meal, I’ll lie down for a bit.
Marinette: Et moi, je vais sauter de joie ! | And I’ll jump for joy!

[vocab]
vocab: garder | to keep | gar-DAY
example: Je garde les clés. | I keep the keys.
vocab: montrer | to show | mohn-TRAY
example: Tu me montres la photo. | You show me the photo.
vocab: s'asseoir | to sit down | sah-SWAHR
example: Je m'assois ici. | I sit here.
vocab: nettoyer | to clean | neh-twah-YAY
example: Je nettoie la table. | I clean the table.
vocab: cuisiner | to cook | kwee-zee-NAY
example: Elle cuisine ce soir. | She cooks tonight.
vocab: s'allonger | to lie down | sah-lohn-ZHAY
example: Il s'allonge sur le canapé. | He lies down on the couch.
vocab: promettre | to promise | proh-METR
example: Je te promets de venir demain. | I promise to come tomorrow.
vocab: essuyer | to wipe | eh-swee-YAY
example: Tu peux essuyer la table ? | Can you wipe the table?
vocab: s'amuser | to have fun | sah-moo-ZAY
example: Les enfants s'amusent dans le jardin. | The children are having fun in the garden.
vocab: sauter | to jump | soh-TAY
example: Je saute par-dessus la flaque. | I jump over the puddle.

[grammar]
grammar: Reflexive verbs | Some French verbs are reflexive: the pronoun changes with the subject and comes before the verb. With je, the pronoun is me, or m’ before a vowel.
example: Je m’assois ici. | I sit down here.
example: Tu t’allonges. | You lie down.
