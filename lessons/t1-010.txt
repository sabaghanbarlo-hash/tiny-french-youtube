[story]
Adrien: Tu as déjà fini tes devoirs ? | Have you already finished your homework?
Marinette: Oui, parce que c'était très facile. | Yes, because it was very easy.
Adrien: Tu travailles toujours ici ? | Do you still work here?
Marinette: Oui, et je peux aussi t'aider. | Yes, and I can help you too.
Adrien: Merci ! J'aime beaucoup ce café. | Thanks! I like this café a lot.
Marinette: Il est plus calme et moins bruyant le matin. | It's calmer and less noisy in the morning.
Adrien: Tu viens encore demain ? | Are you coming again tomorrow?
Marinette: Oui, sauf le dimanche : je ne viens jamais ce jour-là. | Yes, except on Sundays: I never come that day.

example: Je reste parce que j'attends Marie. | I stay because I am waiting for Marie.

[grammar]
grammar: Saying “never” | In standard French, put ne before the verb and jamais after it to say “never.” In everyday speech, people sometimes leave out ne, but it is helpful to learn the full form.
example: Je ne viens jamais le dimanche. | I never come on Sundays.
example: Il ne travaille jamais le soir. | He never works in the evening.
