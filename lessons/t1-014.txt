title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 14 · Colors and shapes
episode: T1-014
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Bonjour, Marinette ! Regarde le cercle bleu. | Hello, Marinette! Look at the blue circle.
Marinette: Bonjour, Adrien ! Le carré est rouge. | Hello, Adrien! The square is red.
Adrien: Le triangle est vert, et le cercle est rond. | The triangle is green, and the circle is round.
Marinette: La petite forme est jaune. | The little shape is yellow.
Adrien: Voilà un petit carré noir. | Here is a little black square.
Marinette: Merci ! Le cercle blanc est joli. | Thanks! The white circle is pretty.
Adrien: Tu préfères le triangle rouge ? | Do you prefer the red triangle?
Marinette: Oui, il est très beau, merci. | Yes, it is very nice, thanks.

[grammar]
grammar: Color adjectives agree | In French, color adjectives usually follow the noun and agree with it in gender and number. For example, bleu becomes bleue with a feminine noun.
example: Voici le cercle bleu. | Here is the blue circle.
example: Voici la forme bleue. | Here is the blue shape.
