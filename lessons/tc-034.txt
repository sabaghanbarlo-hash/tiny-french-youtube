title: Tiny French Conversations · Lesson 34 · Returning a Shirt
episode: TC-034
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Bonjour, je voudrais rendre le T-shirt, s'il vous plaît. | Hello, I would like to return the T-shirt, please.
Marinette: Bonjour. Bien sûr. Il est trop petit ? | Hello. Of course. Is it too small?
Adrien: Oui, il est trop petit. | Yes, it is too small.
Marinette: Vous voulez un autre T-shirt ? | Would you like another T-shirt?
Adrien: Non, merci, je préfère récupérer l'argent. | No, thank you. I prefer to get my money back.
Marinette: D'accord. Vous avez payé par carte ? | All right. Did you pay by card?
Adrien: Oui, par carte. | Yes, by card.
Marinette: Voilà l'argent. | Here is the money.
Adrien: Merci beaucoup. Au revoir. | Thank you very much. Goodbye.

[grammar]
grammar: Yes-or-no questions with rising intonation | In everyday French, you can ask a yes-or-no question by keeping the usual word order and raising your voice at the end. This is common in conversation and sounds natural and direct.
