title: Introducing Yourself
episode: FC2
scene: terrace

[story]
Adrien: Salut ! Comment tu t'appelles ? | Hi! What's your name?
Marinette: Je m'appelle Marinette. Et toi ? | My name is Marinette. And you?
Adrien: Moi, c'est Adrien. Tu es française ? | Me, I'm Adrien. Are you French?
Marinette: Oui, je suis française. J'habite à Paris. | Yes, I'm French. I live in Paris.
Marinette: Et toi ? Tu viens d'où ? | And you? Where are you from?
Adrien: Je viens du Canada, mais j'habite à Lyon. | I come from Canada, but I live in Lyon.
Marinette: Tu parles anglais ? | Do you speak English?
Adrien: Oui ! Je parle anglais et français. | Yes! I speak English and French.
Marinette: Moi, je parle français et un peu anglais. | Me, I speak French and a little English.

[grammar]
grammar: Je suis + nationality | Nationalities are adjectives. For a woman add an -e. Français becomes française, canadien becomes canadienne. The masculine and feminine often sound different here, because the final consonant is pronounced in the feminine.
example: Je suis français. | I am French. (a man)
example: Je suis française. | I am French. (a woman)
example: Je suis canadienne. | I am Canadian. (a woman)
grammar: J'habite à… and je viens de… | Use à with a city to say where you live, and de with a city to say where you come from. With a masculine country such as le Canada, de + le becomes du.
example: J'habite à Lyon. | I live in Lyon.
example: Je viens de Paris. | I come from Paris.
example: Je viens du Canada. | I come from Canada.
grammar: Je parle + language | Use je parle with the name of a language, with no word for "the". Add un peu to be modest.
example: Je parle français. | I speak French.
example: Je parle un peu anglais. | I speak a little English.

[quiz]
quiz: How do you ask a friend their name? | Tu viens d'où ? | Comment tu t'appelles ? | Tu parles anglais ? | B | Comment tu t'appelles ? literally asks how you call yourself.
quiz: Marinette is a woman. How does she say she is French? | Je suis français | Je suis habite | Je suis française | C | Add -e for a woman: française.
quiz: What does “Tu viens d'où ?” mean? | Where are you from? | Where do you live? | What is your name? | A | D'où means “from where”.
quiz: What does “Je parle un peu anglais” mean? | I speak English well | I speak a little English | I don't speak English | B | Un peu means a little.
