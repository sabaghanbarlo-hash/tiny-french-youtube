title: French Rhythm and Questions
episode: FS7

[story]
Marinette: En français, chaque syllabe est presque égale. | In French, every syllable is almost equal.
Adrien: Pas comme en anglais ? | Not like in English?
Marinette: Non. Écoute : bon-jour. Pas de syllabe très forte au milieu. | No. Listen: bon-jour. No very strong syllable in the middle.
Adrien: Bon-jour. Cho-co-lat. Ma-ri-nette. | Bon-jour. Cho-co-lat. Ma-ri-nette.
Marinette: Très bien ! Pour une question, la voix monte à la fin. | Very good! For a question, the voice goes up at the end.
Adrien: Tu aimes le chocolat ? | Do you like chocolate?
Marinette: Oui, j'aime le chocolat. | Yes, I like chocolate.
Adrien: Et avec est-ce que ? | And with est-ce que?
Marinette: Est-ce que tu aimes le thé ? C'est pareil, mais plus long. | Est-ce que tu aimes le thé? It's the same, but longer.
Adrien: Maintenant, je parle comme un Français ! | Now I speak like a Frenchman!
Marinette: Presque ! | Almost!

[grammar]
grammar: Even rhythm | French syllables are almost equal in length, and the only longer syllable is the last one of a group of words. Say cho-co-LAT and ma-ri-NETTE, with no strong stress in the middle.
example: bon-jour | hello
example: cho-co-lat | chocolate
grammar: Questions by intonation | The easiest way to ask a yes / no question is to say a normal sentence and let your voice rise at the end. In writing, add a question mark.
example: Tu aimes le chocolat ? | Do you like chocolate?
example: Tu parles français ? | Do you speak French?
grammar: Est-ce que | Put est-ce que in front of a normal sentence to make it a question. The rest of the sentence keeps its normal order. Que becomes qu' before a vowel.
example: Est-ce que tu aimes le thé ? | Do you like tea?
example: Est-ce qu'il parle anglais ? | Does he speak English?

[quiz]
quiz: Where is the longer syllable in French? | On the first syllable | On the middle syllable | On the last syllable of a group of words | C | Say cho-co-LAT.
quiz: How do you turn “Tu aimes le thé.” into a question? | Raise your voice at the end | Lower your voice | Add the word pas | A | Rising intonation makes a yes / no question.

quiz: What does “C'est pareil” mean? | It's different | It's long | It's the same | C | Pareil = the same.
