title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 46 · Travel and sightseeing
episode: T1-046
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Salut Marinette ! On va visiter le musée et le château demain ? | Hi, Marinette! Shall we visit the museum and the castle tomorrow?
Marinette: Oui, bonne idée ! Je veux voyager à Lyon avec toi. | Yes, good idea! I want to travel to Lyon with you.
Adrien: J'ai réservé l'hôtel. As-tu ton passeport ? | I booked the hotel. Do you have your passport?
Marinette: Oui, et ma valise est prête. | Yes, and my suitcase is ready.
Adrien: Super ! Le guide nous attend au musée à neuf heures. | Great! The guide is waiting for us at the museum at nine o'clock.
Marinette: Je veux prendre une photo du château. | I want to take a photo of the castle.
Adrien: Un touriste prend une photo près du château. | A tourist is taking a photo near the castle.
Marinette: Alors, allons-y ! | Then, let's go!

[vocab]
vocab: le musée | museum | mew-ZAY
example: Nous visitons le musée. | We visit the museum.
vocab: visiter | to visit | vee-zee-TAY
example: Je visite le château. | I visit the castle.
vocab: voyager | to travel | vwah-yah-ZHAY
example: Je voyage en train. | I travel by train.
vocab: réserver | to book | ray-zair-VAY

vocab: un touriste | tourist | too-REEST
example: Le touriste visite le musée. | The tourist visits the museum.

[grammar]
grammar: Vouloir + infinitive | Use vouloir (to want) followed by an infinitive to say what you want to do. The second verb stays in its infinitive form.
example: Je veux visiter le musée. | I want to visit the museum.
example: Nous voulons voyager en France. | We want to travel in France.

[quiz]
quiz: What does « la valise » mean? | suitcase | passport | photo | A | « La valise » is the French word for suitcase.
quiz: What does « réserver » mean? | to visit | to book | to travel | B | You can réserver a hotel or a ticket.
quiz: What does “visiter” mean? | to visit | guide | to book | A | visiter means “to visit”.
quiz: What does “la photo” mean? | to visit | photo | tourist | B | la photo means “photo”.
