title: Tiny French Conversations · Lesson 46 · A Delayed Train
episode: TC-046
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Bonjour, vous attendez le train ? | Hello, are you waiting for the train?
Marinette: Oui, mais il n'arrive pas. | Yes, but it isn't arriving.
Adrien: Vous savez pourquoi ? | Do you know why?
Marinette: Je ne sais pas. Et vous ? | I don't know. And you?
Adrien: Oui. Il est déjà tard. | Yes. It's already late.
Marinette: Peut-être une panne ? | Maybe a breakdown?
Adrien: C'est possible. Nous pouvons attendre ici. | It's possible. We can wait here.
Marinette: D'accord. Merci, c'est gentil. | All right. Thank you, that's kind.
Adrien: Avec plaisir. Il arrive bientôt ? | You're welcome. Is it arriving soon?

[grammar]
grammar: Making a negative sentence | In French, put ne before the verb and pas after it to make a sentence negative. In everyday speech, ne is often dropped, but it is used in these examples.
example: Il n'arrive pas. | It isn't arriving.
example: Je ne sais pas. | I don't know.
