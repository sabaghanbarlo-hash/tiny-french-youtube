title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 29 · Hobbies and free time
episode: T1-029
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Salut Marinette, qu’est-ce que tu aimes faire pendant ton temps libre ? | Hi Marinette, what do you like to do in your free time?
Marinette: J’aime lire et écouter de la musique. | I like to read and listen to music.
Adrien: Moi, j’aime jouer au tennis et regarder des films. | I like to play tennis and watch movies.
Marinette: Tu aimes aussi danser ? | Do you like to dance too?
Adrien: Oui, un peu. Et toi, tu aimes dessiner ? | Yes, a little. And you, do you like to draw?
Marinette: Oui ! J’aime dessiner, me promener au parc et bricoler. | Yes! I like to draw, go for a walk in the park, and do DIY projects.
Adrien: Super ! On peut se promener, puis jouer au parc. | Great! We can go for a walk, then play in the park.
Marinette: Avec plaisir ! J’aime le sport aussi. | With pleasure! I like sports too.

[vocab]
vocab: jouer | to play | ZHWAY
example: Je joue au tennis avec Paul. | I play tennis with Paul.
vocab: lire | to read | LEER
example: Elle lit un livre le soir. | She reads a book in the evening.
vocab: écouter | to listen to | ay-koo-TAY
example: Nous écoutons de la musique. | We listen to music.
vocab: regarder | to watch | ruh-gar-DAY
example: Il regarde un film à la maison. | He watches a movie at home.
vocab: danser | to dance | dahn-SAY
example: Elles dansent le samedi soir. | They dance on Saturday evenings.
vocab: dessiner | to draw | day-see-NAY
example: Tu dessines un chat. | You draw a cat.
vocab: se promener | to go for a walk | suh prohm-NAY
example: Je me promène dans le parc. | I go for a walk in the park.
vocab: le sport | sports | luh SPOR
example: Le sport est amusant. | Sports are fun.
vocab: la musique | music | lah mee-ZEEK
example: J’aime la musique. | I like music.
vocab: bricoler | to do DIY | bree-koh-LAY
example: Bricoler me détend. | Doing DIY relaxes me.

[grammar]
grammar: Aimer + an infinitive | To say you like doing an action, use a form of aimer followed by the infinitive. The infinitive stays unchanged after aimer.
example: J’aime lire. | I like to read.
example: Tu aimes dessiner ? | Do you like to draw?
