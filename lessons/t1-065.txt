title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 65 · Nature and weather 2
episode: T1-065
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Bonjour, Marinette. Tu as regardé la météo ? | Hi, Marinette. Did you check the weather?
Marinette: Oui. Le ciel est gris et il va pleuvoir. | Yes. The sky is gray, and it’s going to rain.
Adrien: Il y a un grand nuage. Tu vois de la neige ? | There’s a big cloud. Do you see any snow?
Marinette: Oui, un peu. J’espère qu’il n’y aura pas d’orage. | Yes, a little. I hope there won’t be a thunderstorm.
Adrien: La rivière est belle. Le lac est près d’ici. | The river is beautiful. The lake is nearby.
Marinette: Oui, allons-y ! Regarde cette fleur dans l’herbe. | Yes, let’s go! Look at that flower in the grass.
Adrien: Elle est jolie. La neige fond déjà. | It’s pretty. The snow is already melting.
Marinette: Rentrons avant l’orage, d’accord ? | Let’s go home before the thunderstorm, okay?

[vocab]
vocab: le ciel | sky | luh see-EL
example: Le ciel est clair. | The sky is clear.
vocab: la météo | weather | lah may-tay-OH
example: La météo change souvent. | The weather changes often.
vocab: la neige | snow | lah NEZH
example: La neige tombe doucement. | The snow falls gently.
vocab: un nuage | cloud | uhn nwee-AHZH
example: Un nuage cache le ciel. | A cloud hides the sky.
vocab: l'orage | thunderstorm | loh-RAHZH
example: L'orage approche. | The storm is approaching.
vocab: la rivière | river | lah ree-VYEHR
example: La rivière traverse le village. | The river flows through the village.
vocab: un lac | lake | uhn LAK
example: Le lac est calme. | The lake is calm.
vocab: une fleur | flower | ewn FLUHR
example: Une fleur pousse ici. | A flower grows here.
vocab: l'herbe | grass | LEHRB
example: L'herbe pousse vite. | The grass grows quickly.
vocab: pleuvoir | to rain | pluh-VWAHR
example: Il pleut dehors. | It's raining outside.

[grammar]
grammar: The near future | To say that something is going to happen, use a form of aller followed by an infinitive. In the dialogue, va is the form of aller used with il, and pleuvoir is the infinitive.
example: Il va pleuvoir. | It is going to rain.
example: Nous allons regarder le lac. | We are going to look at the lake.
