title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 20 · Common adjectives
episode: T1-020
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Ce café est facile à trouver, non ? | This café is easy to find, isn't it?
Marinette: Oui, et le menu est simple. | Yes, and the menu is simple.
Adrien: C'est l'ancien café de mon père. | It's my father's former café.
Marinette: La soupe est fraîche et l'eau est fraîche. | The soup is fresh, and the water is cool.
Adrien: La rue est large, mais le café est bruyant. | The street is wide, but the café is noisy.
Marinette: Prenons une table dans un coin sombre. | Let's take a table in a dark corner.
Adrien: Bonne idée. Cette carte est pratique. | Good idea. This map is convenient.
Marinette: C'est normal : le plan du quartier est compliqué. | That's normal; the neighborhood map is complicated.

[vocab]
vocab: facile | easy | fah-SEEL
example: Ce livre est facile. | This book is easy.
vocab: simple | simple | SAHMP(L)
example: C'est un problème simple. | It's a simple problem.
vocab: ancien | former; old | ahn-SYEHN
example: Mon grand-père vit dans un ancien hôtel. | My grandfather lives in a former hotel.
vocab: frais | fresh; cool | FREH
example: Le pain est frais ce matin. | The bread is fresh this morning.
vocab: sombre | dark; gloomy | SOHMBR
example: Le ciel est sombre aujourd'hui. | The sky is dark today.
vocab: large | wide | LARZH
example: Ce chemin est large. | This path is wide.
vocab: bruyant | noisy | brwee-YAHN
example: Le quartier est bruyant la nuit. | The neighborhood is noisy at night.
vocab: pratique | practical; convenient | prah-TEEK
example: Ce livre est pratique pour voyager. | This book is practical for traveling.
vocab: normal | normal | nor-MAHL
example: C'est normal, ne t'inquiète pas. | That's normal; don't worry.
vocab: compliqué | complicated | kohm-plee-KAY
example: Ce travail est compliqué. | This work is complicated.

[grammar]
grammar: Adjective agreement | In French, an adjective usually agrees with the noun it describes in gender and number. For example, the feminine form of frais is fraîche.
example: L'eau est fraîche. | The water is cool.
example: Le café est bruyant. | The café is noisy.
