title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 98 · Adverbs and conjunctions 3
episode: T1-098
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: D'abord, on achète du pain, puis on va au parc. | First, we'll buy some bread, then we'll go to the park.
Marinette: D'accord. Pourtant, il pleut un peu. | Okay. However, it's raining a little.
Adrien: Puisque tu as un parapluie, on peut marcher. | Since you have an umbrella, we can walk.
Marinette: Je ne veux ni courir ni prendre le bus. | I want neither to run nor to take the bus.
Adrien: Lorsque la pluie s'arrêtera, on s'assiéra ici. | When the rain stops, we'll sit here.
Marinette: Tu liras, tandis que je dessinerai ? | You'll read while I draw?
Adrien: Oui, je veux simplement me reposer. | Yes, I simply want to rest.
Marinette: Justement, le soleil revient ; cependant, gardons le parapluie. | As it happens, the sun is coming back; however, let's keep the umbrella.

[vocab]
vocab: d'abord | first | dah-BOR
example: D'abord, je prends un café. | First, I have a coffee.
vocab: puis | then | PWEE
example: Je mange, puis je pars. | I eat, then I leave.
vocab: pourtant | however | poor-TAHN
example: Il pleut, pourtant je sors. | It’s raining, yet I’m going out.
vocab: puisque | since | PWEES-kuh
example: Puisqu'il est tard, on rentre. | Since it’s late, we’re going home.
vocab: ni | neither / nor | NEE
example: Je ne prends ni thé ni café. | I’m having neither tea nor coffee.
vocab: lorsque | when | lor-SKUH
example: Lorsque tu arrives, appelle-moi. | When you arrive, call me.
vocab: tandis que | while; whereas | tahn-DEE kuh
example: Il lit tandis qu'elle cuisine. | He reads while she cooks.
vocab: simplement | simply | sahm-pluh-MAHN
example: Elle explique simplement la règle. | She explains the rule simply.
vocab: justement | exactly; as it happens | zhoost-MAHN
example: C'est justement ce que je pense. | That's exactly what I think.
vocab: cependant | however | suh-pahn-DAHN
example: Il pleut ; cependant, nous sortons. | It's raining; however, we're going out.

[grammar]
grammar: Neither...nor with ni | To say “neither...nor” in French, use ne before the verb and ni before each option. In everyday speech, ne is often dropped, but it is included in the example here.
example: Je ne veux ni courir ni prendre le bus. | I want neither to run nor to take the bus.
example: Elle ne mange ni viande ni poisson. | She eats neither meat nor fish.

[quiz]
quiz: What does pourtant mean? | however | first | simply | A | Pourtant introduces a contrast and means “however.”
quiz: Which phrase means “neither to run nor to take the bus”? | ni courir ni prendre le bus | puis courir puis prendre le bus | lorsque courir et prendre le bus | A | Ni...ni connects two things that are both refused.
quiz: What does “lorsque” mean? | when | neither | however | A | lorsque means “when”.
quiz: What does “d'abord” mean? | when | however | first | C | d'abord means “first”.
quiz: What does “tant pis” mean? | of course | too bad | on the contrary | B | tant pis means “too bad”.
