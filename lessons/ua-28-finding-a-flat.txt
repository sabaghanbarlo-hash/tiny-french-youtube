title: Finding a Flat
episode: UA28
scene: town

[story]
Adrien: Bonjour, je cherche un appartement avec deux chambres. | Hello, I'm looking for an apartment with two bedrooms.
Marinette: Voici une annonce : un trois-pièces de quarante mètres carrés, loyer six cents euros par mois. | Here's a listing: a three-room flat of forty square metres, rent six hundred euros a month.
Adrien: Les charges sont comprises ? | Are the charges included?
Marinette: Oui, charges comprises. L'appartement est à dix minutes du métro. | Yes, charges included. The flat is ten minutes from the metro.
Adrien: Je peux le visiter demain ? | Can I visit it tomorrow?
Marinette: Bien sûr, à quatorze heures. | Of course, at two p.m.

[grammar]
grammar: Rent and numbers | Say rents with the hundreds you learned: six cents euros, huit cents euros, mille euros. Remember that cent takes an s only when it ends the number: six cents, but six cent cinquante. Add par mois to say per month.
example: Six cents euros par mois. | Six hundred euros per month.
example: Huit cent cinquante euros. | Eight hundred and fifty euros.
grammar: Reading a small ad | Ads use short forms: T3 or trois-pièces, 40 m², meublé, CC, proche métro. Ask Je peux visiter ? to arrange a viewing and quand ? for the time.
example: Un T3 de 40 m². | A three-room flat of 40 m².
example: Charges comprises. | Charges included.
