title: Sizes and Prices
episode: UA19
scene: store

[story]
Adrien: Bonjour, vous avez ce pull en taille M ? | Hello, do you have this jumper in size M?
Marinette: Oui, il coûte quarante-neuf euros. | Yes, it costs forty-nine euros.
Adrien: C'est un peu cher. Vous l'avez en promotion ? | That's a bit expensive. Is it on sale?
Marinette: Oui, moins vingt pour cent. Il fait trente-neuf euros vingt. | Yes, twenty percent off. It comes to 39.20.
Adrien: Parfait, je le prends. Je paie par carte. | Perfect, I'll take it. I'll pay by card.
Marinette: Très bien. Voici votre ticket. | Very good. Here is your receipt.

[grammar]
grammar: Hundreds and thousands | Cent takes an s when it is multiplied and ends the number: deux cents, trois cents. It loses the s when another number follows: deux cent cinquante. Mille never takes an s: deux mille. Say prices with euros and centimes: trente-neuf euros vingt.
example: Deux cents euros. | Two hundred euros.
example: Deux cent cinquante euros. | Two hundred and fifty euros.
grammar: Le, la, les as an object | To avoid repeating the noun, replace it with le, la or les placed before the verb: Je prends ce pull becomes Je le prends. Vous avez la veste ? Oui, nous l'avons. Il is for the subject; le, la, les is for the thing you take.
example: Je le prends. | I'll take it.
example: Nous les cherchons. | We're looking for them.
