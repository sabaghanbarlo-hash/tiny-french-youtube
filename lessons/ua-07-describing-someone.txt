[story]
Adrien: Comment est ton amie Léa ? | What is your friend Léa like?
Marinette: Elle est grande et brune, et elle a les yeux verts. Elle est très sympa. | She is tall and dark-haired, and she has green eyes. She is very nice.
Adrien: Et son frère ? | And her brother?
Marinette: Il est petit, blond et un peu timide. | He's short, blond and a bit shy.
Adrien: Ils sont drôles ? | Are they funny?
Marinette: Oui, ils sont drôles et gentils. | Yes, they're funny and kind.

[grammar]
grammar: Adjectives agree with the noun | Add -e for the feminine and -s for the plural. If the adjective already ends in -e, nothing changes for the feminine: il est drôle, elle est drôle. In the feminine you hear the final consonant: grand sounds like "grahn", grande like "grahnd".
example: Il est grand. | He is tall.
example: Elles sont grandes. | They are tall.
grammar: Where does the adjective go? | Most adjectives come after the noun: un ami sympa, une fille brune. A few short, common ones come before: un petit chat, une grande maison. Describe someone with être or with avoir for features.
example: Elle a les yeux verts. | She has green eyes.
example: C'est un garçon gentil. | He is a kind boy.
