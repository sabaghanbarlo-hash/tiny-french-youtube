title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 89 · Adverbs 2
episode: T1-089
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Bonjour, Marinette ! Tu vas bien ? | Hi, Marinette! Are you doing well?
Marinette: Oui, merci. Je travaille dehors aujourd’hui. | Yes, thanks. I’m working outside today.
Adrien: D’abord, on range les livres, puis on nettoie à l’intérieur. | First, we put away the books, then we clean inside.
Marinette: D’accord. Je peux le faire vite et bien. | Okay. I can do it quickly and well.
Adrien: Ensuite, regarde partout : le crayon est juste sous la table. | Then, look everywhere: the pencil is just under the table.
Marinette: Je le vois ! Je chante mal, mais je dessine rapidement. | I see it! I sing badly, but I draw quickly.
Adrien: Merci ! Tu peux m’aider, s’il te plaît ? | Thanks! Can you help me, please?
Marinette: Bien sûr, allons-y ensemble. | Of course, let’s go together.

[vocab]
vocab: bien | well | BYEHN
example: Je vais bien aujourd’hui. | I’m doing well today.
vocab: mal | badly | MAHL
example: Il dort mal cette nuit. | He is sleeping badly tonight.
vocab: vite | quickly | VEET
example: Nous marchons vite. | We walk quickly.
vocab: juste | just | ZHYST
example: Je veux juste un café. | I just want a coffee.
vocab: partout | everywhere | par-TOO
example: Il y a des fleurs partout. | There are flowers everywhere.
vocab: dehors | outside | duh-OR
example: Les enfants jouent dehors. | The children are playing outside.
vocab: dedans | inside | duh-DAHN
example: Le chat reste dedans. | The cat stays inside.
vocab: d’abord | first | dah-BOR
example: D’abord, je prends un café. | First, I have a coffee.
vocab: ensuite | then | ahn-SWEET
example: Ensuite, nous allons au marché. | Then, we go to the market.
vocab: rapidement | quickly | rah-peed-MAHN
example: Il parle rapidement. | He speaks quickly.

[grammar]
grammar: Adverbs after verbs | Many French adverbs that describe how an action happens come after the verb. This is true for short adverbs like bien and vite, and longer ones like rapidement.
example: Je travaille bien. | I work well.
example: Je dessine rapidement. | I draw quickly.
