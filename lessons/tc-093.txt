title: Tiny French Conversations · Lesson 93 · Life in My Hometown
episode: TC-093
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Bonsoir, vous venez d'où ? | Good evening, where are you from?
Marinette: Je viens d'une grande ville. Et vous ? | I come from a big city. And you?
Adrien: Je viens d'un petit village, près de la montagne. | I come from a small village near the mountain.
Marinette: La ville est bruyante, mais le centre est très beau. | The city is noisy, but the center is very beautiful.
Adrien: Le village est calme, avec un grand parc. | The village is quiet, with a big park.
Marinette: Un grand parc est aussi dans la ville. | A big park is also in the city.
Adrien: Vous aimez la ville ? | Do you like the city?
Marinette: Oui, beaucoup. Et vous ? | Yes, very much. And you?
Adrien: Oui, j'aime le village. Merci ! | Yes, I like the village. Thank you!

[grammar]
grammar: Venir de: saying where you're from | Use venir de followed by a place to say where you come from. De becomes d’ before a vowel sound, as in d’une and d’un.
example: Je viens d'une grande ville. | I come from a big city.
example: Je viens d'un petit village. | I come from a small village.
