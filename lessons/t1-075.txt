title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 75 · Life events
episode: T1-075
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Ma sœur attend un bébé : la naissance est prévue en juin. | My sister is expecting a baby: the birth is planned for June.
Marinette: Félicitations ! Elle va accoucher à Paris ? | Congratulations! Is she going to give birth in Paris?
Adrien: Oui, et le bébé va naître à Paris. | Yes, and the baby is going to be born in Paris.
Marinette: Mon cousin va se marier samedi. Le mariage est à la mairie. | My cousin is getting married on Saturday. The wedding is at city hall.
Adrien: C'est super ! Mes grands-parents parlent de la retraite. | That's great! My grandparents are talking about retirement.
Marinette: Mes voisins vont divorcer. Leur divorce est difficile. | My neighbors are going to get divorced. Their divorce is difficult.
Adrien: Ils cherchent un appartement après leur déménagement. | They're looking for an apartment after their move.
Marinette: Je comprends. Quelle année : une grossesse, un mariage et la retraite ! | I understand. What a year: a pregnancy, a wedding, and retirement!

[vocab]
vocab: la naissance | birth | lah neh-SAHNSS
example: Sa naissance est une grande joie. | Her birth is a great joy.
vocab: le mariage | marriage; wedding | luh mah-ree-AHZH
example: Le mariage est samedi. | The wedding is Saturday.
vocab: le divorce | divorce | luh dee-VORSS
example: Le divorce est officiel. | The divorce is official.
vocab: la grossesse | pregnancy | lah groh-SESS
example: Sa grossesse se passe bien. | Her pregnancy is going well.
vocab: la retraite | retirement | lah ruh-TRET
example: Elle prend sa retraite demain. | She retires tomorrow.
vocab: un déménagement | a move | uhn day-may-nazh-MAHN
example: Le déménagement est lundi. | The move is Monday.
vocab: naître | to be born | NETR
example: Un bébé naît en juin. | A baby is born in June.
vocab: se marier | to get married | suh mah-ree-AY
example: Ils se marient en juin. | They are getting married in June.
vocab: divorcer | to get divorced | dee-vor-SAY
example: Ils divorcent après dix ans. | They are getting divorced after ten years.
vocab: accoucher | to give birth | ah-koo-SHAY
example: Elle accouche demain. | She is giving birth tomorrow.

[grammar]
grammar: Talking about the near future | Use a form of aller followed by an infinitive to talk about a future event, often one that is planned or expected soon. The verb aller changes to match the subject, while the next verb stays in its infinitive form.
example: Elle va accoucher en juin. | She is going to give birth in June.
example: Ils vont divorcer. | They are going to get divorced.
