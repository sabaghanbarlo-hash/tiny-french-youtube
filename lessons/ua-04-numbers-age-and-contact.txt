[grammar]
grammar: Age with avoir | In French you do not "are" an age, you "have" it. Say J'ai vingt ans, with avoir: j'ai, tu as, il / elle a. Never forget ans: J'ai vingt sounds unfinished.
example: J'ai vingt ans. | I'm twenty.
example: Elle a onze ans. | She is eleven.
grammar: Phone numbers in pairs | French phone numbers are read in groups of two digits. Say the zero first, then each pair as a number: 06 12 34 56 78 is zéro six, douze, trente-quatre, cinquante-six, soixante-dix-huit.
example: Zéro six, douze, trente-quatre… | 06 12 34…
grammar: Spelling and e-mail | Ask Ça s'écrit comment ? to have a word spelled. For an e-mail, say arobase for @ and point for the dot: nom arobase mail point com.
example: Ça s'écrit comment ? | How do you spell that?
example: Adrien arobase mail point com | adrien@mail.com
