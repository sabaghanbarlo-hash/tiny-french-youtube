title: Tiny French Conversations · Lesson 7 · Meeting the Host
episode: TC-007
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Bonjour, Marinette ! | Hello, Marinette!
Marinette: Bonjour, Adrien ! Bienvenue chez moi. | Hello, Adrien! Welcome to my home.
Adrien: Merci. Je peux entrer ? | Thank you. May I come in?
Marinette: Oui, bien sûr. Entrez, s'il vous plaît. | Yes, of course. Come in, please.
Adrien: Voici un petit cadeau pour vous. | Here is a small gift for you.
Marinette: Merci, c'est très gentil. | Thank you, that's very kind.
Adrien: La maison est très belle. | The house is very beautiful.
Marinette: Vous voulez entrer dans le salon ? | Would you like to come into the living room?
Adrien: Oui, volontiers. | Yes, gladly.

[grammar]
grammar: A conjugated verb plus an infinitive | In French, a conjugated verb can be followed directly by an infinitive, with no word for “to.” In the dialogue, pouvoir and vouloir are followed by entrer.
example: Je peux entrer ? | May I come in?
example: Vous voulez entrer dans le salon ? | Would you like to come into the living room?
