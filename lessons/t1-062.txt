title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 62 · Abstract nouns
episode: T1-062
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: La vie en France est belle, tu ne trouves pas ? | Life in France is beautiful, don't you think?
Marinette: Oui ! Je ressens de la joie, mais je suis un peu fatiguée aujourd'hui. | Yes! I feel joy, but I'm a little tired today.
Adrien: Tu as un problème ? Tu sembles inquiète. | Do you have a problem? You seem worried.
Marinette: Ma mémoire me joue des tours : j'ai oublié mon rendez-vous. Quelle honte ! | My memory is playing tricks on me: I forgot my appointment. How embarrassing!
Adrien: Ce n'est pas grave ; tu as de la chance, il est encore temps. | It's okay; you're in luck, there's still time.
Marinette: Oui, l'amour de mes amis me donne du courage. | Yes, the love of my friends gives me courage.
Adrien: La vérité, c'est que la liberté et la joie sont importantes. | The truth is that freedom and joy are important.
Marinette: C'est vrai. Allons-y ensemble ! | That's true. Let's go together!

[grammar]
grammar: Articles with nouns | French nouns are usually learned with an article, such as la or le. Before a vowel or a silent h, le or la is elided to l', as in l'amour.
example: La vie est belle. | Life is beautiful.
example: L'amour de mes amis me donne du courage. | The love of my friends gives me courage.
