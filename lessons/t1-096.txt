title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 96 · Verbs 10
episode: T1-096
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: On doit se dépêcher ; le café ferme bientôt. | We have to hurry; the café is closing soon.
Marinette: D’accord, mais arrête de courir ! | Okay, but stop running!
Adrien: Je vais me garer ici, puis on continue à pied. | I’ll park here, then we’ll continue on foot.
Marinette: Je vais prévenir Lucas que nous arrivons. | I’ll let Lucas know that we’re arriving.
Adrien: Le café sert du chocolat chaud ; on en prend à emporter ? | The café serves hot chocolate; shall we get some to go?
Marinette: Oui, puis on installe la couverture au parc. | Yes, then we’ll set up the blanket in the park.
Adrien: Après, on se repose un peu, puis je dois revenir chez moi. | Afterward, we’ll rest a little, then I have to go back home.
Marinette: D’accord, je t’accompagne. | Okay, I’ll come with you.

[vocab]
vocab: arrêter | to stop | ah-reh-TAY
example: J’arrête la musique. | I’m stopping the music.
vocab: continuer | to continue | kohn-tee-new-AY
example: Nous continuons après le déjeuner. | We continue after lunch.
vocab: se dépêcher | to hurry | suh day-pay-SHAY
example: Dépêche-toi, nous sommes en retard. | Hurry up, we're late.
vocab: se reposer | to rest | suh ruh-poh-ZAY
example: Je me repose après le travail. | I rest after work.
vocab: se garer | to park | suh gah-RAY
example: Je me gare devant la maison. | I park in front of the house.
vocab: servir | to serve | sehr-VEER
example: Ce restaurant sert du poisson. | This restaurant serves fish.
vocab: installer | to install; to set up | an-stah-LAY
example: Installe l’application sur ton téléphone. | Install the app on your phone.
vocab: emporter | to take along | ahn-por-TAY
example: J’emporte un sandwich pour midi. | I'm taking a sandwich for lunch.
vocab: prévenir | to warn; to let know | pray-vuh-NEER
example: Préviens-moi quand tu arrives. | Let me know when you arrive.
vocab: revenir | to come back; to return | ruh-vuh-NEER
example: Je vais revenir demain. | I’m coming back tomorrow.

[grammar]
grammar: Reflexive verbs | Some French verbs use a reflexive pronoun, such as se dépêcher (to hurry) and se reposer (to rest). The pronoun changes to match the subject: for example, je me dépêche, but on se dépêche.
example: Je vais me garer ici. | I’m going to park here.
example: On se repose un peu. | We’re resting a little.

[quiz]
quiz: What does se garer mean? | to park | to hurry | to serve | A | Se garer means to park a vehicle.
quiz: Which verb means “to take along”? | revenir | emporter | arrêter | B | Emporter means to take something along or away.
quiz: What does “servir” mean? | to install | to continue | to serve | C | servir means “to serve”.
