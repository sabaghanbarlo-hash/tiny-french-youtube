title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 40 · Verbs of change and state
episode: T1-040
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Salut, Marinette ! Tu as changé de coiffure ? | Hi, Marinette! Have you changed your hairstyle?
Marinette: Oui, et je vais devenir styliste. Je me sens calme aujourd’hui. | Yes, and I’m going to become a stylist. I feel calm today.
Adrien: Tu sembles heureuse. Tu as grandi depuis l’an dernier ! | You seem happy. You’ve grown since last year!
Marinette: Merci ! Mon grand-père vieillit, mais il reste très actif. | Thanks! My grandfather is getting older, but he stays very active.
Adrien: Oh, il est malade ? | Oh, is he sick?
Marinette: Non, il va bien. Il est tombé hier, mais rien de grave. | No, he’s fine. He fell yesterday, but nothing serious.
Adrien: Tant mieux. On change de sujet et on va au café ? | That’s good. Shall we change the subject and go to the café?
Marinette: Avec plaisir ! | With pleasure!

[vocab]
vocab: changer | to change | shahn-ZHAY
example: Le ciel change vite. | The sky changes quickly.
vocab: devenir | to become | duh-vuh-NEER
example: Paul devient calme. | Paul becomes calm.
vocab: rester | to stay; remain | res-TAY
example: Le bébé reste calme. | The baby stays calm.
vocab: tomber | to fall | tom-BAY
example: La nuit tombe tôt. | Night falls early.
vocab: se sentir | to feel | suh sahn-TEER
example: Lina se sent bien. | Lina feels good.
vocab: grandir | to grow up | grahn-DEER
example: Le bébé grandit vite. | The baby grows quickly.
vocab: vieillir | to grow old; age | vyeh-YEER
example: Paul vieillit bien. | Paul is aging well.
vocab: malade | sick; ill | mah-LAHD
example: Marc est malade. | Marc is sick.
vocab: calme | calm | KAHLM
example: La classe est calme. | The class is calm.
vocab: sembler | to seem | sahn-BLAY
example: Il semble fatigué. | He seems tired.

[grammar]
grammar: The near future | Use a form of aller followed by an infinitive to talk about something that will happen soon. In English, this often means “going to” do something.
example: Je vais devenir styliste. | I’m going to become a stylist.
example: On va aller au café ? | Shall we go to the café?

[quiz]
quiz: What does devenir mean? | to remain | to become | to fall | B | Devenir means “to become.”
quiz: Which French word means “sick” or “ill”? | malade | calme | sembler | A | Malade means “sick” or “ill.”
quiz: What does “malade” mean? | to seem | to grow old | sick | C | malade means “sick”.
quiz: What does “calme” mean? | to feel | sick | calm | C | calme means “calm”.
quiz: What does “du coup” mean? | in short | so | there | B | du coup means “so”.
