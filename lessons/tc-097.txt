title: Tiny French Conversations · Lesson 97 · Best Holiday Memories
episode: TC-097
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Bonjour, Marinette. Tu as passé de bonnes vacances ? | Hello, Marinette. Did you have a good holiday?
Marinette: Oui, merci. Je suis allée à la mer avec ma famille. | Yes, thanks. I went to the seaside with my family.
Adrien: C'était où ? | Where was it?
Marinette: Dans un petit village, près de la plage. | In a small village, near the beach.
Adrien: Tu as nagé ? | Did you swim?
Marinette: Oui, et j'ai vu le soleil. | Yes, and I saw the sun.
Adrien: C'est bien ! Moi, j'ai visité la ville avec mon frère. | That's nice! I visited the city with my brother.
Marinette: C'était pendant les vacances ? | Was that during the holidays?
Adrien: Oui, et nous avons beaucoup marché. | Yes, and we walked a lot.

[grammar]
grammar: Talking about past actions | Use the passé composé with avoir to talk about a completed action in the past. Put the present-tense form of avoir before the past participle: tu as passé, tu as nagé.
example: Tu as passé de bonnes vacances ? | Did you have a good holiday?
example: Tu as nagé ? | Did you swim?
