title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 3 · Questions and Politeness
episode: T1-003
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Bonjour, Marinette ! Où est la boulangerie ? | Hello, Marinette! Where is the bakery?
Marinette: Elle est près de l'école. | It's near the school.
Adrien: Merci. Quand ouvre-t-elle ? | Thanks. When does it open?
Marinette: À neuf heures. Pourquoi ? | At nine o'clock. Why?
Adrien: Je veux du pain. Que veux-tu ? | I want some bread. What do you want?
Marinette: Un croissant, s'il te plaît. | A croissant, please.
Adrien: Pardon, Marinette, qui arrive ? | Excuse me, Marinette, who's coming?
Marinette: C'est Alya ! Comment vas-tu ? | It's Alya! How are you?

[vocab]
vocab: qui | who | KEE
example: Qui est là ? | Who is there?
vocab: que | what | KUH
example: Que fais-tu ? | What are you doing?
vocab: où | where | OO
example: Où est la gare ? | Where is the station?
vocab: quand | when | KAHN
example: Tu pars quand ? | When are you leaving?
vocab: comment | how | koh-MAHN
example: Comment ça va ? | How are you?
vocab: pourquoi | why | poor-KWAH
example: Pourquoi tu pars ? | Why are you leaving?
vocab: bonjour | hello | bohn-ZHOOR
example: Bonjour, madame ! | Hello, madam!
vocab: merci | thank you | mehr-SEE
example: Merci pour votre aide. | Thank you for your help.
vocab: s'il vous plaît | please | seel voo PLEH
example: Un café, s'il vous plaît. | A coffee, please.
vocab: pardon | sorry; excuse me | par-DOHN
example: Pardon, où est la sortie ? | Excuse me, where is the exit?

[grammar]
grammar: Question words at the start | French question words usually come at the beginning of a question. They can be followed by an inverted verb and subject, as in “Quand ouvre-t-elle ?”, or by a subject and verb in casual speech, as in “Pourquoi tu demandes ?”.
example: Où est la boulangerie ? | Where is the bakery?
example: Comment tu vas ? | How are you?
