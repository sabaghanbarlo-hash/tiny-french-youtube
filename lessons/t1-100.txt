[story]
Adrien: Bonjour, Marinette ! Voici un truc pour notre soirée. | Hello, Marinette! Here’s a thing for our evening.
Marinette: Merci ! Tu as quelque chose à regarder à la télévision ? | Thanks! Do you have something to watch on TV?
Adrien: Oui, quelqu’un a laissé ce film ici, car il est très drôle. | Yes, someone left this movie here because it’s very funny.
Marinette: Super ! Il dure environ deux heures ? | Great! Is it about two hours long?
Adrien: Oui, et il y a un détail amusant parmi les scènes. | Yes, and among the scenes, there’s a funny detail.
Marinette: J’aime cette façon de raconter une histoire. | I like this way of telling a story.
Adrien: Tu veux du thé aussi ? | Would you like some tea too?
Marinette: Oui, s’il te plaît ! | Yes, please!

[vocab]
vocab: quelqu’un | someone | kel-KUN
example: Quelqu’un frappe à la porte. | Someone knocks at the door.
vocab: quelque chose | something | kel-kuh SHOZ
example: Je veux quelque chose de chaud. | I want something hot.
vocab: un truc | a thing; a bit of stuff | uhn TROOK
example: C'est un truc très utile. | It's a very useful thing.
vocab: la télévision | television; TV | lah tay-lay-vee-ZYOHN
example: La télévision est dans le salon. | The TV is in the living room.
vocab: un détail | a detail | uhn day-TIE
example: J'ai oublié un détail important. | I forgot an important detail.
vocab: une façon | a way; a manner | ewn fah-SOHN
example: C'est une façon simple de faire. | It's a simple way to do it.
vocab: car | because; for | KAR
example: Je reste, car il pleut. | I'm staying because it's raining.
vocab: voici | here is; here are | vwah-SEE
example: Voici votre café. | Here's your coffee.
vocab: parmi | among | par-MEE
example: Choisis parmi ces trois options. | Choose among these three options.
vocab: environ | about; approximately | ahn-vee-ROHN
example: Il arrive dans environ dix minutes. | He'll arrive in about ten minutes.

[grammar]
grammar: Giving a reason with car | Car means “because” or “for” when it introduces a reason. The clause after car keeps the usual French word order: subject, then verb.
example: Je reste ici, car il pleut. | I’m staying here because it’s raining.
example: Elle sourit, car le film est drôle. | She’s smiling because the movie is funny.

[quiz]
quiz: What does quelqu’un mean? | someone | something | a detail | A | Quelqu’un means “someone” or “somebody.”
quiz: Which word means “here is” or “here are”? | parmi | voici | car | B | Voici is used to present something: “here is” or “here are.”
quiz: What does “un truc” mean? | someone | something | a thing | C | un truc means “a thing”.
quiz: What does “voici” mean? | a detail | here is | television | B | voici means “here is”.
quiz: What does “le sol” mean? | wall | drawer | floor | C | le sol means “floor”.
