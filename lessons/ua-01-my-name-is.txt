[grammar]
grammar: Tu or vous? | Use tu with friends, family, children and people your age. Use vous with someone you do not know, someone older, or in a formal place like an office or a shop. Vous is also the plural: it means "you all". When in doubt, start with vous.
example: Comment tu t'appelles ? | What's your name? (to a friend)
example: Comment vous appelez-vous ? | What is your name? (to a stranger)
grammar: The verb s'appeler | To say your name, use a reflexive verb: je m'appelle, tu t'appelles, il / elle s'appelle. Notice the double l. You can also say simply Moi, c'est in a relaxed conversation.
example: Je m'appelle Marinette. | My name is Marinette.
example: Il s'appelle Adrien. | His name is Adrien.
grammar: Enchanté or enchantée? | Enchanté has an extra e when a woman says it, but the two forms sound exactly the same. A man writes enchanté, a woman writes enchantée.
example: Enchanté, Marinette ! | Nice to meet you, Marinette! (a man speaking)
example: Enchantée, Adrien ! | Nice to meet you, Adrien! (a woman speaking)
