title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 64 · Describing things
episode: T1-064
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Regarde ce sac : il est joli et léger. | Look at this bag: it's pretty and lightweight.
Marinette: Oui, mais celui-là est lourd. | Yes, but that one is heavy.
Adrien: Tu préfères le sac gris ou le sac marron ? | Do you prefer the gray bag or the brown bag?
Marinette: Le gris, s’il te plaît. Et cette écharpe rose est jolie. | The gray one, please. And this pink scarf is pretty.
Adrien: Oui. Le coussin est mou, mais le galet est dur et lisse. | Yes. The cushion is soft, but the pebble is hard and smooth.
Marinette: Et la serviette est sèche maintenant. | And the towel is dry now.
Adrien: Parfait ! Le sac gris est léger. | Perfect! The gray bag is lightweight.
Marinette: Oui, il est joli. Merci, Adrien. | Yes, it's pretty. Thank you, Adrien.

[grammar]
grammar: Adjective agreement | French adjectives usually agree in gender and number with the noun they describe. Some change their spelling, such as sec becoming sèche with a feminine noun.
example: Le sac est léger. | The bag is lightweight.
