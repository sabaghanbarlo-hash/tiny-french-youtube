title: Tiny French Conversations · Lesson 94 · A Film You Loved
episode: TC-094
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Bonjour, Marinette. Tu as vu le film hier ? | Hello, Marinette. Did you see the film yesterday?
Marinette: Oui, je l’ai vu avec une copine. | Yes, I saw it with a friend.
Adrien: Tu as aimé le film ? | Did you like the film?
Marinette: Oui, beaucoup. Le film est vraiment drôle. | Yes, a lot. The film is really funny.
Adrien: Moi aussi. L’acteur est très bon. | Me too. The actor is very good.
Marinette: Et la musique ? | And the music?
Adrien: Elle est belle, et le film est très bon. | It’s beautiful, and the film is very good.
Marinette: D’accord. Tu veux revoir le film avec moi ? | Okay. Do you want to watch the film again with me?
Adrien: Oui, avec plaisir. | Yes, gladly.

[grammar]
grammar: Talking about the past with avoir | Use the present tense of avoir plus a past participle to say what someone did. In the dialogue, tu as vu means “you saw,” and tu as aimé means “you liked.”
example: Tu as vu le film hier ? | Did you see the film yesterday?
example: Je l’ai vu avec une copine. | I saw it with a friend.
