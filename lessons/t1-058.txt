title: Tiny French 1000 · Lesson 58 · Verbs: meet, start, finish, forget, remember, lose, find...
episode: T1-058
scene: classroom

[story]
Adrien: Salut, Marinette ! Tu te souviens de notre rendez-vous ? | Hi, Marinette! Do you remember our appointment?
Marinette: Oui, mais j'ai oublié l'heure. Tu peux me la rappeler ? | Yes, but I forgot the time. Can you remind me?
Adrien: On débute à dix heures, puis on cherche Léa près de la place. | We start at ten, then we look for Léa near the square.
Marinette: D'accord. Je vais la saluer et te la présenter. | Okay. I'll greet her and introduce her to you.
Adrien: Super ! Après, tu veux rejoindre notre groupe ? | Great! Afterward, do you want to join our group?
Marinette: Oui, et je serai contente de revoir tout le monde. | Yes, and I'll be happy to see everyone again.
Adrien: À la fin, on terminera le projet et on aura fini avant midi. | At the end, we'll finish the project and be done before noon.
Marinette: Parfait, à tout à l'heure ! | Perfect, see you later!

[vocab]
vocab: finir | to finish | fee-NEER
example: Nous finissons le repas ensemble. | We finish the meal together.
vocab: oublier | to forget | oo-blee-AY
example: J'oublie parfois mon parapluie. | I sometimes forget my umbrella.
vocab: se souvenir | to remember | suh soo-vuh-NEER
example: Je me souviens de son prénom. | I remember his first name.
vocab: chercher | to look for | share-SHAY
example: Je cherche mes lunettes partout. | I’m looking for my glasses everywhere.
vocab: revoir | to see again | ruh-VWAHR
example: On se revoit demain. | We'll see each other again tomorrow.
vocab: saluer | to greet | sah-lyoo-AY
example: Je salue mes voisins. | I greet my neighbors.
vocab: présenter | to introduce | pray-zahn-TAY
example: Il présente son ami. | He introduces his friend.
vocab: rejoindre | to join; to meet up with | ruh-JWAHN-dr
example: Je vous rejoins au café. | I'll meet you at the café.
vocab: débuter | to begin | day-byoo-TAY
example: Le film débute à huit heures. | The movie begins at eight.
vocab: terminer | to finish | tehr-mee-NAY
example: Nous terminons le travail. | We finish the work.

[grammar]
grammar: Remembering with se souvenir | The verb se souvenir is reflexive, so it changes with the subject: tu te souviens, je me souviens. To say what you remember, use de before a noun or an infinitive.
example: Tu te souviens de notre rendez-vous ? | Do you remember our appointment?
example: Je me souviens de Léa. | I remember Léa.

[quiz]
quiz: Which verb means “to see again”? | revoir | oublier | saluer | A | Revoir means to see someone or something again.
quiz: What does rejoindre mean in the dialogue? | to join | to forget | to finish | A | Rejoindre notre groupe means to join our group.
quiz: What does “terminer” mean? | to look for | to finish | to greet | B | terminer means “to finish”.
quiz: What does “chercher” mean? | to look for | to finish | to forget | A | chercher means “to look for”.
