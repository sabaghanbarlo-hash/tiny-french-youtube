VOCABULARY
ouvrir (oo-VREER) - to open
fermer (fair-MAY) - to close
mettre (METR) - to put
porter (por-TAY) - to carry; to wear
allumer (ah-lyoo-MAY) - to turn on
éteindre (ay-TANDR) - to turn off
poser (poh-ZAY) - to put down
enlever (ahn-luh-VAY) - to remove; to take off
amener (ahm-NAY) - to bring someone
emmener (ahn-mNAY) - to take someone along

GRAMMAR
Object pronouns before verbs - In French, a short object pronoun usually goes before the verb. Le or la becomes l’ before a vowel sound or a silent h, as in « je l’emmène » (I take him or her along).
