VOCABULARY
bien (BYEHN) - well
mal (MAHL) - badly
vite (VEET) - quickly
juste (ZHYST) - just
partout (par-TOO) - everywhere
dehors (duh-OR) - outside
dedans (duh-DAHN) - inside
d’abord (dah-BOR) - first
ensuite (ahn-SWEET) - then
rapidement (rah-peed-MAHN) - quickly

GRAMMAR
Adverbs after verbs - Many French adverbs that describe how an action happens come after the verb. This is true for short adverbs like bien and vite, and longer ones like rapidement.
