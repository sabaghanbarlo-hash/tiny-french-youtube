hier (YEHR) - yesterday

GRAMMAR
No capital letters - Days of the week are masculine and are written with a small letter in French: lundi, mardi, jeudi. The week starts on Monday on French calendars.
Samedi or le samedi? - A day on its own means this coming day: samedi, on va au cinéma. With le it means every week: le samedi, je dors.
Quel jour sommes-nous ? - Ask Quel jour sommes-nous ? or Quel jour est-ce ? and answer Nous sommes + day, or simply C'est + day. Hier c'était means "yesterday it was".
