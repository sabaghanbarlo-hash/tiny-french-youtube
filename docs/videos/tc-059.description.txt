VOCABULARY
Bienvenue chez nous. (byehn-vuh-NEW shay NOO) - Welcome to our home.
Tu veux un thé ou un café ? (tyoo vuh uh(n) TAY oo uh(n) kah-FAY) - Would you like tea or coffee?
Où sont les parents ? (OOH sohn lay pah-RAHN) - Where are the parents?
Je peux aider pour le repas ? (zhuh puh ay-DAY poor luh ruh-PAH) - Can I help with the meal?
Tu peux mettre la table ? (tyoo puh MEHTR lah TAHBL) - Can you set the table?

GRAMMAR
Pouvoir + infinitive - Use pouvoir followed by an infinitive to ask if someone can do something. Je peux means “I can,” and tu peux means “you can” when speaking to one person informally.
