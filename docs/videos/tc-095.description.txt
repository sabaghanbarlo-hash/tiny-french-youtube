VOCABULARY
Tu aimes la musique ? (tew em lah mew-ZEEK) - Do you like music?
Tu écoutes souvent la radio ? (tew ay-KOOT soo-VAHN lah rah-DYOH) - Do you often listen to the radio?
J’aime chanter. (zhem shahn-TAY) - I like to sing.
Tu veux aller au concert demain ? (tew vuh zah-LAY oh kohn-SAIR duh-MAN) - Do you want to go to the concert tomorrow?
Quand ? (KAHN) - When?

GRAMMAR
Aimer + an activity - Use aimer followed by an infinitive to say that you like doing something. The infinitive stays unchanged after aimer.
