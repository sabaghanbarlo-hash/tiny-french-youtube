VOCABULARY
Je voudrais payer, s'il vous plaît. (zhuh voo-DRAY pay-YAY, seel voo PLAY) - I would like to pay, please.
Vous payez avec la carte ? (voo pay-YAY ah-VEK lah KART) - Are you paying by card?
Le prix est de dix euros. (luh PREE ay duh DEEZ uh-ROH) - The price is ten euros.
Je peux payer ici ? (zhuh puh pay-YAY ee-SEE) - Can I pay here?
Au revoir ! (oh ruh-VWAHR) - Goodbye!

GRAMMAR
Pouvoir + infinitive - Use pouvoir followed by an infinitive to ask if you can do something. This pattern makes a polite question; the infinitive stays unchanged.
