VOCABULARY
Bonjour ! (bohn-ZHOOR) - Hello!
Bienvenue chez moi. (byehn-vuh-NEW shay MWA) - Welcome to my home.
Je peux entrer ? (zhuh puh ahn-TRAY) - May I come in?
Entrez, s'il vous plaît. (ahn-TRAY seel voo PLEH) - Come in, please.
Oui, volontiers. (wee voh-lohn-TYAY) - Yes, gladly.

GRAMMAR
A conjugated verb plus an infinitive - In French, a conjugated verb can be followed directly by an infinitive, with no word for “to.” In the dialogue, pouvoir and vouloir are followed by entrer.
