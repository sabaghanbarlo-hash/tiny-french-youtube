VOCABULARY
parler (par-LAY) - to speak
habiter (ah-bee-TAY) - to live (in a place)
travailler (trah-vah-YAY) - to work
aimer (eh-MAY) - to like, to love
adorer (ah-doh-RAY) - to love, to adore
écouter (ay-koo-TAY) - to listen to
manger (mahn-ZHAY) - to eat
danser (dahn-SAY) - to dance

GRAMMAR
The -er pattern - Take the infinitive (parler), remove -er to get the stem (parl-) and add: je parle, tu parles, il / elle parle, nous parlons, vous parlez, ils / elles parlent. Four of the six forms sound identical: parle, parles, parle, parlent are all “parl”.
Elision and small spelling changes - Je becomes j' before a vowel or silent h: j'habite, j'écoute, j'adore. Verbs in -ger keep the e in the nous form so the g stays soft: nous mangeons.
Aimer + noun or verb - With a noun use the article le / la / les to talk about things you like in general: J'aime le café. With another verb, use the infinitive: J'aime danser. Use adorer for “love” and détester for “hate”.
