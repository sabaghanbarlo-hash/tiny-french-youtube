VOCABULARY
Bonjour, Marinette ! (bohn-ZHOOR, mah-ree-NET) - Hello, Marinette!
Tu te promènes aussi ? (tew tuh proh-MEN oh-SEE) - Are you out for a walk too?
J'aime parler avec ma famille. (zhem par-LAY ah-VEK mah fah-MEE-yuh) - I like talking with my family.
Qu'est-ce qui te rend heureux ? (kess-kee tuh rahn uh-RUH) - What makes you happy?
Bonne journée ! (bun zhoor-NAY) - Have a nice day!

GRAMMAR
J'aime + infinitive - Use aimer followed by a verb in the infinitive to say that you like doing something. The infinitive stays the same, even when the subject changes.
