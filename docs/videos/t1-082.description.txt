VOCABULARY
le dos (luh DOH) - back
le ventre (luh VAHN-truh) - stomach; belly
la gorge (lah GORZH) - throat
le cœur (luh KUHR) - heart
la peau (lah POH) - skin
la dent (lah DAHN) - tooth
le doigt (luh DWAH) - finger
le genou (luh zhuh-NOO) - knee
respirer (rehs-pee-RAY) - to breathe
saigner (seh-NYAY) - to bleed

GRAMMAR
Saying what hurts - To say that a body part hurts, French usually uses avoir mal à, followed by the body part with its definite article. À + le contracts to au, as in au dos; à + la stays à la, as in à la gorge; à + l’ becomes à l’ before a vowel or a mute h; and à + les contracts to aux. When referring to a specific tooth, an indefinite article is also possible, as in avoir mal à une dent.
