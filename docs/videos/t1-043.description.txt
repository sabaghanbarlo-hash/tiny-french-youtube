VOCABULARY
l’ordinateur (or-dee-nah-TUHR) - computer
le téléphone (tay-lay-FON) - telephone; phone
un client (klee-YAHN) - client; customer
une réunion (ray-oo-nee-YON) - meeting
un rendez-vous (rahn-day-VOO) - appointment; meeting
signer (seen-YAY) - to sign
un dossier (doh-see-AY) - a file
un document (doh-koo-MAHN) - a document
un agenda (ah-zhen-DAH) - a planner
un compte (KOHNT) - an account

GRAMMAR
French articles and gender - French nouns are masculine or feminine, and the article changes to match. Use un with masculine nouns and une with feminine nouns for “a/an”; use le with masculine nouns and la with feminine nouns for “the.” Le and la become l’ before a vowel or a silent h.
