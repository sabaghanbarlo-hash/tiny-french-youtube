VOCABULARY
la moitié (la mwah-TYAY) - half
la plupart (lah plee-PAHR) - most
autant (oh-TAHN) - as much; as many
davantage (da-vahn-TAZH) - more
aucun (oh-KUN) - no; not any
rien (RYEHN) - nothing
chacun (shah-KUN) - each; each one
un kilo (uhn kee-LO) - a kilogram
un litre (uhn LEE-truh) - a liter
un morceau (uhn mor-SOH) - a piece

GRAMMAR
De after quantity words - Many quantity expressions are followed by de before a noun; use d’ before a vowel sound or a mute h. This pattern appears in units like « un kilo de » and « un litre de ».
