VOCABULARY
je voudrais… (zhuh voo-DRAY) - I would like…
vous désirez ? (voo day-zee-RAY) - what would you like? (polite)
un thé (uhn TAY) - a tea
un jus d'orange (uhn zhew doh-RAHNZH) - an orange juice
une limonade (ewn lee-moh-NAHD) - a lemonade
l'addition (lah-dee-SYOHN) - the bill
tout de suite (too duh SWEET) - right away
s'il vous plaît (seel voo PLEH) - please (polite)

GRAMMAR
Je voudrais = I would like - Je voudrais is the polite way to order. Je veux means "I want" and sounds too direct in a café. Add s'il vous plaît at the end.
Vous with a waiter - Tu is for friends and family. With waiters, shop staff and people you do not know, use vous and s'il vous plaît. Adrien says vous to the waiter, but tu to Marinette.
Un or une? - French nouns are masculine or feminine. Use un for masculine and une for feminine. Learn each new word with its un or une.
