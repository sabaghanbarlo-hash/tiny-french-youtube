VOCABULARY
la pharmacie (lah far-ma-SEE) - the pharmacy
le médicament (luh may-dee-ka-MAHN) - the medicine
la tête (lah TET) - the head
la gorge (lah GORZH) - the throat
se reposer (suh ruh-poh-ZAY) - to rest

GRAMMAR
J'ai mal à + body part - Use this to say something hurts
Il faut + infinitive - Use this to say what you must do
