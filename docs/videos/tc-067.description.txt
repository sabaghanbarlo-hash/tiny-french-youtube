VOCABULARY
Je viens d'arriver. (zhuh vyen dah-ree-VAY) - I've just arrived.
Il y a un embouteillage. (eel yah uhn ahm-boo-tay-YAHZH) - There's a traffic jam.
Vous avez un souci ? (voo-zah-VAY uhn soo-SEE) - Is something wrong?
Prévenez-moi, s'il vous plaît. (preh-vuh-NAY mwah seel voo PLEH) - Please let me know.
Je peux commencer maintenant. (zhuh puh koh-mahn-SAY mahn-teh-NAHN) - I can start now.

GRAMMAR
Polite questions with vous - Use vous with a verb to address someone politely. The same form can also address more than one person.
