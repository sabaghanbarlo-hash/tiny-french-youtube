VOCABULARY
Tu as passé de bonnes vacances ? (ty ah pah-SAY duh bun vah-KANS) - Did you have a good holiday?
Je suis allée à la mer. (zhuh swee zah-LAY ah lah MAIR) - I went to the seaside.
Tu as nagé ? (ty ah nah-ZHAY) - Did you swim?
J'ai vu le soleil. (zhay vy luh soh-LAY) - I saw the sun.
Nous avons beaucoup marché. (noo zah-VON boh-KOO mar-SHAY) - We walked a lot.

GRAMMAR
Talking about past actions - Use the passé composé with avoir to talk about a completed action in the past. Put the present-tense form of avoir before the past participle: tu as passé, tu as nagé.
