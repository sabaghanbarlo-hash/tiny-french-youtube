VOCABULARY
Bonjour, Marinette. (bohn-ZHOOR mah-ree-NET) - Hello, Marinette.
Pardon, vous entendez la musique ? (par-DOHN voo-zahn-tahn-DAY lah myoo-ZEEK) - Sorry, can you hear the music?
Je peux mettre la musique moins fort. (zhuh puh MEHTR lah myoo-ZEEK mwan FOR) - I can turn the music down.
Je dois me lever tôt demain. (zhuh dwah muh luh-VAY toh duh-MAN) - I have to get up early tomorrow.
Merci, c'est gentil. (mehr-SEE say zhahn-TEE) - Thank you, that's kind.

GRAMMAR
Aller + infinitive for the near future - Use aller followed by an infinitive to say what someone is going to do. The form changes with the subject: je vais means “I am going to.”
