VOCABULARY
Où est la chose ? (oo eh lah SHOHZ) - Where is the thing?
Je ne sais pas. (zhuh nuh say PAH) - I don’t know.
Regarde sur la table, s'il te plaît. (ruh-GARD sur lah TAH-bluh, seel tuh PLEH) - Look on the table, please.
Je la vois sous le coussin ! (zhuh lah vwah soo luh koo-SAN) - I see it under the cushion!
Tu peux allumer la télévision ? (tyuh puh ah-lyoo-MAY lah tay-lay-vih-ZYON) - Can you turn on the TV?

GRAMMAR
Saying “I don’t know” - To say that you do not do something, French often places ne before the verb and pas after it. In everyday speech, ne is sometimes dropped, but this dialogue uses the full form.
