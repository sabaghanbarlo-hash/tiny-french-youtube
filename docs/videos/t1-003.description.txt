VOCABULARY
qui (KEE) - who
que (KUH) - what
où (OO) - where
quand (KAHN) - when
comment (koh-MAHN) - how
pourquoi (poor-KWAH) - why
bonjour (bohn-ZHOOR) - hello
merci (mehr-SEE) - thank you
s'il vous plaît (seel voo PLEH) - please
pardon (par-DOHN) - sorry; excuse me

GRAMMAR
Question words at the start - French question words usually come at the beginning of a question. They can be followed by an inverted verb and subject, as in “Quand ouvre-t-elle ?”, or by a subject and verb in casual speech, as in “Pourquoi tu demandes ?”.
