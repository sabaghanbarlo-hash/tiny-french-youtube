VOCABULARY
conduire (kohn-DWEER) - to drive
traverser (trah-vehr-SAY) - to cross
freiner (freh-NAY) - to brake
ralentir (rah-lahn-TEER) - to slow down
le feu (luh FUH) - fire
le camion (luh kah-MYOHN) - truck
la moto (lah moh-TOH) - motorcycle
l'autoroute (loh-toh-ROOT) - highway
un embouteillage (uhn ahn-boo-tay-YAHZH) - traffic jam
le parking (luh par-KEENG) - parking lot

GRAMMAR
The near future - To say what someone is going to do, use the present tense of aller followed by an infinitive. In the dialogue, vais freiner means “am going to brake.”
