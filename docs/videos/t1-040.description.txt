VOCABULARY
changer (shahn-ZHAY) - to change
devenir (duh-vuh-NEER) - to become
rester (res-TAY) - to stay; remain
tomber (tom-BAY) - to fall
se sentir (suh sahn-TEER) - to feel
grandir (grahn-DEER) - to grow up
vieillir (vyeh-YEER) - to grow old; age
malade (mah-LAHD) - sick; ill
calme (KAHLM) - calm
sembler (sahn-BLAY) - to seem

GRAMMAR
The near future - Use a form of aller followed by an infinitive to talk about something that will happen soon. In English, this often means “going to” do something.
