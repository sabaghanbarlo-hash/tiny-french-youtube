VOCABULARY
Bonjour, Adrien. (bohn-ZHOOR ah-dree-EHN) - Hello, Adrien.
L'eau tombe du robinet. (loh tohmb duh roh-bee-NAY) - Water is dripping from the tap.
Pouvez-vous venir, s'il vous plaît ? (poo-VAY voo vuh-NEER, seel voo PLEH) - Could you come, please?
Je peux venir maintenant. (zhuh puh vuh-NEER man-tuh-NAHN) - I can come now.
Je vais le réparer tout de suite. (zhuh vay luh ray-pah-RAY too duh SWEET) - I’ll repair it right away.

GRAMMAR
Pouvoir + infinitive - Use a form of pouvoir (to be able to) followed by an infinitive to talk about what someone can do. In a question, this pattern can make a polite request.
