VOCABULARY
aider (ay-DAY) - to help
une urgence (oon oor-ZHAHNS) - an emergency
les secours (lay suh-KOOR) - emergency services
la police (lah poh-LEES) - the police
blessé (bleh-SAY) - injured
au secours (oh suh-KOOR) - help!
une ambulance (ewn ahn-bew-LAHNS) - an ambulance
un incendie (uhn an-sahn-DEE) - a fire
un pompier (uhn pohn-PYAY) - a firefighter
sauver (soh-VAY) - to rescue; to save

GRAMMAR
Il y a - Il y a means “there is” or “there are” and is followed by a noun. The phrase stays the same whether the noun is singular or plural.
