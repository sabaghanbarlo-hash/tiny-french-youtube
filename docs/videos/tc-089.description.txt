VOCABULARY
Il pleut beaucoup aujourd'hui. (eel pluh boh-koo oh-zhoor-DWEE) - It's raining a lot today.
Restons à la maison. (res-ton ah lah meh-ZOHN) - Let's stay at home.
Nous pouvons regarder un film. (noo poo-von ruh-gar-day uhn FEELM) - We can watch a film.
Bonne idée. (bun ee-DAY) - Good idea.
Je vais préparer le thé. (zhuh vay pray-pah-ray luh TAY) - I'm going to make the tea.

GRAMMAR
Pouvoir + infinitive - Use a conjugated form of pouvoir followed by an infinitive to say what someone can do. In the dialogue, pouvons means “can” with nous.
