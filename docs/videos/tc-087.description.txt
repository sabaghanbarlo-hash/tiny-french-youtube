VOCABULARY
Tu as un plan pour le week-end ? (tyu ah uhn plahn poor luh week-END) - Do you have a plan for the weekend?
Je vais à la mer samedi. (zhuh vay ah lah mehr sahm-DEE) - I’m going to the seaside on Saturday.
Tu pars avec un ami ? (tyu pahr ah-VEHK uhn-nah-MEE) - Are you leaving with a friend?
Tu te reposes ? (tyu tuh ruh-POHZ) - Are you resting?
Bon week-end ! (bohn week-END) - Have a good weekend!

GRAMMAR
Aller à a place - Use aller + à to say that you are going to a place. Before a masculine noun with le, à + le becomes au, as in au parc.
