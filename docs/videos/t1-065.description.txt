VOCABULARY
le ciel (luh see-EL) - sky
la météo (lah may-tay-OH) - weather
la neige (lah NEZH) - snow
un nuage (uhn nwee-AHZH) - cloud
l'orage (loh-RAHZH) - thunderstorm
la rivière (lah ree-VYEHR) - river
un lac (uhn LAK) - lake
une fleur (ewn FLUHR) - flower
l'herbe (LEHRB) - grass
pleuvoir (pluh-VWAHR) - to rain

GRAMMAR
The near future - To say that something is going to happen, use a form of aller followed by an infinitive. In the dialogue, va is the form of aller used with il, and pleuvoir is the infinitive.
