VOCABULARY
un souci (uhn soo-SEE) - a problem
une erreur (oon eh-RUHR) - a mistake
une panne (oon PAHN) - a breakdown
un accident (uhn ak-see-DAHN) - an accident
casser (kah-SAY) - to break
réparer (ray-pah-RAY) - to repair
se tromper (suh trohn-PAY) - to be mistaken
manquer (mahn-KAY) - to be missing
difficile (dee-fee-SEEL) - difficult
grave (GRAHV) - serious

GRAMMAR
C'est + adjective - Use c'est + an adjective to give a general description or evaluation, often of a situation or thing. To make it negative, put ne and pas around est: ce n'est pas grave means “it's not serious.”
