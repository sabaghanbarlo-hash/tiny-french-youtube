VOCABULARY
peu (PUH) - little; not much
assez (ah-SAY) - enough
trop (TROH) - too much
quelques (KELK) - a few; some
plusieurs (plew-ZYUR) - several
chaque (SHAHK) - each; every
tout (TOO) - all; whole
une bouteille (ewn boo-TAY) - a bottle
une boîte (ewn BWAT) - a box; a can
une tranche (ewn TRAHNSH) - a slice

GRAMMAR
Quantity words + de - Before a noun, peu, assez, and trop are followed by de (or d’ before a vowel). The noun usually has no article in this pattern.
