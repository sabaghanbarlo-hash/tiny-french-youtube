VOCABULARY
l'information (lan-for-ma-SYON) - information
un journal (zhoor-NAHL) - newspaper
un journaliste (zhoor-na-LEEST) - journalist
un article (ar-TEEKL) - article
la presse (la PRESS) - the press
une chaîne (ewn SHEN) - chain
un reportage (uhn ruh-por-TAZH) - news report
un titre (uhn TEETR) - title
informer (an-for-MAY) - to inform
annoncer (ah-non-SAY) - to announce

GRAMMAR
The passé composé - Use the passé composé to talk about completed actions in the past. For many verbs, form it with the present tense of avoir followed by the past participle.
