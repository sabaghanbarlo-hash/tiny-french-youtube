VOCABULARY
la France (lah FRAHNS) - France
le français (luh frahn-SEH) - French (language)
la culture (lah kuul-TUR) - culture
l'art (LAHR) - art
le monument (luh moh-new-MAHN) - monument
la tradition (lah trah-dee-SYOHN) - tradition
connaître (koh-NETR) - to know
découvrir (day-koo-VREER) - to discover
célèbre (seh-LEBR) - famous
un festival (fes-tee-VAHL) - festival

GRAMMAR
French noun articles - French nouns usually take an article such as le, la, or l’. The article agrees with the noun in gender and number; l’ is used before a vowel or a mute h.
