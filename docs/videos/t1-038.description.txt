VOCABULARY
salut (sah-LEW) - hi; bye
bonsoir (bohn-SWAHR) - good evening
au revoir (oh ruh-VWAHR) - goodbye
bienvenue (byehn-vuh-NOO) - welcome
allô (ah-LOH) - hello (on the phone)
enchanté (ahn-shahn-TAY) - pleased to meet you
s'excuser (sehk-kew-ZAY) - to apologize
une excuse (ewn ehk-SKEWZ) - an excuse
un monsieur (uhn muh-SYUH) - a gentleman; sir
une madame (ewn mah-DAHM) - a lady; ma’am

GRAMMAR
Enchanté : masculin et féminin - In French, enchanté agrees with the gender of the person speaking, not the person they meet. A male speaker says enchanté, while a female speaker adds -e and says enchantée.
