VOCABULARY
le bus (luh BYOOS) - bus
le train (luh TRAHN) - train
le métro (luh may-TROH) - subway
le vélo (luh vay-LOH) - bicycle
le taxi (luh tack-SEE) - taxi
l’avion (lah-VYOHN) - plane
la gare (lah GAHR) - train station
le billet (luh bee-YAY) - ticket
un bateau (uhn bah-TOH) - boat
un scooter (uhn skoo-TEHR) - scooter

GRAMMAR
French articles - For singular nouns beginning with a consonant sound, use le with masculine nouns and la with feminine nouns as the definite article.
