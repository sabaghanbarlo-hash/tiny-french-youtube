Stop Saying Just “Merci”! 3 Ways to Say Thank You in French | Learn French in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny French story and a quick quiz with Marinette and Adrien. Follow for a new lesson every day!

Adrien, tu dis toujours juste merci. - Adrien, you always say just thank you.
Et alors ? C'est mal ? - So what? Is that bad?
Non, mais essaie : merci beaucoup ! - No, but try: thank you very much!
Merci beaucoup, Marinette ! - Thank you very much, Marinette!
Bravo ! Pour être très poli : je vous remercie. - Bravo! To be very polite: I thank you.

WORDS
merci beaucoup (mehr-SEE boh-KOO) - thank you very much
je vous remercie (zhuh voo ruh-mehr-SEE) - I thank you (very polite)
