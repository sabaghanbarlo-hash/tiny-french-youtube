la récréation (ray-kreh-ah-SYOHN) - recess; break

GRAMMAR
Ton and ta - French possessive adjectives agree with the thing someone owns, not with the person who owns it. Use ton before a masculine singular noun and ta before a feminine singular noun. Before a feminine singular noun that begins with a vowel or a silent h, use ton to avoid an awkward vowel sound.
