VOCABULARY
Bonjour Marinette. (bohn-ZHOOR mah-ree-NET) - Hello, Marinette.
Tu veux me donner le numéro de téléphone ? (tyuh vuh muh doh-NAY luh noo-may-ROH duh tay-lay-FOHN) - Would you like to give me the phone number?
Le numéro (luh noo-may-ROH) - The number
Et après ? (ay ah-PRAY) - And then?
À bientôt ! (ah byehn-TOH) - See you soon!

GRAMMAR
Tu veux + infinitive - Use tu veux followed by an infinitive to ask someone if they want to do something. The verb after veux stays in its infinitive form.
