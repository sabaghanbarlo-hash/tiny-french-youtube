VOCABULARY
arrêter (ah-reh-TAY) - to stop
continuer (kohn-tee-new-AY) - to continue
se dépêcher (suh day-pay-SHAY) - to hurry
se reposer (suh ruh-poh-ZAY) - to rest
se garer (suh gah-RAY) - to park
servir (sehr-VEER) - to serve
installer (an-stah-LAY) - to install; to set up
emporter (ahn-por-TAY) - to take along
prévenir (pray-vuh-NEER) - to warn; to let know
revenir (ruh-vuh-NEER) - to come back; to return

GRAMMAR
Reflexive verbs - Some French verbs use a reflexive pronoun, such as se dépêcher (to hurry) and se reposer (to rest). The pronoun changes to match the subject: for example, je me dépêche, but on se dépêche.
