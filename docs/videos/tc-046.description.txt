VOCABULARY
Bonjour (bohn-ZHOOR) - Hello
Vous savez pourquoi ? (voo sah-VAY poor-KWAH) - Do you know why?
Je ne sais pas. (zhuh nuh say PAH) - I don't know.
Peut-être une panne ? (puh-TET oon PAHN) - Maybe a breakdown?
Avec plaisir. (ah-VEK play-ZEER) - You're welcome.

GRAMMAR
Making a negative sentence - In French, put ne before the verb and pas after it to make a sentence negative. In everyday speech, ne is often dropped, but it is used in these examples.
