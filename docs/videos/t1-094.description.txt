le silence (luh see-LAHNS) - silence

GRAMMAR
Articles with abstract nouns - French nouns have grammatical gender, so when talking about an abstract noun in general, they usually take the definite article le, la, or l’. Use l’ before a vowel or a mute h, as in l’habitude.
