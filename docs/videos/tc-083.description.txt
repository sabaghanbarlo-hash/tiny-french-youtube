VOCABULARY
Tu veux visiter le musée ? (tyuh vuh vee-zee-TAY luh myoo-ZAY) - Do you want to visit the museum?
Je préfère voir les photos. (zhuh pray-FAIR vwar lay foh-TOH) - I prefer to see the photos.
Je peux attendre. (zhuh puh ah-TONDR) - I can wait.
Nous pouvons voir les photos ensemble. (noo poo-VON vwar lay foh-TOH ahn-SAHMBL) - We can see the photos together.
Allons voir les photos d'abord. (ah-LOHN vwar lay foh-TOH dah-BOR) - Let's see the photos first.

GRAMMAR
Vouloir + infinitive - Use vouloir (to want) followed by an infinitive to say what someone wants to do. The infinitive stays the same after different forms of vouloir.
