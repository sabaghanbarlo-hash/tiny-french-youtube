VOCABULARY
Bonjour (bohn-ZHOOR) - Hello
Un billet pour la ville (uhn bee-YAY poor lah VEEL) - One ticket to the city
Combien coûte le billet ? (kohm-BYEN koot luh bee-YAY) - How much is the ticket?
Je peux payer par carte ? (zhuh puh pay-YAY par KART) - Can I pay by card?
Voici le billet. (vwah-SEE luh bee-YAY) - Here is the ticket.

GRAMMAR
Pouvoir + infinitive - Use pouvoir followed by an infinitive to ask or say whether someone can do something. Je peux means “I can,” and vous pouvez means “you can.”
