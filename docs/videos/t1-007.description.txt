VOCABULARY

même (MEM) - same
autre (OHTR) - other; another
mauvais (moh-VAY) - bad

GRAMMAR
Adjective agreement - French adjectives often change to agree with the gender of the noun they describe. Many add -e in the feminine, while vieux becomes vieille.
