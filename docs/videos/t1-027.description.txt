VOCABULARY
partir (par-TEER) - to leave
arriver (ah-ree-VAY) - to arrive
entrer (ahn-TRAY) - to enter
sortir (sor-TEER) - to go out
marcher (mar-SHAY) - to walk
rentrer (rahn-TRAY) - to return home
monter (mohn-TAY) - to go up
descendre (day-SAHN-druh) - to go down
avancer (ah-vahn-SAY) - to move forward
reculer (ruh-kew-LAY) - to move backward

GRAMMAR
Present tense of -er verbs - Many French verbs ending in -er use the present-tense ending -e with je and -es with tu. Remove -er from the infinitive, then add the ending: marcher becomes je marche and tu marches.
