VOCABULARY
comment tu t'appelles ? (koh-mahn tew tah-PEL) - what's your name? (to a friend)
moi, c'est… (mwah SEH) - me, I'm… / I'm…
je suis français / française (zhuh swee frahn-SEH / frahn-SEHZ) - I am French (man / woman)
j'habite à… (zhah-BEET ah) - I live in…
tu viens d'où ? (tew VYAN doo) - where are you from? (to a friend)
je viens de… (zhuh VYAN duh) - I come from…
je parle (zhuh PARL) - I speak
un peu (uhn PUH) - a little

GRAMMAR
Je suis + nationality - Nationalities are adjectives. For a woman add an -e. Français becomes française, canadien becomes canadienne. The masculine and feminine often sound different here, because the final consonant is pronounced in the feminine.
J'habite à… and je viens de… - Use à with a city to say where you live, and de with a city to say where you come from. With a masculine country such as le Canada, de + le becomes du.
Je parle + language - Use je parle with the name of a language, with no word for "the". Add un peu to be modest.
