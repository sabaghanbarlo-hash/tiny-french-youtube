VOCABULARY
la santé (lah sahn-TAY) - health
un médicament (uhn may-dee-kah-MAHN) - medicine
la fièvre (lah FYEVR) - fever
tousser (too-SAY) - to cough
guérir (geh-REER) - to recover
la douleur (lah doo-LUHR) - pain
éternuer (ay-tehr-NWEH) - to sneeze
vomir (voh-MEER) - to vomit
la toux (TOO) - cough
le rhume (ROOM) - common cold

GRAMMAR
Using avoir for illness - French often uses avoir (to have) to talk about being sick or having a symptom. The noun phrase takes the article required by the expression: un rhume, de la fièvre, or une toux.
