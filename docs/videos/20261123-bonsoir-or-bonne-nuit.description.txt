Bonsoir or Bonne Nuit? Don't Say Good Night Too Early in French | Learn French in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny French story and a quick quiz with Marinette and Adrien. Follow for a new lesson every day!

Il est huit heures. Bonne nuit, Marinette ! - It's eight o'clock. Good night, Marinette!
Déjà ? Il est tôt ! Dis plutôt : bonsoir. - Already? It's early! Say bonsoir instead.
Bonsoir, Marinette ! - Good evening, Marinette!
Bonsoir, Adrien ! Et plus tard, bonne nuit. - Good evening, Adrien! And later, good night.

WORDS
bonsoir (bohn-SWAHR) - good evening (hello or goodbye)
bonne nuit (bun NWEE) - good night (before sleeping)
