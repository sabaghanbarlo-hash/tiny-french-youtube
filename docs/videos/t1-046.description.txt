VOCABULARY
le musée (mew-ZAY) - museum
visiter (vee-zee-TAY) - to visit
voyager (vwah-yah-ZHAY) - to travel
réserver (ray-zair-VAY) - to book
le passeport (pass-POR) - passport
la valise (vah-LEEZ) - suitcase
la photo (foh-TOH) - photo
le guide (GEED) - guide
le château (shah-TOH) - castle
un touriste (too-REEST) - tourist

GRAMMAR
Vouloir + infinitive - Use vouloir (to want) followed by an infinitive to say what you want to do. The second verb stays in its infinitive form.
