VOCABULARY
L’addition, s’il vous plaît. (lah-dee-SYOHN, seel voo PLEH) - The bill, please.
Vous pouvez apporter l’addition, s’il vous plaît ? (voo poo-VAY-zah-por-TAY lah-dee-SYOHN, seel voo PLEH) - Could you bring the bill, please?
Nous payons ensemble ? (noo peh-YOHN ahn-SAHMBL) - Are we paying together?
Je peux payer avec la carte. (zhuh puh pay-YAY ah-VEHK lah KART) - I can pay by card.
Le pourboire est compris dans le prix ? (luh poor-BWAR eh kom-PREE dahn luh PREE) - Is the tip included in the price?

GRAMMAR
Pouvoir + infinitive - Use pouvoir followed by an infinitive to ask politely if someone can do something, or to say what you can do. The verb pouvoir changes form, while the next verb stays in the infinitive.
