VOCABULARY
la chambre (lah SHAHMBR) - bedroom
la cuisine (lah kwee-ZEEN) - kitchen
le salon (luh sah-LOHN) - living room
la salle de bains (lah sahl duh BAN) - bathroom
le lit (luh LEE) - bed
la table (lah TAH-bl) - table
la chaise (lah SHEHZ) - chair
la porte (lah PORT) - door
la fenêtre (lah fuh-NETR) - window
un coussin (uhn koo-SAN) - a cushion

GRAMMAR
Noun gender and articles - French nouns are masculine or feminine, and their articles agree in gender. For singular nouns, use le or un with masculine nouns, and la or une with feminine nouns. For example, le lit is masculine and la table is feminine.
