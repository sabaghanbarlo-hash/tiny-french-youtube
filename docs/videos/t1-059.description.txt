VOCABULARY
le quartier (kar-TYAY) - neighborhood
le pays (pay-EE) - country
le village (vee-LAHZH) - village
le centre (SAHN-truh) - center
le chemin (shuh-MAN) - path
le carrefour (kar-FOOR) - intersection
le trottoir (troh-TWAHR) - sidewalk
le pont (POHN) - bridge
le rond-point (rohn-PWAHN) - roundabout
le coin (KWAHN (nasal)) - corner

GRAMMAR
À + le = au - When à comes before the masculine singular article le, they combine to form au. Use au to say “at” or “to” a masculine singular place introduced by le, as in the dialogue.
