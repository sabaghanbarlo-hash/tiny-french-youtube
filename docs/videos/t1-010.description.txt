VOCABULARY
très (TREH) - very
plus (PLÜ) - more
moins (MWAHN) - less
beaucoup (boh-KOO) - a lot
aussi (oh-SEE) - also; too
toujours (too-ZHOOR) - always; still
jamais (zhah-MEH) - never
déjà (day-ZHAH) - already
encore (ahn-KOR) - again; still; more
parce que (pars-KUH) - because

GRAMMAR
Saying “never” - In standard French, put ne before the verb and jamais after it to say “never.” In everyday speech, people sometimes leave out ne, but it is helpful to learn the full form.
