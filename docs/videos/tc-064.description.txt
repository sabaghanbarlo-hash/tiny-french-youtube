VOCABULARY
Tu as un stylo ? (tyoo-ah-uhn stee-LOH) - Do you have a pen?
S'il te plaît. (seel tuh PLEH) - Please.
Tu peux m'en prêter un ? (tyoo puh mahn preh-tay UHN) - Can you lend me one?
Je te le rends demain matin. (zhuh tuh luh rahn duh-MAN mah-TAN) - I'll return it to you tomorrow morning.
Tu peux le garder aujourd'hui. (tyoo puh luh gar-day oh-zhoor-DWEE) - You can keep it today.

GRAMMAR
Pouvoir + infinitive for polite requests - Use pouvoir (to be able to/can) followed by an infinitive to ask if someone can do something. A question with tu peux is a natural, polite request in everyday conversation.
