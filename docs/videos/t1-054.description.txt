VOCABULARY
le film (luh FEELM) - film
la chanson (lah shahn-SOHN) - song
la télé (lah tay-LAY) - TV
la série (lah say-REE) - TV series
le concert (luh kohn-SEHR) - concert
la radio (lah rah-DYOH) - radio
le théâtre (luh tay-AHTR) - theater
chanter (shahn-TAY) - to sing
un acteur (uhn ak-TUHR) - actor
le spectacle (spek-TAH-kl) - show; performance

GRAMMAR
Aimer + le or la - Use aimer followed by le or la when talking about liking something in general. The article agrees with the noun: le goes with masculine nouns, and la goes with feminine nouns.
