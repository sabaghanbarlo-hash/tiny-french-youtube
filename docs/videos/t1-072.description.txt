VOCABULARY
la mode (lah MOD) - fashion
les vêtements (lay vet-MAHN) - clothes
la couleur (lah koo-LUHR) - color
le chapeau (luh shah-POH) - hat
les lunettes (lay loo-NET) - glasses
le jean (luh DJIN) - jeans
les baskets (lay bas-KET) - sneakers
le costume (luh kawss-TEWM) - suit
élégant (ay-lay-GAHN) - elegant
le maquillage (mah-kee-YAHZH) - makeup

GRAMMAR
The definite articles le, la, and les - French uses le before a masculine singular noun, la before a feminine singular noun, and les before plural nouns. Before a vowel or silent h, le and la become l’. These articles usually mean “the,” but French also uses them when talking about things in general.
