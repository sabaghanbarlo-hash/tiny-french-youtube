VOCABULARY
bien sûr (byan SUR) - of course
bien entendu (byan ahn-tahn-DEW) - of course
tant mieux (tahn MYUH) - that's good
tant pis (tahn PEE) - too bad
ça dépend (sah day-PAHN) - it depends
ça suffit (sah swee-FEE) - that's enough
ça roule (sah ROOL) - sounds good
par contre (par KOHNTR) - however
au contraire (oh kohn-TRAIR) - on the contrary
n'importe quoi (nan-port KWAH) - nonsense

GRAMMAR
Fixed expressions - Many everyday expressions are used as set phrases, so it is helpful to learn them as a whole rather than translate each word separately. For example, ça dépend means “it depends,” and ça suffit means “that's enough.”
