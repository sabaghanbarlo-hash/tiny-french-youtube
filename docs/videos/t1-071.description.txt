VOCABULARY
la monnaie (lah moh-NAY) - change
une pièce (ewn PYEHSS) - coin
un portefeuille (un por-tuh-FUHY) - wallet
une facture (ewn fak-TÜR) - bill
une réduction (ewn ray-dük-SYOHN) - discount
les espèces (lay-zess-PESS) - cash
la caisse (lah KESS) - checkout
dépenser (day-pahn-SAY) - to spend
économiser (ay-koh-noh-mee-ZAY) - to save
gratuit (grah-TWEE) - free

GRAMMAR
The near future - Use aller followed by an infinitive to talk about something that is going to happen. The form of aller changes to match the subject, while the main verb stays in its infinitive form.
