VOCABULARY
Au revoir (oh ruh-VWAR) - Goodbye
Enchanté (ahn-shahn-TAY) - Nice to meet you (said by a man)
C'était un plaisir (say-TAY uhn pleh-ZEER) - It was a pleasure
À bientôt, alors (ah byan-TOH ah-LOR) - See you soon, then
À demain, peut-être (ah duh-MAN puh-TETR) - See you tomorrow, maybe

GRAMMAR
À + a time expression - Use à before a time expression to say when you expect to meet again. In these farewells, bientôt means “soon” and demain means “tomorrow.”
