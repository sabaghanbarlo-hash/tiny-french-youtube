VOCABULARY
Bienvenue chez moi ! (byehn-vuh-new sheh MWAH) - Welcome to my home!
Tu veux jouer à un jeu ? (tew vuh zhweh ah uhn ZHUH) - Do you want to play a game?
Tu préfères le jeu ou un autre ? (tew preh-FEHR luh ZHUH oo uhn OH-TRUH) - Do you prefer the game or another one?
Tu veux commencer ? (tew vuh koh-mahn-SAY) - Do you want to start?
Comment on joue ? (koh-MAHN ohn ZHOO) - How do we play?

GRAMMAR
Vouloir + jouer - Use vouloir (to want) followed by an infinitive to ask what someone wants to do. The verb after vouloir stays in its infinitive form.
