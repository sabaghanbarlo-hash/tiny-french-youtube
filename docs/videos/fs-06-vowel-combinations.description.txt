VOCABULARY
moi (MWAH) - me
trois (TRWAH) - three
jaune (ZHOHN) - yellow
beau (BOH) - beautiful / nice
un bateau (uhn bah-TOH) - a boat
deux (DUH) - two
bleu (BLUH) - blue
le lait (luh LEH) - milk

GRAMMAR
oi sounds like “wah” - The letters o and i together make a "wah" sound: moi, toi, trois, voiture. Do not say "oy" as in English.
au and eau sound like “oh” - Au and eau both make a closed "oh" sound. Eau appears in many everyday words: beau, bateau, eau, gâteau.
eu, ai and ou - Eu is like "uh" with rounded lips: deux, bleu. Ai and ei sound like "eh": lait, j'aime. Ou is "oo": rouge, bonjour. Learning these four teams solves many spelling puzzles.
