VOCABULARY
patienter (pah-syan-TAY) - to wait
rappeler (rah-PLAY) - to call back
joindre (ZHWAHNDR) - to reach, contact
retourner (ruh-toor-NAY) - to return, go back
poster (poh-STAY) - to mail, post
livrer (lee-VRAY) - to deliver
récupérer (ray-kew-pay-RAY) - to get back, pick up
un appel (uhn ah-PELL) - a call
contacter (kon-tak-TAY) - to contact
rembourser (rah(n)-boor-SAY) - to pay back

GRAMMAR
Pouvoir + infinitive - Use pouvoir (can, to be able to) followed by an infinitive to say what someone can do. The verb after pouvoir stays in its infinitive form.
