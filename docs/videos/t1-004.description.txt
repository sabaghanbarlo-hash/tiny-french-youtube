VOCABULARY
aller (ah-LAY) - to go
faire (FEHR) - to do; to make
dire (DEER) - to say; to tell
voir (VWAR) - to see
pouvoir (poo-VWAR) - to be able to; can
vouloir (voo-LWAR) - to want
savoir (sah-VWAR) - to know
venir (vuh-NEER) - to come
prendre (PRAHNDR) - to take
parler (par-LAY) - to speak; to talk

GRAMMAR
Aller + infinitive - Use the present tense of aller followed by an infinitive to express an intended or upcoming action (the near future). In the dialogue, vais dire means “am going to tell,” and on va faire means “we are going to do.”
