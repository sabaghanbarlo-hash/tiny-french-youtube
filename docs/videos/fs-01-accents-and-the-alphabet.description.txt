VOCABULARY
comment ça s'écrit ? (koh-mahn sah say-KREE) - how do you spell that?
une lettre (ewn LEH-truh) - a letter
l'alphabet (lahl-fah-BEH) - the alphabet
un accent aigu (é) (uhn ahk-SAHN eh-GEW) - an acute accent
un accent grave (è, à) (uhn ahk-SAHN GRAHV) - a grave accent
un accent circonflexe (ê) (uhn ahk-SAHN seer-kohn-FLEX) - a circumflex accent
une cédille (ç) (ewn say-DEEY) - a cedilla
un nom de famille (uhn nohm duh fah-MEEY) - a surname

GRAMMAR
The French letter names - Every letter has a French name that is different from English. Listen, and repeat after me. The best practice is to spell your own name out loud.
What accents do - Accents change how a letter sounds. É sounds like "ay". È and ê sound like "eh". Ç sounds like "s" before a, o or u. The accent on à only separates words: a and à sound the same, as do ou and où.
Double letters - When spelling, say deux before a double letter: deux T. The doubled consonant is still pronounced just once in normal speech.
