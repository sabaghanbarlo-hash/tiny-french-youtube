VOCABULARY
Je veux apprendre le français. (zhuh vuh ah-prahn-DRUH luh frahn-SEH) - I want to learn French.
Que veux-tu apprendre ? (kuh vuh-tew ah-prahn-DRUH) - What do you want to learn?
Tu veux apprendre avec un livre ? (tew vuh ah-prahn-DRUH ah-VEK uhn LEEVR) - Do you want to learn with a book?
Nous pouvons étudier ensemble. (noo poo-VOHN-zay-tew-DYAY ahn-SAHMBL) - We can study together.
À demain ! (ah duh-MAN) - See you tomorrow!

GRAMMAR
Vouloir + another verb - Use vouloir (to want) followed by an infinitive to say what someone wants to do. With je, vouloir becomes veux; the next verb stays in its infinitive form.
