VOCABULARY
le pain (luh PAN) - the bread (masculine)
la confiture (lah kohn-fee-TEWR) - the jam (feminine)
un couteau (uhn koo-TOH) - a knife
une assiette (ewn ah-see-ET) - a plate
le fromage (luh froh-MAHZH) - the cheese
la pomme (lah POM) - the apple
l'orange (f.) (loh-RAHNZH) - the orange
des œufs (day ZUH) - some eggs

GRAMMAR
Every noun has a gender - French nouns are masculine or feminine, even objects. The gender is shown by the little word before the noun. Always learn the noun together with its article: le pain, la confiture, not just pain and confiture.
A or the? - Un / une mean “a”: un couteau, une assiette. Le / la mean “the”: le pain, la table. Before a vowel or a silent h, le and la both become l': l'orange, l'hôtel. For more than one thing use les (the) or des (some): les pommes, des œufs.
Making a plural - Most plurals just add -s, which you do not pronounce. The article is what you hear: la pomme / les pommes, un œuf / des œufs. Before a vowel, les and des link with a z sound: les œufs (lay-ZUH).
