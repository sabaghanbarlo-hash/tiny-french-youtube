VOCABULARY
Il est tard. (eel eh TAR) - It’s late.
Tu te brosses les dents ? (tyoo tuh bross lay DAHN) - Are you brushing your teeth?
Je veux lire un peu. (zhuh vuh leer uhn PUH) - I want to read a little.
Tu viens me dire bonsoir, s'il te plaît ? (tyoo vyehn muh deer bon-SWAHR, seel tuh PLEH) - Will you come say good night to me, please?
Dors bien. (dor BYEHN) - Sleep well.

GRAMMAR
Reflexive verbs for routines - French often uses a reflexive pronoun with verbs for actions you do to yourself. The pronoun changes with the subject: je me brosse, while tu te couches.
