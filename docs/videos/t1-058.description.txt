VOCABULARY
finir (fee-NEER) - to finish
oublier (oo-blee-AY) - to forget
se souvenir (suh soo-vuh-NEER) - to remember
chercher (share-SHAY) - to look for
revoir (ruh-VWAHR) - to see again
saluer (sah-lyoo-AY) - to greet
présenter (pray-zahn-TAY) - to introduce
rejoindre (ruh-JWAHN-dr) - to join; to meet up with
débuter (day-byoo-TAY) - to begin
terminer (tehr-mee-NAY) - to finish

GRAMMAR
Remembering with se souvenir - The verb se souvenir is reflexive, so it changes with the subject: tu te souviens, je me souviens. To say what you remember, use de before a noun or an infinitive.
