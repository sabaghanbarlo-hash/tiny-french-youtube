VOCABULARY
Bonjour, avez-vous une chemise plus grande, s'il vous plaît ? (bon-ZHOOR, ah-vay-VOO ewn shuh-MEEZ ploo GRAHND, seel voo PLEH) - Hello, do you have a larger shirt, please?
J'en ai une plus grande. (zhahn ay ewn ploo GRAHND) - I have a larger one.
La chemise est trop petite. (lah shuh-MEEZ eh troh puh-TEET) - The shirt is too small.
Combien coûte-t-elle ? (kohm-BYEN koot-TELL) - How much does it cost?
Je la prends, merci. (zhuh lah PRAHN, mehr-SEE) - I'll take it, thank you.

GRAMMAR
Comparing with plus - Use plus + an adjective to say something is more of a quality, like “bigger.” The adjective agrees with the noun; chemise is feminine, so grande ends in -e.
