VOCABULARY
passer (pah-SAY) - to pass; to spend (time)
tenir (tuh-NEER) - to hold; to keep
utiliser (oo-tee-lee-ZAY) - to use
suivre (SWEEVR) - to follow
ajouter (ah-zhoo-TAY) - to add
bouger (boo-ZHAY) - to move
cacher (kah-SHAY) - to hide
ranger (rahn-ZHAY) - to put away; to tidy
remplir (rahn-PLEER) - to fill
vérifier (vay-ree-fee-AY) - to check

GRAMMAR
Giving informal commands - To give a command to someone you know, use the tu form of the verb without saying tu. For regular -er verbs, drop the final -s in the command, as in ajoute and range.
