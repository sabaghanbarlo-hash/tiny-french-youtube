VOCABULARY
d'abord (dah-BOR) - first
puis (PWEE) - then
pourtant (poor-TAHN) - however
puisque (PWEES-kuh) - since
ni (NEE) - neither / nor
lorsque (lor-SKUH) - when
tandis que (tahn-DEE kuh) - while; whereas
simplement (sahm-pluh-MAHN) - simply
justement (zhoost-MAHN) - exactly; as it happens
cependant (suh-pahn-DAHN) - however

GRAMMAR
Neither...nor with ni - To say “neither...nor” in French, use ne before the verb and ni before each option. In everyday speech, ne is often dropped, but it is included in the example here.
