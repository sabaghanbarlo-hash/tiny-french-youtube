VOCABULARY
le corps (luh KOR) - body
la tête (lah TET) - head
les yeux (lay ZYUH) - eyes
le nez (luh NAY) - nose
la bouche (lah BOOSH) - mouth
l'oreille (lor-AY) - ear
la main (lah MAHN) - hand
le bras (luh BRAH) - arm
la jambe (lah ZHAHMB) - leg
le pied (luh PYAY) - foot

GRAMMAR
Definite articles - French nouns usually appear with an article like le, la, or les. Use l' before a vowel sound, as in l'oreille.
