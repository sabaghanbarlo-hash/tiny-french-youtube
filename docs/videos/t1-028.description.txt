VOCABULARY
donner (doh-NAY) - to give
recevoir (ruh-suh-VWAHR) - to receive
apporter (ah-por-TAY) - to bring
offrir (oh-FREER) - to offer; to give
envoyer (ahn-vwah-YAY) - to send
préparer (pray-pah-RAY) - to prepare
fabriquer (fah-bree-KAY) - to make; to manufacture
rendre (RAHN-druh) - to return; to give back
obtenir (ob-tuh-NEER) - to obtain, to get
construire (kohn-STWEER) - to build

GRAMMAR
Pouvoir + infinitive - After pouvoir (to be able to, can), use another verb in the infinitive form. The infinitive does not change to match the person.
