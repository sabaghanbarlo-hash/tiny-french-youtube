VOCABULARY
un garçon (uhn gar-SON) - boy
un bébé (uhn bay-BAY) - baby
la tante (lah TAHNT) - aunt
l'oncle (LOHNKL) - uncle
un cousin (uhn koo-ZAN) - male cousin
une cousine (ewn koo-ZEEN) - female cousin
un copain (uhn koh-PAN) - boyfriend
une copine (ewn koh-PEEN) - girlfriend
rencontrer (rahn-kohn-TRAY) - to meet
un couple (KOOPL) - a couple

GRAMMAR
De + le becomes du - In French, de + le contracts to du. This often appears when saying that someone or something is near a person or belongs to someone.
