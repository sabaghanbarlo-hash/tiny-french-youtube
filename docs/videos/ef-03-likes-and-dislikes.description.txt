VOCABULARY
j'aime (zhem) - I like / I love
j'adore (zhah-DOR) - I love
je n'aime pas (zhuh nem PAH) - I don't like
je déteste (zhuh day-TEST) - I hate
je préfère (zhuh pray-FEHR) - I prefer
beaucoup (boh-KOO) - a lot
surtout (sewr-TOO) - especially
moi aussi (mwah oh-SEE) - me too

GRAMMAR
Aimer + le, la, les - In French, add the word for "the" when you talk about things you like in general: j'aime le café, j'aime la musique, j'aime les films. Le is for masculine words, la for feminine words and les for plurals.
Saying no: ne … pas - Wrap ne and pas around the verb: je n'aime pas le jazz. Ne becomes n' before a vowel. In everyday speech people often skip the ne, but write it in full while you learn.
Moi aussi / moi non plus - To agree with a positive sentence say moi aussi. To agree with a negative sentence say moi non plus.
