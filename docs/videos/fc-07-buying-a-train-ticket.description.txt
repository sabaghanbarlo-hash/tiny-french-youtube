VOCABULARY
un billet (uhn bee-YEH) - a ticket
un aller simple (uhn ah-LAY SAN-pluh) - a one-way ticket
un aller-retour (uhn ah-LAY ruh-TOOR) - a return ticket
pour quel jour ? (poor kehl ZHOOR) - for which day?
un train (uhn TRAN) - a train
un quai (uhn KEH) - a platform
ça vous va ? (sah voo VAH) - does that suit you?
bon voyage (bohn vwah-YAHZH) - have a good trip

GRAMMAR
Un billet pour + place - Use un billet pour and the name of the place to ask for a ticket. The word pour means "for" or "to" here.
Aller simple or aller-retour? - An aller simple is one way. An aller-retour is a return ticket, literally "go and back". Staff nearly always ask this question first.
Quel + noun - Quel means "which" or "what". It agrees with the noun: quel jour, quel quai, but quelle heure and quelle gare. It always comes before the noun.
