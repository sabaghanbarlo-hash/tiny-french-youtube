VOCABULARY
Je peux les nettoyer. (zhuh puh lay neh-twah-YAY) - I can clean them.
Tu peux les essuyer ? (ty puh lay zeh-swee-YAY) - Can you dry them?
Tu ranges la cuisine ? (ty rahnj lah kwee-ZEEN) - Will you tidy the kitchen?
Nous faisons ça ensemble. (noo fuh-ZOHN sah ahn-SAHMBL) - We do it together.
À demain ! (ah duh-MAN) - See you tomorrow!

GRAMMAR
Pouvoir + infinitive - Pouvoir (to be able to/can) followed by an infinitive expresses ability or possibility. In a question, with a polite tone, this pattern can soften a request.
