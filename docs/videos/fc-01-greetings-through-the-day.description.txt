VOCABULARY
bonsoir (bohn-SWAHR) - good evening
salut (sah-LEW) - hi / bye (informal)
ça va ? (sah VAH) - how are you? / how's it going?
ça va bien (sah vah BYAN) - I'm fine
et toi ? (ay TWAH) - and you? (to a friend)
au revoir (oh ruh-VWAHR) - goodbye
à demain (ah duh-MAN) - see you tomorrow
bonne nuit (bun NWEE) - good night

GRAMMAR
Bonjour, bonsoir or salut? - Use bonjour from morning until evening, and bonsoir once it is evening. Salut is relaxed and works for both hello and goodbye, but only with friends and family. With a shopkeeper or someone older, choose bonjour and au revoir.
Ça va ? — asking and answering - Ça va ? is the everyday way to ask how someone is. Answer with ça va, ça va bien or ça va très bien, then hand the question back with et toi ?
Toi or vous? - Toi goes with tu, the friendly "you". To a stranger or an older person, say et vous ? instead. The rest of the sentence stays the same.
