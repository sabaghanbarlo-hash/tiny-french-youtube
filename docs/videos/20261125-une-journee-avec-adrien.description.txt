VOCABULARY
se réveiller (suh ray-vay-YAY) - to wake up
le petit-déjeuner (luh puh-tee day-zhuh-NAY) - breakfast
travailler (tra-vah-YAY) - to work
le soir (luh SWAR) - in the evening
dormir (dor-MEER) - to sleep

GRAMMAR
Je + verb (-er verbs) - Use the "je" form to say what you do
À quelle heure ... ? - Use this to ask what time something happens
