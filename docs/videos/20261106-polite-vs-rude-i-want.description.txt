Polite vs Rude: How to Say “I Want” in French (Not Je Veux!) | Learn French in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny French story and a quick quiz with Marinette and Adrien. Follow for a new lesson every day!

Bonjour. Je veux un café. - Hello. I want a coffee.
Oh là là ! C'est un peu direct. - Oh dear! That's a bit direct.
Ah bon ? Alors... je voudrais un café, s'il vous plaît. - Really? Then... I would like a coffee, please.
Voilà, c'est beaucoup mieux ! - There, that's much better!

WORDS
je veux (zhuh VUH) - I want (too direct)
je voudrais (zhuh voo-DRAY) - I would like (polite)
