VOCABULARY
Où est la caisse, s'il vous plaît ? (oo eh lah KESS, seel voo PLEH) - Where is the ticket office, please?
Combien coûte un billet ? (kom-BYAN koot uhn bee-YEH) - How much is a ticket?
Je peux payer par carte ? (zhuh puh pay-YAY par KART) - Can I pay by card?
Je prends deux billets. (zhuh prahn duh bee-YEH) - I'll take two tickets.
Le concert commence bientôt. (luh kon-SAIR kuh-MAHNS byan-TOH) - The concert starts soon.

GRAMMAR
Pouvoir + infinitive - Use a form of pouvoir followed by an infinitive to ask if something is possible. In a question, you can keep the same word order and raise your voice at the end.
