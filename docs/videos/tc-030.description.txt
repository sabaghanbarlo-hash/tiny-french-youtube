VOCABULARY
Vous voulez du thé ? (voo voo-lay dy TAY) - Would you like some tea?
Oui, merci. (wee mehr-SEE) - Yes, thank you.
avec du lait (ah-vek dy LEH) - with milk
s'il vous plaît (seel voo PLEH) - please
Le thé est très bon. (luh tay eh treh BON) - The tea is very good.

GRAMMAR
Asking what someone wants - Use “Vous voulez… ?” to politely ask someone what they would like. “Vous” is the polite form of “you,” and “voulez” means “want.”
