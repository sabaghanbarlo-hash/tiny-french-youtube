raconter (rah-kohn-TAY) - to tell
discuter (dees-kyoo-TAY) - to discuss
remercier (ruh-mehr-SYAY) - to thank

GRAMMAR
The near future - To say what someone is going to do, use a form of aller followed by an infinitive. The infinitive stays the same, even when the subject changes.
