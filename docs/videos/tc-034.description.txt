VOCABULARY
Je voudrais rendre le T-shirt, s'il vous plaît. (zhuh voo-DRAY rahn-DRUH luh tee-SHURT, seel voo PLEH) - I would like to return the T-shirt, please.
Il est trop petit ? (eel eh troh puh-TEE) - Is it too small?
Vous voulez un autre T-shirt ? (voo voo-LAY uhn-NOH-truh tee-SHURT) - Would you like another T-shirt?
Je préfère récupérer l'argent. (zhuh pray-FAIR ray-koo-pay-RAY lar-ZHAHN) - I prefer to get my money back.
Vous avez payé par carte ? (voo zah-VAY pay-YAY par KART) - Did you pay by card?

GRAMMAR
Yes-or-no questions with rising intonation - In everyday French, you can ask a yes-or-no question by keeping the usual word order and raising your voice at the end. This is common in conversation and sounds natural and direct.
