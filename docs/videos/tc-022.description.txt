VOCABULARY
Bonjour (bohn-ZHOOR) - Hello
Je voudrais un pain, s'il vous plaît. (zhuh voo-DREH uhn PAHN, seel voo PLEH) - I would like a loaf of bread, please.
Il est frais ? (eel eh FREH) - Is it fresh?
Combien coûte le pain ? (kohm-BYAN koot luh PAHN) - How much does the bread cost?
Merci beaucoup. (mehr-SEE boh-KOO) - Thank you very much.

GRAMMAR
A polite request with voudrais - Use “Je voudrais” followed by a noun to make a polite request. It means “I would like”; add “s'il vous plaît” to say “please.”
