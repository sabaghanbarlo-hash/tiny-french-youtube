VOCABULARY
jouer (ZHWAY) - to play
lire (LEER) - to read
écouter (ay-koo-TAY) - to listen to
regarder (ruh-gar-DAY) - to watch
danser (dahn-SAY) - to dance
dessiner (day-see-NAY) - to draw
se promener (suh prohm-NAY) - to go for a walk
le sport (luh SPOR) - sports
la musique (lah mee-ZEEK) - music
bricoler (bree-koh-LAY) - to do DIY

GRAMMAR
Aimer + an infinitive - To say you like doing an action, use a form of aimer followed by the infinitive. The infinitive stays unchanged after aimer.
