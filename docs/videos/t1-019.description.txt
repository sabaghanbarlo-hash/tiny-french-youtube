VOCABULARY
le café (luh kuh-FAY) - coffee
le repas (luh ruh-PAH) - meal
le déjeuner (luh day-zhuh-NAY) - lunch
le dîner (luh dee-NAY) - dinner
le thé (TAY) - tea
le jus (ZHEW) - juice
la bière (BYAIR) - beer
le vin (VAN (nasal)) - wine
une assiette (ah-SYET) - a plate
un bol (BOHL) - a bowl

GRAMMAR
French noun articles - French nouns have grammatical gender, and their articles match: le and un go with masculine nouns, while la and une go with feminine nouns. The article is part of how you use the noun, so learn them together.
