VOCABULARY
alors (ah-LOR) - then; so
donc (DONK) - so; therefore
si (SEE) - if
sans (SAHN) - without
sur (SEWR) - on; over
sous (SOO) - under
avant (ah-VAHN) - before
après (ah-PREH) - after
comme (KOM) - as; like
chez (SHAY) - at; to someone's home

GRAMMAR
Using si for “if” - Si means “if” when it introduces a condition. In everyday plans, it is often followed by a present-tense verb.
