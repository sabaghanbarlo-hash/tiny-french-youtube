VOCABULARY
le lundi (luhn-DEE) - Monday
le mardi (mahr-DEE) - Tuesday
le mercredi (mehr-kruh-DEE) - Wednesday
le jeudi (zhuh-DEE) - Thursday
le vendredi (vahn-druh-DEE) - Friday
le samedi (sahm-DEE) - Saturday
le dimanche (dee-MAHNSH) - Sunday
le lendemain (lahn-duh-MAN) - the following day
la veille (lah vey) - the day before
après-demain (ah-preh duh-MAN) - the day after tomorrow

GRAMMAR
Le with weekdays - In French, le + weekday often describes a regular routine, like “on Tuesdays.” For a specific upcoming day with a date, French often uses the weekday without le.
