VOCABULARY
le four (FOOR) - oven
la poêle (PWAHL) - frying pan
la casserole (kah-suh-ROHL) - saucepan
le sel (SELL) - salt
le sucre (SOOKR) - sugar
couper (koo-PAY) - to cut
cuire (KWEER) - to cook
mélanger (may-lahn-ZHAY) - to mix
éplucher (eh-ploo-SHAY) - to peel
la spatule (spa-TÜL) - spatula

GRAMMAR
Giving a simple command - To give an informal singular command in French, use the tu imperative form of the verb without saying tu. For most -er verbs, the imperative drops the final -s of the tu form. Add s'il te plaît to make a request sound polite.
