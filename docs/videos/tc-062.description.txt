VOCABULARY
Bonjour, Marinette. (bohn-ZHOOR, mah-ree-NET) - Hello, Marinette.
Tu es libre demain ? (ty eh LEE-bruh duh-MAN) - Are you free tomorrow?
Nous avons une réunion. (noo zah-VOHN ewn ray-oo-NYOHN) - We have a meeting.
Tu peux venir ? (ty puh vuh-NEER) - Can you come?
Au bureau ? (oh byoo-ROH) - At the office?

GRAMMAR
Using être to describe availability - Use être (to be) with libre (free/available) to ask or say whether someone is available. The form for tu is es, and the form for je is suis.
