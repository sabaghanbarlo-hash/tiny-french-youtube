VOCABULARY
Bonjour, Marinette. (bohn-ZHOOR, mah-ree-NET) - Hello, Marinette.
Où veux-tu aller ? (oo vuh-TEW ah-LAY) - Where do you want to go?
près du bureau (preh dew bew-ROH) - near the office
Le restaurant n’est pas cher. (luh res-toh-RAHN nay pah SHAIR) - The restaurant isn’t expensive.
Nous partons maintenant ? (noo par-TOHN man-tuh-NAHN) - Shall we leave now?

GRAMMAR
Modal verb + infinitive - After a modal verb like vouloir (to want) or pouvoir (to be able to), use the infinitive form of the next verb. The infinitive stays the same for different subjects.
