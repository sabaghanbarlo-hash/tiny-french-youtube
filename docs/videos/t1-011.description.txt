VOCABULARY
les parents (lay pah-RAHN) - parents
le fils (luh FEESS) - son
la fille (lah FEE-y) - daughter
le mari (luh mah-REE) - husband
la grand-mère (grahn-MEHR) - grandmother
le grand-père (grahn-PEHR) - grandfather
la belle-mère (bel-MEHR) - stepmother; mother-in-law
le beau-père (boh-PEHR) - stepfather; father-in-law
le demi-frère (duh-mee-FREHR) - half-brother
la demi-sœur (duh-mee-SUHR) - half-sister

GRAMMAR
Possessive adjectives - French possessive adjectives agree with the noun that follows them, not with the person who owns it. Use mon with a masculine singular noun, ma with a feminine singular noun, and mes with a plural noun. Before a feminine singular noun beginning with a vowel or mute h, use mon to avoid two vowel sounds.
