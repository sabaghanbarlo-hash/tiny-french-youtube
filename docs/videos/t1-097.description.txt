VOCABULARY
le mur (luh MEWR) - wall
le plafond (luh plah-FOHN) - ceiling
le sol (luh SOHL) - floor
le miroir (luh mee-RWAHR) - mirror
le savon (luh sah-VOHN) - soap
le dentifrice (luh dahn-tee-FREESS) - toothpaste
la serviette (lah sehr-VYEHT) - towel
le tiroir (luh tee-RWAHR) - drawer
le cadeau (luh kah-DOH) - gift
la page (PAHZH) - page

GRAMMAR
Masculine and feminine nouns - With singular nouns, the definite article is le for masculine nouns and la for feminine nouns; both become l’ before a vowel or silent h.
