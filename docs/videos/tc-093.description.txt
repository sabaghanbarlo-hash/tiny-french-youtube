VOCABULARY
Bonsoir, vous venez d'où ? (bohn-SWAHR, voo vuh-NAY doo) - Good evening, where are you from?
Je viens d'une grande ville. (zhuh vee-AN DÜN grahnd VEEL) - I come from a big city.
Je viens d'un petit village, près de la montagne. (zhuh vee-AN dun puh-TEE vee-LAHZH, preh duh lah mohn-TAHN-yuh) - I come from a small village near the mountain.
Vous aimez la ville ? (voo zeh-MAY lah VEEL) - Do you like the city?
Oui, beaucoup. Et vous ? (wee, boh-KOO. ay VOO) - Yes, very much. And you?

GRAMMAR
Venir de: saying where you're from - Use venir de followed by a place to say where you come from. De becomes d’ before a vowel sound, as in d’une and d’un.
