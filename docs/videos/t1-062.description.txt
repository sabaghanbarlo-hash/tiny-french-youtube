VOCABULARY
la vie (lah VEE) - life
l'amour (lah-MOOR) - love
le problème (luh proh-BLEM) - problem
la chance (lah SHAHNS) - luck
la joie (lah ZHWAH) - joy
la vérité (lah vay-ree-TAY) - truth
la liberté (lah lee-behr-TAY) - freedom
la honte (lah OHNT) - shame
la mémoire (lah may-MWAHR) - memory
la fatigue (lah fah-TEEG) - tiredness

GRAMMAR
Articles with nouns - French nouns are usually learned with an article, such as la or le. Before a vowel or a silent h, le or la is elided to l', as in l'amour.
