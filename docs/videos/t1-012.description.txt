VOCABULARY
se réveiller (suh ray-vay-YAY) - to wake up
se lever (suh luh-VAY) - to get up
se laver (suh lah-VAY) - to wash
s’habiller (sah-bee-YAY) - to get dressed
manger (mahn-ZHAY) - to eat
boire (BWAHR) - to drink
travailler (trah-vah-YAY) - to work
dormir (dor-MEER) - to sleep
se brosser (suh broh-SAY) - to brush
se coucher (suh koo-SHAY) - to go to bed

GRAMMAR
Reflexive verbs - Some daily routine verbs are reflexive: they use a small pronoun like me or te before the verb. The pronoun changes with the person, as in je me lave and tu te réveilles.
