VOCABULARY
Tu as un nouveau portable ? (ty ah uhn noo-VOH por-TAHBL) - Do you have a new phone?
Il est nouveau. (eel eh noo-VOH) - It is new.
Il est grand. (eel eh GRAHN) - It is big.
Tu peux me montrer l’écran, s’il te plaît ? (ty puh muh mohn-TRAY lay-KRAHN seel tuh PLEH) - Can you show me the screen, please?
Les deux sont très pratiques. (lay duh sohn tray prah-TEEK) - Both are very practical.

GRAMMAR
Describing things with être - Use être (to be) followed by an adjective to describe a phone or screen. Adjectives agree in number with the noun or pronoun they describe. In the plural, they often add -s, as in différents and pratiques.
