VOCABULARY
Tu veux manger dans le parc demain ? (ty vuh mahn-zhay dahn luh park duh-MAN) - Do you want to eat in the park tomorrow?
Nous pouvons apporter du pain, du fromage et des pommes. (noo poo-VOHN ah-por-TAY dü pan, dü froh-MAHZH ay day POM) - We can bring bread, cheese, and apples.
J’apporte une bouteille d’eau. (zhah-PORT oon boo-TAY doh) - I’ll bring a bottle of water.
À deux heures, si le temps est beau. (ah duh-ZUHR, see luh tahn ay boh) - At two o’clock, if the weather is nice.
Nous pouvons nous rencontrer près de l’arbre ? (noo poo-VOHN noo rahn-kohn-TRAY pray duh LARBR) - Can we meet near the tree?

GRAMMAR
Pouvoir + infinitive - Use pouvoir (“can” or “to be able to”) followed by an infinitive to say what someone can do. The verb pouvoir changes to match the subject, while the next verb stays in its infinitive form.
