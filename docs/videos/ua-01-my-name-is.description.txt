VOCABULARY
je m'appelle (zhuh mah-PEHL) - my name is
comment tu t'appelles ? (koh-mahn too mah-PEHL) - what's your name? (friendly)
comment vous appelez-vous ? (koh-mahn voo zah-play-VOO) - what is your name? (formal)
moi, c'est… (mwah SEH) - I'm… (informal)
enchanté (ahn-shahn-TAY) - nice to meet you (said by a man)
enchantée (ahn-shahn-TAY) - nice to meet you (said by a woman)
et toi ? (ay TWAH) - and you? (friendly)
et vous ? (ay VOO) - and you? (formal)
le prénom (luh pray-NOHM) - first name
le nom de famille (luh NOHM duh fah-MEE) - last name

GRAMMAR
Tu or vous? - Use tu with friends, family, children and people your age. Use vous with someone you do not know, someone older, or in a formal place like an office or a shop. Vous is also the plural: it means "you all". When in doubt, start with vous.
The verb s'appeler - To say your name, use a reflexive verb: je m'appelle, tu t'appelles, il / elle s'appelle. Notice the double l. You can also say simply Moi, c'est in a relaxed conversation.
Enchanté or enchantée? - Enchanté has an extra e when a woman says it, but the two forms sound exactly the same. A man writes enchanté, a woman writes enchantée.
