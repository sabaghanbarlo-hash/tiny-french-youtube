VOCABULARY
la date (lah DAT) - date
le calendrier (luh kah-lahn-dree-AY) - calendar
prochain (proh-SHAN) - next
dernier (dehr-NYAY) - last
janvier (zhahn-VYAY) - January
février (fay-vree-AY) - February
mars (MAHRS) - March
une saison (oon say-ZOHN) - a season
les vacances (lay vah-KAHNS) - the holidays; vacation
un trimestre (tree-MESTR) - a quarter; a school term

GRAMMAR
Saying the date - In French, you can say the date with « Nous sommes le » followed by the day and month. Use a cardinal number for the day, except for the first, which is « premier ».
