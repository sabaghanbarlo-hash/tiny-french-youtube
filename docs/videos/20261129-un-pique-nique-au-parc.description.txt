VOCABULARY
le pique-nique (luh peek-NEEK) - the picnic
le pain (luh PAN) - the bread
le fromage (luh froh-MAHZH) - the cheese
une orange (oon oh-RAHNZH) - an orange
il pleut (eel PLUH) - it's raining

GRAMMAR
Il fait + adjective - Use this to talk about the weather
Il y a + noun - Use this to say "there is" or "there are"
