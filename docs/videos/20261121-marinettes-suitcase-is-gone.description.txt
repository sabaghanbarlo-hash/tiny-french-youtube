Marinette's Suitcase Is Gone at the Airport! | Learn French in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny French story and a quick quiz with Marinette and Adrien. Follow for a new lesson every day!

Oh no, my suitcase is gone! - 
Excusez-moi, où est ma valise ? - Excuse me, where is my suitcase?
Elle est comment ? - What does it look like?
Elle est rouge et grande. - It is red and big.
Regarde, elle est là-bas ! - Look, it's over there!
Oh, ce n'est pas la mienne ! - Oh, it's not mine!
Alors, où est la tienne ? - So where is yours?
