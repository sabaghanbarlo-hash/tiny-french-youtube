VOCABULARY
la langue (LAHNG) - language
le mot (luh MOH) - word
la phrase (lah FRAHZ) - sentence
la question (lah kehss-TYOHN) - question
la réponse (lah ray-POHNS) - answer
la grammaire (lah gra-MEHR) - grammar
un exercice (uhn ehg-zehr-SEES) - exercise
un dictionnaire (uhn deek-syoh-NEHR) - dictionary
traduire (trah-DWEER) - to translate
réviser (ray-vee-ZAY) - to review

GRAMMAR
French noun gender - French nouns are masculine or feminine, and their articles show this. For example, langue is feminine and uses la, while mot is masculine and uses le.
