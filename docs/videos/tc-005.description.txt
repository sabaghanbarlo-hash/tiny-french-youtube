VOCABULARY
Bonjour. (bohn-ZHOOR) - Hello.
Tu parles français ? (tyoo parl frahn-SAY) - Do you speak French?
Je parle français. (zhuh parl frahn-SAY) - I speak French.
Tu veux pratiquer avec moi ? (tyoo vuh prahk-tee-KAY ah-vek MWAH) - Would you like to practice with me?
Avec plaisir. (ah-vek pleh-ZEER) - Gladly.

GRAMMAR
Speaking a language - Use parler followed directly by the language name, without an article. For a question, you can keep the same word order and raise your voice at the end.
