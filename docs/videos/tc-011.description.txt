VOCABULARY
Bonjour, pardon. (bohn-ZHOOR, par-DOHN) - Hello, excuse me.
Où est la gare, s'il vous plaît ? (oo eh lah GAHR, seel voo PLEH) - Where is the train station, please?
Allez tout droit. (ah-LAY too DRWAH) - Go straight.
Au carrefour, tournez à gauche. (oh kar-FOOR, toor-NAY ah GOHSH) - At the intersection, turn left.
Merci beaucoup. (mehr-SEE boh-KOO) - Thank you very much.

GRAMMAR
Polite directions with vous - To give a direction politely, French often uses the vous command form. In this conversation, allez means “go” and tournez means “turn.”
