VOCABULARY
la maison (lah meh-ZOHN) - house
la ville (lah VEEL) - city
la voiture (lah vwah-TYUR) - car
un livre (uhn LEE-vruh) - book
l'eau (LOH) - water
le pain (luh PAN) - bread
le travail (luh trah-VAI) - work
l'école (lay-KOHL) - school
le magasin (luh mah-gah-ZAN) - shop
la rue (lah ROO) - street

GRAMMAR
Articles with nouns - French nouns usually appear with an article. Articles agree with a noun's gender and number and show whether it is definite or indefinite. Le and la mean “the” with singular masculine and feminine nouns; un and une mean “a” or “an.”
