GRAMMAR
What is a nasal vowel? - In a nasal vowel the air goes through your nose and your mouth at the same time. The letter n or m after the vowel is not pronounced as a separate n. Say "bon" and the n disappears: you only hear a nasal "oh".
Three main sounds - An and en sound like a nasal "ah". On sounds like a nasal "oh". In, ain and ein sound like a nasal "a" as in "can". Un is close to in.
When the n is NOT nasal - If the n is doubled or followed by a vowel, the vowel is not nasal and the n is pronounced: bon but bonne; un but une. That difference often marks masculine and feminine.
