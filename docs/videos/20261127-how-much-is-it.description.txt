How to Ask “How Much Is It?” in French (Too Expensive!) | Learn French in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny French story and a quick quiz with Marinette and Adrien. Follow for a new lesson every day!

Excusez-moi, c'est combien, ce sac ? - Excuse me, how much is this bag?
C'est trente euros. - It's thirty euros.
Trente euros ? Oh là là, c'est trop cher ! - Thirty euros? Oh dear, it's too expensive!
Regardez ce petit sac : c'est dix euros. - Look at this small bag: it's ten euros.
Parfait ! Je le prends. - Perfect! I'll take it.

WORDS
c'est combien ? (seh kohm-BYAN) - how much is it?
c'est trop cher (seh troh SHAIR) - it's too expensive
