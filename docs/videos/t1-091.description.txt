VOCABULARY
vivre (VEEVR) - to live
entendre (ahn-TAHN-dr) - to hear
rire (REER) - to laugh
sourire (soo-REER) - to smile
appuyer (ah-pwee-YAY) - to press
suffire (sü-FEER) - to be enough
plaire (PLEHR) - to please; to appeal to
rêver (reh-VAY) - to dream
s’amuser (sah-mü-ZAY) - to have fun
durer (dü-RAY) - to last

GRAMMAR
The verb s’amuser - S’amuser is a pronominal verb, so it uses a small pronoun that matches the subject. With on, the pronoun se becomes s’ before a vowel, and the verb takes a singular form: on s’amuse.
