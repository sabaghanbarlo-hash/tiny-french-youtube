VOCABULARY
Bienvenue chez moi ! (byeh(n)-vuh-nü sheh-MWAH) - Welcome to my home!
Merci ! (mehr-SEE) - Thank you!
Entre, s'il te plaît. (ahntr, seel tuh PLEH) - Come in, please.
Tu veux t'asseoir ? (tyuh vuh tah-SWAHR) - Would you like to sit down?
De l'eau, s'il te plaît. (duh loh, seel tuh PLEH) - Water, please.

GRAMMAR
Tu veux… ? - Use tu veux… ? to ask someone informally what they want or would like. Follow it with an infinitive for an activity or a noun for a thing.
