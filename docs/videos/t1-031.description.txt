VOCABULARY
heureux (uh-RUH) - happy
triste (TREEST) - sad
content (kohn-TAHN) - glad, happy
fatigué (fa-tee-GAY) - tired
inquiet (an-kee-AY) - worried
stressé (stray-SAY) - stressed
la peur (lah PUHR) - fear
la colère (lah koh-LAIR) - anger
aimer (eh-MAY) - to like, love
détester (day-tes-TAY) - to hate

GRAMMAR
Adjective agreement - French adjectives often change to match the gender of the person they describe. The feminine form often has a different ending, as with heureux and heureuse or content and contente.
