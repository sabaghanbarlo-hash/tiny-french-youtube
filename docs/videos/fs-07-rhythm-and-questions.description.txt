VOCABULARY
une syllabe (ewn see-LAHB) - a syllable
la voix (lah VWAH) - the voice
monter (mohn-TAY) - to go up
une question (ewn kehs-TYOHN) - a question
est-ce que (ehs kuh) - (question marker)
pareil (pah-REY) - the same
presque (PREHSK) - almost
chaque (SHAHK) - each / every

GRAMMAR
Even rhythm - French syllables are almost equal in length, and the only longer syllable is the last one of a group of words. Say cho-co-LAT and ma-ri-NETTE, with no strong stress in the middle.
Questions by intonation - The easiest way to ask a yes / no question is to say a normal sentence and let your voice rise at the end. In writing, add a question mark.
Est-ce que - Put est-ce que in front of a normal sentence to make it a question. The rest of the sentence keeps its normal order. Que becomes qu' before a vowel.
