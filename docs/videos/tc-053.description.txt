VOCABULARY
Tu peux préparer la table ? (tyuh puh pray-pah-ray lah tah-BLUH) - Can you set the table?
Je pose les assiettes. (zhuh pohz lay zah-SYET) - I put down the plates.
Tu peux apporter les fourchettes ? (tyuh puh ah-por-tay lay foor-SHET) - Can you bring the forks?
Elles sont où ? (el sohn OO) - Where are they?
le pain et le fromage (luh PAN ay luh froh-MAHZH) - the bread and the cheese

GRAMMAR
Tu peux + infinitive - Use “tu peux” followed by a verb in its infinitive form to ask someone informally if they can do something. It is a common, informal request; a friendly tone can make it courteous, but it is not formal.
