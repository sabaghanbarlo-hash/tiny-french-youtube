VOCABULARY
Tu veux jouer au football demain ? (ty vuh zhoo-AY oh foot-BALL duh-MAN) - Do you want to play football tomorrow?
Quand ? (KAHN) - When?
À trois heures, au parc. (ah twah-ZUHR oh PARK) - At three o’clock, at the park.
Tu peux apporter un ballon ? (ty puh ah-por-TAY uhn bah-LOHN) - Can you bring a ball?
À demain ! (ah duh-MAN) - See you tomorrow!

GRAMMAR
Using au - Au is a contraction of à + le, meaning “at the” or “to the.” Use it before a masculine singular noun, as in au parc. It also appears in jouer au football, meaning “to play football.”
