VOCABULARY
Tu as un morceau de papier, s'il te plaît ? (ty ah uhn mor-SOH duh pah-PYAY, seel tuh PLEH) - Do you have a piece of paper, please?
Tu es blessé ? (ty eh bleh-SAY) - Are you hurt?
Je saigne au doigt. (zhuh seny oh DWAH) - My finger is bleeding.
Tu peux m'aider ? (ty puh meh-DAY) - Can you help me?
Je vais mieux. (zhuh veh MYUH) - I’m feeling better.

GRAMMAR
Tu peux + infinitive - Use tu peux followed by an infinitive to ask whether someone can do something. It also makes a polite everyday request, especially with a questioning tone.
