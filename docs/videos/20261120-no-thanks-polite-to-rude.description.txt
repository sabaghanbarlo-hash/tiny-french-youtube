Four ways to say no thanks in French! - 
This one is very polite. - 
Non merci, c'est très gentil. - No thank you, that's very kind.
This one is normal. - 
Non merci. - No thank you.
This one is casual. - 
Non, ça ira. - No, I'm fine.
And this one is rude! - 
Laisse tomber ! - Drop it!
