VOCABULARY
quelqu’un (kel-KUN) - someone
quelque chose (kel-kuh SHOZ) - something
un truc (uhn TROOK) - a thing; a bit of stuff
la télévision (lah tay-lay-vee-ZYOHN) - television; TV
un détail (uhn day-TIE) - a detail
une façon (ewn fah-SOHN) - a way; a manner
car (KAR) - because; for
voici (vwah-SEE) - here is; here are
parmi (par-MEE) - among
environ (ahn-vee-ROHN) - about; approximately

GRAMMAR
Giving a reason with car - Car means “because” or “for” when it introduces a reason. The clause after car keeps the usual French word order: subject, then verb.
