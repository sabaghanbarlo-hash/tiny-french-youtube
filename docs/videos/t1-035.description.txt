VOCABULARY
souvent (soo-VAHN) - often
parfois (par-FWAH) - sometimes
rarement (rar-MAHN) - rarely
normalement (nor-mal-MAHN) - normally
presque (PRESK) - almost
vraiment (vreh-MAHN) - really
seulement (suhl-MAHN) - only
surtout (syur-TOO) - especially
tellement (tel-MAHN) - so; so much
plutôt (plew-TOH) - rather; quite

GRAMMAR
Adverbs after the verb - Many short adverbs are placed after a conjugated verb. This includes frequency adverbs such as souvent and limiting adverbs such as seulement. In the dialogue, souvent follows vas, and seulement follows prends.
