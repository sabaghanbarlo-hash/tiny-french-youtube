VOCABULARY
Bonjour ! (bon-ZHOOR) - Hello!
Je suis Marinette. (zhuh swee mah-ree-NET) - I’m Marinette.
Vous habitez ici ? (voo zah-bee-TAY ee-SEE) - Do you live here?
Vous voulez que je vous aide ? (voo voo-LAY kuh zhuh voo-ZED) - Do you want me to help you?
Vous voulez prendre un café avec moi ? (voo voo-LAY prahndr un kah-FAY ah-vek MWAH) - Would you like to have a coffee with me?

GRAMMAR
Polite questions with vous - Use vous plus a verb to address someone politely, even if you are speaking to just one person. For a simple yes-or-no question, you can keep the usual word order and raise your voice at the end.
