VOCABULARY
la naissance (lah neh-SAHNSS) - birth
le mariage (luh mah-ree-AHZH) - marriage; wedding
le divorce (luh dee-VORSS) - divorce
la grossesse (lah groh-SESS) - pregnancy
la retraite (lah ruh-TRET) - retirement
un déménagement (uhn day-may-nazh-MAHN) - a move
naître (NETR) - to be born
se marier (suh mah-ree-AY) - to get married
divorcer (dee-vor-SAY) - to get divorced
accoucher (ah-koo-SHAY) - to give birth

GRAMMAR
Talking about the near future - Use a form of aller followed by an infinitive to talk about a future event, often one that is planned or expected soon. The verb aller changes to match the subject, while the next verb stays in its infinitive form.
