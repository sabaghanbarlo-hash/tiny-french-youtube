How to Say “No” in French Without Sounding Rude | Learn French in 1 minute #Shorts

A tiny French story and a quick quiz with Marinette and Adrien. Follow for a new lesson every day!

Tu veux un croissant, Adrien ? - Do you want a croissant, Adrien?
Non, merci. - No, thank you.
Et un café ? - And a coffee?
Pas maintenant, merci. - Not now, thank you.
Et une glace ? - And an ice cream?
Non, merci. Je n'ai plus faim ! - No, thank you. I'm not hungry anymore!

WORDS
non, merci (nohn mehr-SEE) - no, thank you
pas maintenant (pah mant-NAHN) - not now
