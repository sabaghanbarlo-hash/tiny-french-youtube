VOCABULARY
essayer (eh-say-YAY) - to try
décider (day-see-DAY) - to decide
choisir (shwah-ZEER) - to choose
pratiquer (prah-tee-KAY) - to practice
s’entraîner (sahn-tray-NAY) - to train, practice
modifier (moh-dee-FYAY) - to modify, change
remplacer (rahm-plah-SAY) - to replace
prévoir (pray-VWAHR) - to plan, expect
le choix (luh SHWAH) - choice
une décision (ewn day-see-ZYOHN) - decision

GRAMMAR
Pouvoir + infinitive - After pouvoir (can), use another verb in its infinitive form. The infinitive does not change to match the person.
