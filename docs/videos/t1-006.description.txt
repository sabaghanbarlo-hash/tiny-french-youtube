VOCABULARY
aujourd'hui (oh-zhoor-DWEE) - today
demain (duh-MAN) - tomorrow
hier (YAIR) - yesterday
maintenant (mant-NAHN) - now
le jour (luh ZHOOR) - day
le matin (luh ma-TAN) - morning
le soir (luh SWAHR) - evening
un an (uh NAHN) - year
le temps (luh TAHN) - time
la semaine (lah suh-MEN) - week

GRAMMAR
French noun articles - French nouns often appear with an article, which shows their grammatical gender. Use le with masculine nouns and la with feminine nouns; learn the article together with each noun.
