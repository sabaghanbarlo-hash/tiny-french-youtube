VOCABULARY
tu (TEW) - you (to a friend)
tout (TOO) - all / everything
une rue (ewn REW) - a street
une roue (ewn ROO) - a wheel
rouge (ROOZH) - red
salut (sah-LEW) - hi / bye (informal)
la gorge (lah GORZH) - the throat
difficile (dee-fee-SEEL) - difficult

GRAMMAR
u is not ou - French has two different sounds. U is made by saying "ee" and rounding your lips as if for "oo": tu, rue, salut. Ou is the plain English "oo": tout, rouge, bonjour. The difference changes the meaning: rue and roue.
The French r - The French r is made at the back of your throat, like a soft gargle. Start by saying "k" or "g" and then relax the movement so air still passes. Do not roll it with your tongue.
Short commands for learners - To tell a friend to do something, use the tu command: écoute, dis, répète. They are very useful when practising.
