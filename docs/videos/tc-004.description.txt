VOCABULARY
Bonjour. (bohn-ZHOOR) - Hello.
Tu viens d'où ? (tyoo vyen DOO) - Where are you from?
Je viens de France. (zhuh vyen duh FRAHNS) - I’m from France.
Et toi ? (ay TWAH) - And you?
À bientôt ! (ah byen-TOH) - See you soon!

GRAMMAR
Using venir de for origin - Use venir de + a place to say where someone is from. The form used with a country depends on the country’s gender and its first sound: for example, de France, du Canada, and d’Italie.
