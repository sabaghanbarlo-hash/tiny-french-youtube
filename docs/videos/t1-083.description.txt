VOCABULARY
le beurre (luh BUR) - butter
le poulet (luh poo-LAY) - chicken
la salade (lah sah-LAHD) - salad
la tomate (lah toh-MAHT) - tomato
la carotte (lah kah-ROHT) - carrot
la banane (lah bah-NAHN) - banana
la soupe (lah SOOP) - soup
le gâteau (luh gah-TOH) - cake
le chocolat (luh shoh-koh-LAH) - chocolate
le yaourt (luh yah-OORT) - yogurt

GRAMMAR
Unspecified amounts - French uses du with masculine nouns, de la with feminine nouns, and des with plural nouns when talking about an unspecified amount. These forms appear when Marinette says what food she is bringing or has.
