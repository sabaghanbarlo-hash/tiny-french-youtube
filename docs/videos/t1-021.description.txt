VOCABULARY
acheter (ahsh-TAY) - to buy
payer (pay-YAY) - to pay
coûter (koo-TAY) - to cost
le prix (luh PREE) - price
l'argent (lar-ZHAHN) - money
la carte (lah KART) - card
un euro (uhn uh-ROH) - euro
cher (SHEHR) - expensive
combien (kohm-BYEN) - how much
le paiement (pèh-MAHN) - payment

GRAMMAR
Vouloir + an infinitive - After vouloir (to want), use the next verb in its infinitive form. The infinitive stays the same, even when the subject changes.
