VOCABULARY
la fête (FET) - party; celebration
l’anniversaire (ah-nee-vair-SAIR) - birthday
le week-end (week-END) - weekend
inviter (ahn-vee-TAY) - to invite
organiser (or-gah-nee-ZAY) - to organize
commencer (koh-mahn-SAY) - to start; begin
ensemble (ahn-SAHMBL) - together
annuler (ah-new-LAY) - to cancel
une sortie (ewn sor-TEE) - an outing
un plan (uhn PLAHN) - a plan

GRAMMAR
Pouvoir + infinitive - In French, pouvoir (can or to be able to) is followed by a verb in its infinitive form. The infinitive keeps its ending, as in organiser or annuler.
