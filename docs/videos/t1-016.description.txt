VOCABULARY
à gauche (ah GOHSH) - to the left
à droite (ah DRWAHT) - to the right
tout droit (too DRWAH) - straight ahead
devant (duh-VAHN) - in front of
derrière (deh-RYEHR) - behind
près de (PREH duh) - near
loin de (LWAHN duh) - far from
ici (ee-SEE) - here
là (LAH) - there
tourner (toor-NAY) - to turn

GRAMMAR
Giving a simple command - To give an informal direction to one person, use the tu command form without the subject tu. For regular -er verbs, the command ends in -e: tu tournes becomes tourne.
