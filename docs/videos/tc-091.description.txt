VOCABULARY
Il fait beau aujourd'hui ? (eel feh boh oh-zhoor-DWEE) - Is it nice out today?
Il y a du vent. (eel yah dew VAHN) - It's windy.
Vous attendez le bus aussi ? (voo zah-tahn-DAY luh bus oh-SEE) - Are you waiting for the bus too?
Pas vraiment. (pah vray-MAHN) - Not really.
Je préfère le soleil. (zhuh pray-FEHR luh soh-LAY) - I prefer sunshine.

GRAMMAR
Talking about the weather with « il fait » - Use the expression « il fait » to describe the weather. The « il » here does not refer to a particular person; it is part of the weather expression.
