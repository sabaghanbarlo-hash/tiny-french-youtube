VOCABULARY

sec (SEHK) - dry
mou (MOO) - soft
lisse (LEES) - smooth

GRAMMAR
Adjective agreement - French adjectives usually agree in gender and number with the noun they describe. Some change their spelling, such as sec becoming sèche with a feminine noun.
