VOCABULARY
devoir (duh-VWAHR) - must; have to
falloir (fah-LWAHR) - to be necessary; must
préférer (pray-fay-RAY) - to prefer
réussir (ray-oo-SEER) - to succeed; manage
permettre (pair-METR) - to allow; enable
souhaiter (swet-TAY) - to wish; would like
un besoin (uhn buh-ZWAN) - a need
une envie (ewn ahn-VEE) - a desire; a craving
capable (kah-PAH-bl) - capable; able
espérer (es-pay-RAY) - to hope

GRAMMAR
Two ways to express obligation - Devoir is conjugated to match the subject, as in « je dois ». Falloir is an impersonal verb used in the third-person singular form « il faut »; it means that something is necessary.
