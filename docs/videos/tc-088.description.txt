VOCABULARY
Bonjour, je m'appelle Adrien. (bohn-ZHOOR, zhuh mah-PELL ah-dree-EN) - Hello, my name is Adrien.
Tu aimes le sport ? (tyehm luh SPOR) - Do you like sports?
J'aime lire et jouer au tennis. (zhem LEER ay zhway oh teh-NEES) - I like reading and playing tennis.
Tu dessines ? (tyew deh-SEEN) - Do you draw?
Oui, volontiers. (wee, voh-lohn-TYEHR) - Yes, gladly.

GRAMMAR
Aimer + an activity - To say that you like doing an activity, use aimer followed by a verb in its infinitive form. For example, j'aime lire means “I like reading.”
