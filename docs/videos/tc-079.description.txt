VOCABULARY
Bonjour (bohn-ZHOOR) - Hello
Je ne trouve pas ma mère. (zhuh-nuh-troov-pah-mah-MEHR) - I can’t find my mother.
Comment est-elle ? (koh-mahn-eh-TELL) - What does she look like?
près de la caisse (preh-duh-lah-KESS) - near the checkout
De rien. (duh-RYEHN) - You’re welcome.

GRAMMAR
Making a sentence negative with ne…pas - In French, put ne before the conjugated verb and pas after it to make a sentence negative. In everyday speech, ne is often dropped, but it is included here for clear beginner practice.
