VOCABULARY
ouvert (oo-VEHR) - open
fermé (fehr-MAY) - closed
poussez (poo-SAY) - push
tirez (tee-RAY) - pull
la sortie (lah sor-TEE) - the exit
l'entrée (lahn-TRAY) - the entrance / the starter
les toilettes (lay twah-LET) - the toilets
la soupe du jour (lah SOOP dew ZHOOR) - the soup of the day

GRAMMAR
Read by the rules - Put the rules together. In poussez, ou is "oo" and the final z is silent, so you say poo-SAY. In fermé, the é is "ay" and the m is pronounced: fehr-MAY. In sortie, the final e is silent: sor-TEE. Silent endings, accents and nasal vowels do most of the work.
Words that look like English - Many French words look like English: restaurant, hôtel, musée, information, menu. Their meaning is easy to guess, but say them the French way: hôtel is oh-TEL, musée is mew-ZAY.
Signs speak to you with vous - Signs and notices often use the vous command form: poussez, tirez, entrez, attendez. Recognise the -ez ending and you will understand many instructions.
