VOCABULARY
le chien (luh SHYEN) - dog
le chat (luh SHAH) - cat
l'animal (lah-nee-MAHL) - animal
un oiseau (uh-nwah-ZOH) - bird
un cheval (uh(n) shuh-VAHL) - horse
un lapin (uh(n) lah-PAN) - rabbit
une souris (ewn soo-REE) - mouse
une tortue (ewn tor-TEW) - turtle
promener (pro-muh-NAY) - to take for a walk
un cochon (uh koh-SHOHN) - pig

GRAMMAR
French noun articles - French nouns have grammatical gender. For masculine singular nouns, use un for “a/an” and le for “the”; for feminine singular nouns, use une for “a/an” and la for “the.”
