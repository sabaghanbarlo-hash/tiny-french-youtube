VOCABULARY
le lait (luh LEH) - milk
le fromage (luh froh-MAHZH) - cheese
le riz (luh REE) - rice
les pâtes (lay PAHT) - pasta
la pomme (lah POM) - apple
l'œuf (LUHF) - egg
la viande (lah VYAHND) - meat
le poisson (luh pwah-SOHN) - fish
la farine (fah-REEN) - flour
l'huile (WEEL) - oil

GRAMMAR
Articles for food - For an unspecified amount of food, French uses du with masculine singular nouns, de la with feminine singular nouns, de l' before a vowel sound, and des with plural nouns. These forms appear in the shopping list.
