VOCABULARY
Après le cours (ah-PRAY luh KOOR) - After class
J'ai une question (zhay oon kes-TYON) - I have a question
Oui, bien sûr (wee byehn SEWR) - Yes, of course
Comment dit-on ce mot ? (koh-MAHN dee-tohn suh MOH) - How do you say this word?
Je ne comprends pas (zhuh nuh kohn-PRAHN PAH) - I don't understand

GRAMMAR
Negation with ne...pas - To say that you do not do or understand something, put ne before the verb and pas after it. In everyday spoken French, people often drop ne, but it is included here for a clear beginner pattern.
