VOCABULARY
le pantalon (pahn-tah-LOHN) - pants
le T-shirt (tee-SHURT) - T-shirt
la chemise (shuh-MEEZ) - shirt
les chaussures (shoh-SOOR) - shoes
la veste (VEST) - jacket
le manteau (mahn-TOH) - coat
la robe (ROHB) - dress
le pull (PEWL) - sweater
la jupe (ZHEWP) - skirt
le sac (SACK) - bag

GRAMMAR
French articles - French nouns use articles that match their grammatical gender and number. Use le with masculine singular nouns, la with feminine singular nouns, and les with plural nouns.
