VOCABULARY
en fait (ahn FEH) - actually; in fact
du coup (dew KOO) - so; as a result
enfin (ahn-FAN) - well; finally
bref (BREHF) - in short; anyway
voilà (vwah-LAH) - there; there you go
euh (UH) - um; uh
ben (BEHN) - well
d'ailleurs (dah-YUHR) - besides; by the way
sinon (see-NOHN) - otherwise
genre (ZHAHN-ruh) - like (filler)

GRAMMAR
Connectors in a sentence - Conversation connectors can appear at the beginning or end of a sentence, or between two ideas. A comma often marks a short pause after a connector at the beginning or before one at the end.
