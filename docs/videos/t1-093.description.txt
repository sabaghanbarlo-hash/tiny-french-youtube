VOCABULARY
la journée (lah zhoor-NAY) - day; daytime
la douche (lah DOOSH) - shower
le frigo (luh free-GOH) - fridge
le placard (luh plah-KAHR) - cupboard
l’escalier (less-kah-LYAY) - stairs
le rideau (luh ree-DOH) - curtain
le robinet (luh roh-bee-NAY) - tap
le couloir (koo-LWAHR) - hallway
le bruit (BRWEE) - noise
le toit (TWAH) - roof

GRAMMAR
French noun articles - French nouns usually appear with an article that indicates their grammatical gender. Use le with masculine nouns and la with feminine nouns. Before a vowel sound, le and la are elided to l', as in l'escalier.
