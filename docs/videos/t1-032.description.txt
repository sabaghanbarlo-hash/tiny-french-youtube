VOCABULARY
penser (pahn-SAY) - to think
croire (KRWAH) - to believe
trouver (troo-VAY) - to think; to find
comprendre (kohm-prahn-DRUH) - to understand
l'idée (lee-DAY) - idea
l'avis (lah-VEE) - opinion
vrai (VRAY) - true
faux (FOH) - false; wrong
peut-être (puh-TETR) - maybe
d'accord (dah-KOR) - in agreement

GRAMMAR
Que after opinion verbs - Use que after penser, croire, or trouver to introduce a thought or opinion. In speech, que often becomes qu’ before a vowel, as in « Je crois qu’il… ».
