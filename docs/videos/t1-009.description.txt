VOCABULARY

trois (TRWAH) - three
quatre (KATR) - four
cinq (SANK) - five
six (SEESS) - six

huit (WEET) - eight
neuf (NUHF) - nine
dix (DEESS) - ten

GRAMMAR
Numbers before nouns - In French, a number usually comes before the noun it counts, as in dix marches (ten steps). When counting aloud, you can also say the numbers on their own.
