VOCABULARY
Je ne trouve pas la clé. (zhuh nuh troov pah lah KLAY) - I can’t find the key.
Où as-tu regardé ? (oo ah-too ruh-gar-DAY) - Where have you looked?
Regarde sous le lit, s'il te plaît. (ruh-GARD soo luh LEE, seel tuh PLEH) - Look under the bed, please.
Elle n'est pas sous le lit. (el nay pah soo luh LEE) - It isn’t under the bed.
Tu as trouvé la clé ! (too ah troo-VAY lah KLAY) - You found the key!

GRAMMAR
Negation with ne…pas - In a simple negative sentence, put ne (or n’ before a vowel) before the conjugated verb and pas after it. In compound tenses, ne and pas go around the auxiliary verb. In everyday speech, ne is often dropped; keeping it makes the sentence complete and standard.
