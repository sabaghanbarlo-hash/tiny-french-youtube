VOCABULARY
tu es libre ? (tew eh LEE-bruh) - are you free?
on va… (ohn VAH) - shall we go…? / we're going…
au cinéma (oh see-nay-MAH) - to the cinema
bonne idée (bun ee-DAY) - good idea
d'accord (dah-KOR) - OK / agreed
devant (duh-VAHN) - in front of
je vais (zhuh VEH) - I'm going / I'm going to
à samedi (ah sahm-DEE) - see you Saturday

GRAMMAR
The verb aller (to go) - Aller is irregular, so learn it by heart: je vais, tu vas, il / elle / on va, nous allons, vous allez, ils / elles vont. On is the everyday word for "we" and uses the same form as il.
à + le = au - Use à to mean "to" or "at". With le it becomes au: au cinéma, au café. With la it stays à la: à la plage. With les it becomes aux.
Aller + infinitive = near future - Je vais + another verb means "I'm going to…". It is the easiest way to talk about the future: je vais acheter, on va manger.
