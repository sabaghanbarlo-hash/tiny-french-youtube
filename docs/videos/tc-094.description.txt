VOCABULARY
Tu as vu le film hier ? (tü ah VÜ luh feelm YER) - Did you see the film yesterday?
Oui, beaucoup. (wee boh-KOO) - Yes, a lot.
Tu as aimé le film ? (tü ah eh-may luh FEELM) - Did you like the film?
Tu veux revoir le film avec moi ? (tü vuh ruh-vwahr luh feelm ah-vek MWAH) - Do you want to watch the film again with me?
Avec plaisir. (ah-vek pleh-ZEER) - Gladly.

GRAMMAR
Talking about the past with avoir - Use the present tense of avoir plus a past participle to say what someone did. In the dialogue, tu as vu means “you saw,” and tu as aimé means “you liked.”
