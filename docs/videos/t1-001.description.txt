VOCABULARY
je (ZHUH) - I
tu (TEW) - you (singular)
il (EEL) - he; it
elle (ELL) - she; it
nous (NOO) - we
vous (VOO) - you (plural or formal)
ils (EEL) - they (masculine or mixed)
elles (ELL) - they (feminine)
être (eh-TRUH) - to be
avoir (ah-VWAHR) - to have

GRAMMAR
Pronouns and verb forms - In French, a subject pronoun usually comes before the verb. The verb form changes to match the subject, as with être in these examples.
