VOCABULARY
mieux (MYUH) - better
meilleur (meh-YUHR) - better; best
pire (peer) - worse; worst
pareil (pah-RAY) - the same; alike
différent (dee-fay-RAHN) - different
égal (ay-GAHL) - equal
identique (ee-dahn-TEEK) - identical
similaire (see-mee-LAIR) - similar
supérieur (syoo-peh-RYUHR) - superior; higher
inférieur (ehn-feh-RYUHR) - inferior; lower

GRAMMAR
Mieux ou meilleur ? - Use meilleur(e)(s) as an adjective to compare or describe nouns, and mieux as an adverb to describe how someone does or feels. To say “the best,” use le, la, or les meilleur(e)(s), agreeing with the noun.
