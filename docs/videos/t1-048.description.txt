VOCABULARY
le menu (muh-NEW) - menu
commander (koh-mahn-DAY) - to order
l'addition (ah-dee-SYOHN) - bill
un plat (uhn PLAH) - dish
une entrée (ewn ahn-TRAY) - starter
un dessert (uhn day-SEHR) - dessert
une boisson (ewn bwah-SOHN) - drink
le pourboire (poor-BWAHR) - tip
la réservation (ray-zehr-vah-SYOHN) - reservation
épicé (ay-pee-SAY) - spicy

GRAMMAR
The near future - Use aller in the present followed by an infinitive to say what someone is going to do. In the dialogue, nous allons commander means “we’re going to order.”
