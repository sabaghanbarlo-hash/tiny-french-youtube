title: Never Say “Je t'aime” to a Friend in French!
scene: street
cast: marinette, adrien

[hook]
Adrien: Careful! This sentence is very strong in French.
[story]
Marinette: Tu es mon ami. Je t'aime ! | You are my friend. I love you!
Adrien: Heu... "Je t'aime", c'est très fort ! | Um... "I love you" is very strong!
Adrien: Pour un ami, on dit : je t'aime bien. | For a friend, we say: I like you.
Marinette: Ah ! Je t'aime bien, Adrien ! | Ah! I like you, Adrien!
[ending]
Marinette: Comment yes if you got it!
