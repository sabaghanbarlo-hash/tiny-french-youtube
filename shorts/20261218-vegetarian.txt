title: Vegetarian in France? Say This
scene: restaurant
cast: marinette, adrien

[hook]
Marinette: Need a meat-free meal? Ask like this!
[story]
Adrien: Je suis végétarien. Il y a de la viande ici ? | I'm vegetarian. Is there meat in this?
Marinette: Non, c'est un plat sans viande. | No, it's a dish without meat.
Adrien: Parfait, merci ! | Perfect, thanks!
[ending]
Marinette: Say it once, then comment done!
