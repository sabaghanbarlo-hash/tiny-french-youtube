title: What Time Is It? Ask in French
scene: station
cast: marinette, adrien

[hook]
Adrien: Need the time? Ask like this!
[story]
Adrien: Pardon, quelle heure est-il ? | Excuse me, what time is it?
Marinette: Il est trois heures. | It's three o'clock.
Adrien: Merci ! | Thanks!
[ending]
Marinette: Say it out loud once, then comment done!
