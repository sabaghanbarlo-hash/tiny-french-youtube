title: How to Ask “How Much Is It?” in French (Too Expensive!)
episode: 21
scene: store

[story]
Marinette: Excusez-moi, c'est combien, ce sac ? | Excuse me, how much is this bag?
Adrien: C'est trente euros. | It's thirty euros.
Marinette: Trente euros ? Oh là là, c'est trop cher ! | Thirty euros? Oh dear, it's too expensive!
Adrien: Regardez ce petit sac : c'est dix euros. | Look at this small bag: it's ten euros.
Marinette: Parfait ! Je le prends. | Perfect! I'll take it.

[vocab]
vocab: c'est combien ? | how much is it? | seh kohm-BYAN
example: C'est combien, s'il vous plaît ? | How much is it, please?
vocab: c'est trop cher | it's too expensive | seh troh SHAIR
example: Oh là là, c'est trop cher ! | Oh dear, it's too expensive!
