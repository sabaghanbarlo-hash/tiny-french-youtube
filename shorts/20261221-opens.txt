title: What Time Does It Open? Ask in French
scene: store
cast: marinette, adrien

[hook]
Marinette: Early at a shop? Ask when it opens!
[story]
Adrien: Pardon, ça ouvre à quelle heure ? | Excuse me, what time does it open?
Marinette: À neuf heures. | At nine o'clock.
Adrien: Merci ! | Thanks!
[ending]
Adrien: Say it out loud once, then comment done!
