title: Buy a Metro Ticket in French
scene: station
cast: marinette, adrien

[hook]
Marinette: Need a ticket in the metro? Say this!
[story]
Adrien: Un ticket de métro, s'il vous plaît. | A metro ticket, please.
Marinette: Voilà votre ticket. | Here is your ticket.
Adrien: Merci ! | Thanks!
[ending]
Adrien: Say it once out loud, then comment done!
