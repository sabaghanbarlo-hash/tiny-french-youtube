title: Ask for the Bill in French: From Polite to Rude
scene: restaurant
cast: marinette, adrien

[hook]
Adrien: Four ways to ask for the bill in French!
[story]
Adrien: This one is very polite.
Marinette: Je voudrais payer, s'il vous plaît. | I would like to pay, please.
Adrien: This one is normal.
Marinette: L'addition, s'il vous plaît. | The bill, please.
Adrien: This one is casual.
Marinette: On peut payer ? | Can we pay?
Adrien: And this one is a bit rude!
Marinette: L'addition ! | The bill!
[ending]
Adrien: Which one would you say? Comment 1, 2, 3 or 4!
