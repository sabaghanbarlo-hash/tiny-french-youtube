title: Friend Is Busy? Say “Bon courage !” in French
scene: cafe
cast: marinette, adrien

[hook]
Marinette: Someone has a hard day? Say this!
[story]
Adrien: J'ai beaucoup de travail aujourd'hui... | I have a lot of work today...

Adrien: Merci, tu es gentille ! | Thanks, you're kind!
[ending]
Marinette: Say it to a friend today! Comment good luck!
