title: Bonsoir or Bonne Nuit? Don't Say Good Night Too Early in French
episode: 18
scene: street

[story]
Adrien: Il est huit heures. Bonne nuit, Marinette ! | It's eight o'clock. Good night, Marinette!
Marinette: Déjà ? Il est tôt ! Dis plutôt : bonsoir. | Already? It's early! Say bonsoir instead.
Adrien: Bonsoir, Marinette ! | Good evening, Marinette!
Marinette: Bonsoir, Adrien ! Et plus tard, bonne nuit. | Good evening, Adrien! And later, good night.

[vocab]
vocab: bonsoir | good evening (hello or goodbye) | bohn-SWAHR
example: Bonsoir, madame ! | Good evening, madam!
vocab: bonne nuit | good night (before sleeping) | bun NWEE
example: Bonne nuit, Marinette ! | Good night, Marinette!
