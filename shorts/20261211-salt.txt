title: Polite vs Rude: Ask for the Salt in French
scene: restaurant
cast: marinette, adrien

[hook]
Marinette: Which one sounds rude? Listen carefully!
[story]
Adrien: Donne-moi le sel ! | Give me the salt!
Marinette: Oh là là, c'est impoli ! | Oh dear, that's impolite!
Adrien: Pardon ! Pouvez-vous me passer le sel, s'il vous plaît ? | Sorry! Could you pass me the salt, please?
Marinette: Voilà, c'est parfait ! | There, that's perfect!
[ending]
Adrien: Comment polite if you'd say the second one!
