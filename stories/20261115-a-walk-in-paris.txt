[vocab]

example: Nous sommes sur la terrasse. | We are on the terrace.

[grammar]
grammar: Elle est + adjective | Use this to describe a feminine noun
example: Elle est très grande. | It is very big.
example: Elle est magnifique. | It is wonderful.
grammar: Je voudrais + noun | Use this to politely ask for something
example: Je voudrais du fromage. | I would like some cheese.
example: Je voudrais un café. | I would like a coffee.

[quiz]
quiz: What does "la terrasse" mean? | the terrace | the tower | the market | A | A terrasse is the outdoor part of a café.
quiz: How do you ask politely for some cheese? | Je voudrais du fromage | Je suis du fromage | Je fromage voudrais | A | "Je voudrais" means "I would like".
