title: Au Marché de Noël
scene: stall

[story]
@time sunset
@scene stall
> Marinette enters left
& Adrien enters right
> Marinette looks excited
Marinette: Regarde ! Le marché de Noël ! | Look! The Christmas market! 
> Adrien looks happy
Adrien: Quelles belles décorations ! | What beautiful decorations!
> Pierre enters right
Pierre: Bonjour ! Venez voir, venez voir ! | Hello! Come and see, come and see!
> Marinette points right
Marinette: C'est combien, cette étoile ? | How much is this star?
> Pierre shows
Pierre: Elle coûte cinq euros. | It costs five euros.
> Adrien looks surprised
Adrien: C'est un peu cher ! | That's a bit expensive!
> Marinette looks shy
Marinette: Mais elle est si jolie ! Je la prends. | But it's so pretty! I'll take it.
> Pierre cheers
Pierre: Merci, mademoiselle ! Voulez-vous une boisson chaude ? | Thank you, miss! Would you like a hot drink?
Marinette: Pour moi, un chocolat chaud. | For me, a hot chocolate.
Adrien: Et pour moi, un vin chaud, s'il vous plaît. | And for me, a mulled wine, please.
> Pierre shows
Pierre: Voilà ! Joyeux Noël ! | Here you go! Merry Christmas!
> Marinette cheers
Marinette: Joyeux Noël ! | Merry Christmas!
> Adrien laughs
Adrien: Joyeux Noël à tous ! | Merry Christmas, everyone!

[vocab]
vocab: le marché de Noël | the Christmas market | luh mar-SHAY duh no-EL
example: On va au marché de Noël. | We're going to the Christmas market.
vocab: la décoration | the decoration | lah day-ko-ra-SYON
example: J'aime cette décoration. | I like this decoration.
vocab: cher / chère | expensive | SHAIR
example: C'est trop cher ! | It's too expensive!
vocab: joli(e) | pretty | zho-LEE
example: Elle est très jolie. | She/It is very pretty.
vocab: Joyeux Noël | Merry Christmas | zhwa-YUH no-EL
example: Joyeux Noël à tous ! | Merry Christmas, everyone!

[grammar]
grammar: C'est combien ? / Ça coûte + price | Use these to ask and say prices
example: C'est combien ? | How much is it?
example: Ça coûte cinq euros. | It costs five euros.
grammar: C'est + adjective | Use this to give your opinion
example: C'est cher. | It's expensive.
example: C'est joli. | It's pretty.

[quiz]
quiz: What does "C'est combien ?" mean? | How much is it? | Where is it? | Who is it? | A | "Combien" asks about price or quantity.
quiz: What does "cher" mean? | cheap | expensive | pretty | B | "Cher" means expensive.
quiz: How do you say "Merry Christmas"? | Joyeux Noël | Bonne nuit | Bon appétit | A | "Joyeux Noël" is Merry Christmas.

[ending]
Marinette and Adrien: À bientôt ! | See you soon!
